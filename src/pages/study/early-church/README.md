# The early church: the page

Study 21 of the Academic shelf, built per [ACADEMIC_PAGE_RUN.md](../../../../../Pages/Topics/StudyPlan/ACADEMIC_PAGE_RUN.md) row 21.
Address: `/study/the-early-church` (preview: http://127.0.0.1:8931/study/the-early-church). Research record:
[21-the-early-church.json](../../../../content/academic-research/21-the-early-church.json). Design note:
[design.md](../../../../content/academic-research/21-the-early-church/design.md). Build script:
[build-early-church-study.py](../../../../scripts/build-early-church-study.py).

**Direction in one sentence:** a document case, with eight drawers (one per kind of writing, from Clement's letter to a council's
decided words) under the record's four periods. It shows that the kinds of witness change as the church's situation changes. Then the four
councils at another scale, with a reception band showing which family of churches receives how many of them. This is the design note's
recommended direction of its three. The owner had not chosen, so the choice can still be overturned.

## How

- Every research string goes through `<Checked>`; `plainText()` is used for titles, labels, keys, the SVG chart text and comparisons.
- 24 check notes have no marker in the text, because they sit on an empty route or on a quotation's reference. Each shows as a small note
  dot beside its field and opens like any other note.
- Opening a record or a verse closes an open check note, and opening a check note closes the record sheet. Escape closes either. Focus returns to the
  opener, or to the chosen drawer's button if the opener is gone.
- A link whose route is `""` shows as a name with "(not yet on the site)" and its note dot. 15 links are like that: the theology studies
  Baptism, The person of Christ, Worship and the Lord's Day, The Lord's Supper, The Christian and the state, Sin and the fall, Saved by
  grace and Election, plus New Testament history (its route was not settled when the record was written), Baptist history and The
  Reformation and the awakenings.
- Seven internal links are made, each confirmed by the build as routed: Academic studies, the creeds collection, the Chalcedonian Definition,
  How the canon took shape, How the church has read the Bible, The medieval church and The Trinity.
- No picture is shown: the record names none.

## What the build script refuses (proved)

`python scripts/build-early-church-study.py` then `--check` both pass. It refuses to write if:
- a listed verse is missing from the Berean, its label is wrong, or its phrase is not in that verse;
- a reference anywhere in the record's text does not exist;
- a "(… · BSB)" quotation is not word for word the Berean;
- Scripture is quoted without the BSB mark;
- a verse label a record uses is not listed;
- a quotation the site holds the text of is not word for word in that held text. Nine are checked: the Chalcedon and Ephesus decided
  words and two quotations from Schaff's notes, all against Schaff's Chalcedonian Definition; Philaret; Westminster ×2; the 1689
  Confession ×2;
- a record has no date span, or its span's words are not in the record's own date;
- an argued alternative date is not in the record;
- an opening figure miscounts;
- the reception rows' words are not in the council records;
- the two accounts of 312 are not sentences of the record;
- a link leaves the site or names an address the site does not route (creed and theology addresses are checked against the held files,
  with traversal refused);
- the data holds "://", or is over 60 KB.

## What the checks prove, and what they do not

- **Proved:**
  - every verse shown is the site's Berean, word for word;
  - every reference exists;
  - the nine held-text quotations match the site's copies;
  - every drawn date stands on the record's own words;
  - the figures count what they say;
  - every link stays inside the site and resolves;
  - the data is current with the record (the test compares a sha256 of the record).
- **Not proved:**
  - that any date or reading is right;
  - the quotations from the early writers (Tacitus, Clement, the Didache, Ignatius, Polycarp, Justin, Tertullian, Eusebius and the rest),
    whose texts the site does not hold. The page marks each one "to be checked word for word before release", as the record does
    (17 of the 38 sources still have the status "check");
  - the Baptist Faith and Message and the 1973 declaration, which are not held either.
- **Drawing choices that are mine, not the record's:**
  - "about" one year is drawn as a soft patch three years either side;
  - "late first or early second century" is drawn as 67–133 (thirds of a century);
  - "second or third century" is drawn as 100–300;
  - Nero (64) and Perpetua (203) are drawn as firm years. The record gives the year, not the day; the design note's "no point dates
    before 250 unless a source gives a day" was read as "no estimate drawn as a point".
  - The chart's caption says all of this on the page.

## Could be confused with

- **The medieval church (33):** its direct neighbour on the shelf. It has a map of north-west Europe with a year slider and its own
  panels. This page has no year slider; its one figure is the case, and its map is a small static frame of the council cities.
- **New Testament history (43):** draws reigns as bars in lanes. Here the bars are kinds of writing, one row per drawer, and there are
  no lanes of rulers.
- **How the church has read the Bible (30):** a glossed page. This page has no gloss.
