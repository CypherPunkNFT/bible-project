Search method

01 / WORDS

The words on the page.

Full-text search across the indexed collection. Put a phrase in quotation marks to keep its words together.

02 / MEANING

A different way to find it.

Local semantic search finds related ideas across wording and languages. Results are passages from the sources, with their context available.

03 / CONNECTIONS

Follow the evidence.

Verse references, recorded family relationships and source citations retain their provenance. A similarity score is a retrieval signal, not a doctrinal conclusion.

What is indexed, and how this library works

This instance has its own SQLite and LanceDB stores. Original documents remain unchanged. Source metadata and duplicate downloads are inventoried; images, application dependencies, build output and private credentials are excluded from text search.

Linked books or articles that have not been downloaded are source records, not searchable full texts. Historic reference works and authored studies keep their own labels; they are not presented as Scripture.
