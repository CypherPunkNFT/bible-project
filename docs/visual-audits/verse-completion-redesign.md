# Verse completion redesign (from its visual audit)

**Done:** 11 November 2026, sequence "Bible Guide: redesign every game from its audit", step 6. The audit it answers is [verse-completion.md](verse-completion.md); its screenshots are in [verse-completion/](verse-completion/) and the new ones in [verse-completion/after/](verse-completion/after/), named the same way so each stage pairs up. The shared instructions were [REDESIGN-BRIEF.md](REDESIGN-BRIEF.md); my step's own brief added one special rule, that Pick and Type and My verses keep working and the Scripture stays the Berean Standard Bible wording, which agrees with the shared brief. No correction arrived during the job.

## 1. What changed

The verse is now the game. Every hidden word is a gap as wide as the word, drawn in the game's lavender, and the next gap to fill breathes slowly, so the card is never still. In Hard mode and in First letters each word you give lands in its own gap, in the verse itself, green when right and red with a wavy line when missed, and the three word tiles below are dealt afresh for every word with a small shake on a wrong one. In Whole words the gaps dim one by one as your typed words reach them, and the typing box sits inside the card, in the serif, with Check above it so a phone keyboard never covers it. On Easy and Medium the three endings are proper cards that deal in, lift under the mouse, press when tapped, and settle green or red while the others fade. When you answer, the card stays: the words land in the gaps left to right a tenth of a second apart, each with its mark attached to it, and a band rises under the card with the stars filling one after another, a short verdict ("Right!", "Nearly there", "Not this time", "Word perfect!") and Next, pinned to the bottom of a phone screen so Next is always in reach. A full three stars gets a brief gold shimmer. Across the top of the round a trail of ten segments fills green, amber or red as the cards go, with the star total beside it. The round ends on a result panel: the title changes with the score, the stars count up from zero out of thirty, the day streak's flame, the round's trail, and Play again right there, with the ten verses below as small cards edged in their colour, each with its stars and an Add button, and "Add all missed to My verses" as one card carrying the count. A perfect round gets one burst of gold and cream. The start screen is a lobby: My verses as a small ring, the day streak as a flame badge, a round in progress as a card you tap to resume, Pick and Type as one two-way switch, and the three levels as cards each showing John 3:16 with its gaps for that level. Leaving a round and clearing progress ask in the site's own dialog. The rules, the pool, the scoring, the review boxes, the saved record and the address are unchanged, and the verses are still the Berean Standard Bible.

## 2. The audit's list, item by item

1. **Fix the small broken pieces first.** Built as asked. Each hidden word is now one unbreakable unit with its symbol attached to its end, so a tick never floats to the next word ([desktop-04](verse-completion/after/desktop-04-pick-easy-right-result.png)). A word you typed wrongly is shown struck through, then a space, then the right word, so a screen reader hears two words and the eye sees two ([phone-15](verse-completion/after/phone-15-pick-hard-result.png)). A word revealed with "Show next word" is drawn as "Shown with a hint" with its own diamond mark in the game's colour, no longer as a wrong copy of itself; it still scores as wrong, as the rules say ([desktop-19](verse-completion/after/desktop-19-type-words-partly-right-result.png)). Leave round opens the kit's site-styled dialog ([desktop-12b](verse-completion/after/desktop-12b-leave-round-dialog.png)), and so does Clear my progress. The site's floating pencil button shrinks while a card is open on a phone and hides on a short one.
2. **Make the answer land in the gaps.** Built as asked. The card stays on screen and every hidden word drops into its gap left to right, a tenth of a second apart, never later than 1.8 seconds in all so a thirty-word Hard card finishes while you are still looking. A right word carries a green tick, a wrong one a red cross with what you gave struck through before it. The stars then pop in one after another in the band, and three stars get one gold shimmer. Under reduced motion the words, the stars and the band appear at once. Built differently in one detail: the wrong word you gave sits small before the right word rather than above it, because a line above would make the verse's lines uneven.
3. **Turn the choices into tiles.** Built, with one part deliberately not done. The three endings are the kit's answer cards ([desktop-03](verse-completion/after/desktop-03-pick-easy-card.png)); the chosen one settles green or red with a shake, the right one is ticked, the rest fade. In Hard the three words are tiles in a strip with their 1, 2, 3 key badges, dealt afresh for every word, and the word you tap lands in the next gap of the verse itself ([desktop-14b](verse-completion/after/desktop-14b-pick-hard-mid-play.png)); a counter under the verse says how far through you are. **Not built: capping a Hard card at about twelve hidden words.** That would change which words are hidden, and the review boxes, the "best" score kept for every saved verse and the Memorised rule all rest on the Hard split as the pool defines it; the brief says the rules and data stay. Instead the slog is eased by the words landing in place, so a long verse is read as it is built rather than as a block of dashes, and the result is readable ([phone-15](verse-completion/after/phone-15-pick-hard-result.png)).
4. **Give the round a real ending.** Built as asked. The kit's result panel: a title by score ("Word perfect round!", "Well remembered", "Good round", "Keep going"), the stars counting up from zero, the day streak's flame, the trail, Play again first, then the verses as small cards with their stars (tap one to read it) and Add on each, and "Add all missed" as one card with the count ([desktop-08](verse-completion/after/desktop-08-round-summary.png), [phone-08](verse-completion/after/phone-08-round-summary.png)). On a 390-wide phone the panel through Play again fits the first screen. A perfect round gets one burst, and a finished round reopened after a reload is still.
5. **Make the start screen a lobby.** Built as asked. Three level cards each carry John 3:16, loaded from the game's own pool so it is the Bible's wording, with its last few words gapped for Easy, half for Medium and nearly all for Hard; Pick and Type are one switch; Whole words and First letters appear under Type; Verses from stays as chips; My verses is a ring whose arc is the share memorised with the count inside; the streak is a flame badge; a round in progress is a card ([desktop-02](verse-completion/after/desktop-02-start.png), [desktop-25b](verse-completion/after/desktop-25b-start-with-resume-card.png)).
6. **Show progress through the round.** Built as asked. Ten segments across the top of the card, green for three stars, amber for one or two, red for none or skipped, the current one outlined, with the star total beside them ([desktop-05](verse-completion/after/desktop-05-pick-easy-wrong-result.png)). The trail's spoken label is still "Round · 3 of 10".
7. **Make typing happen on the verse.** Built as asked. First letters fills the gaps in the verse as you type ([desktop-23](verse-completion/after/desktop-23-type-first-letters-mid.png)); Whole words keeps the box but puts it inside the card under the verse, in the serif, and the gaps dim as the typed words reach them ([desktop-18](verse-completion/after/desktop-18-type-words-typed-with-hint.png)).

**My own additions.**
- **The breathing gap.** The next gap to be filled pulses slowly and loops, so a card in play is never a still form, and the gaps are as wide as their words, so the shape of the verse shows before a word is given.
- **The band with Next inside it.** The verdict, the stars and Next ride in the kit's feedback band under the card, pinned to the bottom of a phone screen. The audit did not ask for it by name, but it is what keeps Next on the first screen after a thirty-word Hard result, and Enter still moves on because the band takes focus.

## 3. Faults fixed

From "Anything broken": the hinted word is marked "shown"; marks attach to their own word; the struck-out word and the right word are separated by a space; Leave round uses the site's dialog; the pencil steps aside on a small phone.

From "Phone problems": Check sits above the typing box, out of a keyboard's way; Hard's dashes are now sized gaps that become the words as they are given; the result is a marked verse rather than a wall of struck-out words; the ending fits one phone screen with Play again on it.

From "Accessibility": keyboard focus shows as the card's or tile's own lift and lavender edge ([desktop-26](verse-completion/after/desktop-26-keyboard-focus-on-second-ending.png)); the gaps are in the game's colour rather than low-contrast grey; the verdict is a live region. Keys 1, 2 and 3 are ignored while the Leave dialog is open or while typing in a form field, so a dialog can never pick a word.
