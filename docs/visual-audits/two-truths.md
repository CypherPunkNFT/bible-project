# Visual audit: Two truths and a lie (/games/two-truths)

Audited 10 November 2026 on the local preview. I played the game through from a fresh start at desktop size (1440 wide) and again at phone size (390 wide): the start screen with and without teams; ten solo rounds with a right answer, a wrong answer, a "Pick again", a round answered from the keyboard, the light theme, keyboard focus, and the final score with Share; then a two-team group game through a reveal, an award, a second round and Finish; and a failed load. No code was changed. The screenshots are in [two-truths/](two-truths/). Names starting `desktop-` or `phone-` give the screen size and the number gives the stage; the same number is the same stage at both sizes.

My step's own brief said only "Audit two truths and a lie", so there was nothing to reconcile with the shared brief, and I followed the shared brief as written.

## What it looks and feels like now

The start screen is the site's article layout: a serif title, one line of lede, a "Best: 0 out of 10 · Games played: 0 · Average: —" line, a grey "Play ten rounds" button, a fieldset called "Group teams (optional)" with a dropdown, a "Lead a group" button, and a "How it works" section ([desktop-01](two-truths/desktop-01-start.png), [phone-01](two-truths/phone-01-start.png)). There is no picture of the game, no sample statement, and the two buttons are the same grey as the dropdown, so nothing says which one is the main way in. Choosing two teams adds two text inputs ([desktop-03](two-truths/desktop-03-start-teams-2.png)).

A round is "Round 1 of 10" in small text, the person's name in large serif, "Which statement was made up?", and three full-width grey boxes labelled A, B, C ([desktop-04](two-truths/desktop-04-round-1.png), [phone-04](two-truths/phone-04-round-1.png)). The boxes are 66 pixels tall on the desktop and the text is 18 pixels. Nothing happens on hover ([desktop-06](two-truths/desktop-06-round-1-hover.png) is identical to [desktop-04](two-truths/desktop-04-round-1.png)). Tapping one draws a thin gold outline round it and a second question appears underneath, "Is this the made-up one?", with "Yes, that's the lie" and "Pick again" ([desktop-07](two-truths/desktop-07-round-1-picked.png), [phone-07](two-truths/phone-07-round-1-picked.png)). Nothing moves at any point: the boxes appear, the outline appears, the confirm row appears.

The reveal is the game's best idea and its heaviest screen. The heading changes to "You spotted it" or "Not quite: that one is true." in serif, and under it the three statements become three stacked articles, each with a coloured left edge (solid green for True, dashed red for Made up), a label like "A · Made up · Your pick", the made-up sentence struck through, "The truth:" in bold, the Bible passage as a quotation with its reference and a "Read in context" link, and a "Reload evidence" button ([desktop-11](two-truths/desktop-11-round-1-reveal-correct.png), [phone-10](two-truths/phone-10-round-1-reveal-correct-top.png)). The one you picked also gets a gold box. On the desktop the reveal page is about 2,200 pixels tall and the Next button sits at 1,387, well below a 900-pixel screen ([desktop-12](two-truths/desktop-12-round-1-reveal-correct-fullpage.png)). On the phone it is about 3,500 pixels, with Next at 1,910, more than two screens down ([phone-12](two-truths/phone-12-round-1-reveal-correct-fullpage.png)). When you are wrong, the article at the top is the true one you picked, labelled green, and the made-up one is 600 to 900 pixels further down, so the first thing you see after "Not quite" is a green True box ([desktop-15](two-truths/desktop-15-round-2-reveal-wrong.png), [phone-15](two-truths/phone-15-round-2-reveal-wrong.png)). Some passages are very long: Joseph's two dreams run to 20 lines on the desktop ([desktop-21](two-truths/desktop-21-round-4-light-reveal.png)) and the spies list for Joshua fills a screen ([desktop-35](two-truths/desktop-35-group-finished.png)).

The end screen is a heading, "6/10 · Well read", a grey Share button, then "The stories behind the answers": a numbered list of all ten people with the lie, the truth, the passage and another Reload evidence button each ([desktop-24](two-truths/desktop-24-summary.png), [phone-24](two-truths/phone-24-summary.png)). It is 4,659 pixels tall on the desktop and 7,651 on the phone, about nine phone screens, with Play again at the very bottom ([desktop-25](two-truths/desktop-25-summary-fullpage.png)). Share, when the clipboard is blocked, shows a text box with a nice Wordle-style row of green and red squares ([desktop-26](two-truths/desktop-26-summary-share.png), [phone-26](two-truths/phone-26-summary-share.png)), but the status line above it is empty, so nothing says "copy this". Those squares are the one piece of game graphic in the whole thing, and they only exist in the share text.

Group mode is the same page with the statements as plain text instead of buttons, 28 pixels on the desktop and 18 on the phone, a "Lions: 0 · Doves: 0" line, and "Show the answer" and "Finish" buttons ([desktop-30](two-truths/desktop-30-group-round-1-fullpage.png), [phone-29](two-truths/phone-29-group-round-1.png)). The reveal adds "Lions got it right" and "Doves got it right" buttons under the three articles ([desktop-33](two-truths/desktop-33-group-awarded.png), [phone-33](two-truths/phone-33-group-awarded.png)). "Group finished" is a heading, the score line, and the same long list ([desktop-35](two-truths/desktop-35-group-finished.png), [phone-35](two-truths/phone-35-group-finished.png)). No winner is named.

Underneath it is well built: every statement links to its verse, the dealing avoids people and lies you have seen recently, a reload mid-round comes back to the same round, the light theme reads cleanly ([desktop-20](two-truths/desktop-20-round-4-light.png)), keyboard play works (A, B, C, Enter, Space, 1 to 4), and the console was clean apart from the site's two font preload notices. The statements themselves are good: "Elisha was mending fishing nets when Elijah called him", "Paul spoke to Lydia at the riverside in Corinth", "John the Baptist wore fine robes of linen" are plausible lies with clean corrections.

## Where it reads as a form or quiz instead of a game

- **The round is a multiple-choice question.** Three grey rows and a letter each, no cards, no entrance, no hover, no press. The only state change is a 2-pixel outline ([desktop-07](two-truths/desktop-07-round-1-picked.png)).
- **The confirm step is a second form.** "Is this the made-up one? Yes / Pick again" under the rows reads like a dialog, not like locking in a guess.
- **The reveal is an article, not a moment.** Right and wrong look the same: a serif heading and three stacked passages. There is no flip, no colour flood, no sound, and the Next button is one to two screens away ([phone-12](two-truths/phone-12-round-1-reveal-correct-fullpage.png)).
- **There is no running score.** "Round 3 of 10" is the only progress. The ten green and red squares that would show it only appear in the share text at the end ([desktop-26](two-truths/desktop-26-summary-share.png)).
- **The end screen is a heading over a reading list.** "6/10 · Well read" has no result card, no count-up, no comparison with your best, and Play again is nine phone screens down ([phone-24](two-truths/phone-24-summary.png)).
- **Group mode has no teams on screen.** Two names and two numbers in a line of text; the award buttons are grey like everything else; no winner is announced ([desktop-35](two-truths/desktop-35-group-finished.png)).

## How the best-known games of this kind do it

- **Fibbage** (Jackbox Party Pack): the lie-spotting game most people know. Every answer sits on its own card; you tap one; then the reveal turns the cards over one at a time with a drum roll, the lies are flung off the screen and the truth is left standing, and anyone fooled is named. The structure here is the same (three statements, one false), but Fibbage makes the turn-over the show.
- **Psych! Outwit Your Friends** (Warner Bros.): the same loop on phones. Big, colourful answer cards, a tap that lifts the card, a reveal where the fakes fall away and the true answer glows, points that fly up to a running total.
- **Kahoot** true/false and **HQ Trivia**: after the pick, the correct answer bar goes green and the others grey in one beat, with the pick marked, before any explanation. The explanation comes second, never first.
- **Duolingo**: a right answer slides a green sheet up from the bottom with a tick and a chime; a wrong one slides up a red sheet with the correction. The sheet holds the Next button, so the player never scrolls to continue.
- **Wordle** and this game's own share text: a row of coloured squares as the score. It is the right picture for ten rounds and it is already drawn in the share string.

## Improvements, ranked by impact for the effort

1. **Make the reveal a turn-over, and bring Next up to the first screen.** Keep the three statements where they are and turn each one over in place, A then B then C a quarter-second apart: True ones tint soft green with a tick, the made-up one tints red with a dashed edge and its sentence struck through, and your pick keeps its gold edge. Above them a short band slides in, "You spotted it" or "Not quite, C was the made-up one", with the Next button on the same band. Fold the passages behind a "Show the verse" line in each card, open by default only for the made-up one. The data and the colours all exist; this is a wrapper, two keyframes and a fold ([desktop-11](two-truths/desktop-11-round-1-reveal-correct.png), [phone-12](two-truths/phone-12-round-1-reveal-correct-fullpage.png)).
2. **Draw the statements as cards and lock in with one button.** Taller cards with a circled serif letter at the left, a small lift and shadow on hover, a press-down on tap, sliding in with a short stagger when the round begins. The picked card gets a filled gold edge and a "Your pick" tag, and the "Is this the made-up one?" row becomes one gold "That's the lie" button under the cards (keep Pick again as plain text beside it). Nothing about the site's palette changes ([desktop-04](two-truths/desktop-04-round-1.png), [desktop-07](two-truths/desktop-07-round-1-picked.png)).
3. **Put the ten squares on screen during play.** Replace "Round 3 of 10" with a row of ten small squares that fill green or red as you go, plus the current score beside them, and let the newest square pop when it fills. It is the share graphic that already exists ([desktop-26](two-truths/desktop-26-summary-share.png)) moved to the top of the round.
4. **Turn the end screen into a result card.** The score counts up from 0 to 6 in large serif, the band ("Well read") in the gold italic the games hub already uses, the ten squares under it, "New best!" when it is one, Share and Play again right there, and the ten stories behind each person's name as fold-outs below. Also give Share a real status line ("Copied", or "Copy this" above the text box) ([desktop-24](two-truths/desktop-24-summary.png), [phone-26](two-truths/phone-26-summary-share.png)).
5. **Show "Reload evidence" only when a passage failed.** It is a 50-pixel button under every passage, three per reveal and ten on the end screen, and it does nothing useful when the verse loaded ([desktop-11](two-truths/desktop-11-round-1-reveal-correct.png)). The alert for a failed load already exists; put the button inside it.
6. **Give group mode teams and a winner.** Each team gets a colour chip, the score line becomes two chips with large numbers, the "got it right" buttons become toggles that show a tick the moment they are tapped (right now the tapped state is invisible until the leader touches something else, see below), and "Group finished" names the leading team in its colour with the chips underneath ([desktop-33](two-truths/desktop-33-group-awarded.png), [desktop-35](two-truths/desktop-35-group-finished.png)).
7. **Show the game on the start screen.** A sample card trio ("Noah planted a vineyard", and two others, one tinted as the lie) in place of "How it works", a gold Play ten rounds as the main button, and the group setup behind one "Lead a group" fold. Keep the Best and Average line ([desktop-01](two-truths/desktop-01-start.png)).

## Phone problems

- **Next is two screens down after every answer.** The reveal page is about 3,500 pixels on an 844-pixel screen, and Next sits at 1,910. The player scrolls past three passages and three Reload buttons ten times a game ([phone-12](two-truths/phone-12-round-1-reveal-correct-fullpage.png)).
- **A wrong answer shows a green box first.** The true statement you picked is at the top with a green "True · Your pick" label; the made-up one is 600 pixels below. On a phone the first screen after "Not quite" contains no red at all ([phone-15](two-truths/phone-15-round-2-reveal-wrong.png)).
- **The end screen is about nine screens tall** and Play again is at the bottom ([phone-24](two-truths/phone-24-summary.png)).
- **In a group, the next round lands with the scores hidden.** After Next in group mode, the page keeps its scroll and the "Lions: 1 · Doves: 0" line sits behind the sticky header ([phone-42](two-truths/phone-42-group-round-2-as-landed-after-next.png), [desktop-42](two-truths/desktop-42-group-round-2-as-landed-after-next.png)). In solo mode the next round does return to the top ([phone-38](two-truths/phone-38-round-2-as-landed-after-next.png)).
- **Starting a group from a scrolled start page leaves the round off-screen.** If the leader has scrolled down to name the teams and taps "Lead a group", the first round appears with the statements above the top of the screen and only the buttons visible ([desktop-29](two-truths/desktop-29-group-round-1.png), [phone-29](two-truths/phone-29-group-round-1.png)).
- Done well: the statement boxes are full width and at least 66 pixels tall, all buttons are 50 pixels tall, the text is 18 pixels, nothing scrolls sideways, and the start screen fits Play ten rounds and Lead a group inside the first screen ([phone-01](two-truths/phone-01-start.png)).

## Accessibility

- **No visible keyboard focus.** Tabbing to a statement or to Next shows nothing ([desktop-19](two-truths/desktop-19-round-4-keyboard-focus-statement.png), [desktop-18](two-truths/desktop-18-round-3-reveal-focus-next.png)). The game's own stylesheet has a focus-visible rule, but the site's base stylesheet switches outlines off everywhere, marked as the owner's call of 6 October 2026, so the game's rule never applies. This matches earlier audits.
- **The award button's pressed state is hidden while it has focus.** The same site rule removes the gold outline that marks "Lions got it right" as pressed, for as long as that button is the focused element. On a phone that is until the leader taps something else, so the tap looks like it did nothing ([phone-40](two-truths/phone-40-group-award-just-tapped.png) against [phone-41](two-truths/phone-41-group-award-after-focus-leaves.png)). The score line far above does change, but it is off-screen. A tick inside the button, or a filled background, would not depend on the outline.
- **The Share status is empty when the clipboard is blocked.** The text box appears with no instruction, and the live region says nothing ([desktop-26](two-truths/desktop-26-summary-share.png)).
- Done well: True and Made up are words as well as colours, and the made-up edge is dashed as well as red; the picked statement uses aria-pressed; the result heading takes focus and is a live region; the first statement takes focus each round; A, B, C, Enter, Space and 1 to 4 all work and are explained on the start screen; the confirm row is a labelled group; the Share text box is labelled; every passage carries its reference and a link to read it in context.

## Anything broken

Nothing broke in play. Three things are worth knowing:

- **A failed game load shows the site's generic screen, not the game's.** When I blocked the game's code chunk the page showed "This page has been updated. Reload to get the latest version" ([desktop-37](two-truths/desktop-37-cant-load.png)) rather than the game's own "The statements could not be loaded" with Try again. The statements ship inside the game chunk, so the site's chunk-failure handler gets there first and the game's Try again path is not reachable on the built site.
- **The award tap looks like nothing happened** (described under Accessibility above).
- **Group rounds do not return to the top** after Next or after starting from a scrolled page (described under Phone problems above).

Two screenshot notes: the full-page captures show the site's sticky header part way down the page ([desktop-30](two-truths/desktop-30-group-round-1-fullpage.png)); that is the capture tool, not the site. And in [desktop-28](two-truths/desktop-28-start-group-named.png) the team-name inputs are shown filled with "Lions" and "Doves" from the audit script.
