# Redesign: Bible Jeopardy (/games/jeopardy)

**Written:** 11 November 2026, from the audit [jeopardy.md](jeopardy.md) and the shared brief [REDESIGN-BRIEF.md](REDESIGN-BRIEF.md). Before-screenshots are in [jeopardy/](jeopardy/), after-screenshots in [jeopardy/after/](jeopardy/after/), named the same way so they pair up (a name ending in `-light` is the light theme). My step's own brief and the shared brief did not disagree. My brief adds one thing: this game is built for a projector and a host, so the board, the clue, the scores and the Final must read across a room, and the host's flow (choose a tile, mark, show the answer, back to the board) must stay as it is. It does: no button moved, no key changed, and every new piece is bigger on the screen than what it replaced.

## 1. What changed

Bible Jeopardy now has the moments the audit said it lacked. When the host presses Start game the category titles drop in one after another and the tiles fill the board column by column, about two seconds in all. A tile the host chooses grows out of its place on the board to fill the stage, and the clue is on it; on the way back the clue shrinks into the tile's place and the value fades away, leaving the gap. A mark lands: the team's row flashes green and settles, or shakes and flashes red, a short chime or a low buzz plays, the score in the strip counts to its new number and "+300" floats up out of it and fades, so nothing stale is left on the strip. After a wrong answer the Right buttons of the teams that may steal lift twice to invite them. The Daily Double floods the stage in the team's colour with "Daily Double!" zooming in with a sting, held for a second and a half or until the host taps or presses a key, and then the bet form is there; once played, that tile carries a small gold "DD" on the board. The timer is three times thicker, in the game's colour, turning gold then red and pulsing for the last five seconds with the seconds beside it, and when it ends "Time!" stamps over the clue in large red serif with a buzz while the mark buttons stay usable. The four choices are dealt-in cards, and on the answer the right one tints green with a tick while the others fade. In the Final each team's row lands large when marked, right in green or wrong in red, with its score counting to its new value, and the next team's row deals in under it; a team at zero simply "Bets nothing". The results open on the winner's name very large in its team colour under a small "Winner" label, with one burst of gold and cream confetti, over a podium rising third, second, first in the teams' colours with the scores counting up; the scoreboard strip is gone from that screen. Sounds can be turned off in the game menu, and the choice is remembered. On a phone the board shows one category at a time, chosen from a row of chips that also say how many tiles each category has left, so every tile is readable and tappable. Rules, data, scoring, saved games, the board link, the host sheet, the keys and the address are unchanged; a game saved yesterday carries on today.

## 2. The audit's list, item by item

1. **Make the tile become the clue.** Built as asked. When a tile is clicked the stage measures where it sits and the clue grows from that place to fill the stage over about a third of a second; the clue's content does not change. On Back a ghost of the tile's colour shrinks from the stage into the tile's place while the played tile's value fades out, leaving the empty slab. The same growth serves the Daily Double. The state changes at once: the animation only decorates it, and under reduced motion it is gone.
2. **Make a mark land.** Built as asked. Right: the team's row flashes green and pops, a two-note chime plays, and its score in the strip counts up over two thirds of a second while "+300" floats up and fades. Wrong: the row shakes and flashes red, a low buzz plays, and the score counts down with "−300" floating. The stale delta the audit found is gone, because the float lasts a second and a half and nothing stays. "Sounds: on" and "Sounds: off" are in the game menu, remembered in this browser; sound is never the only signal.
3. **Give the Daily Double its moment.** Built as asked. Before the bet form the stage fills with the team's colour and "Daily Double!" zooms in from small to large, with the team's name under it and a rising three-note sting, held for a second and a half or until the host taps or presses any key; the bet form is underneath from the first instant, so nothing waits. The board tile that held it shows a small gold "DD" once played.
4. **Make the timer visible from the back of the room.** Built as asked, with the kit's timer bar. The bar is up to three times thicker in the game's colour, gold for the second half, red and pulsing with the seconds for the last five, and the seconds are a number at its right end. When it ends, "Time!" stamps over the clue in large red serif with a two-tone buzz, and the mark buttons stay usable. The Final's thirty seconds use the same bar and keep the Pause button.
5. **Turn the results into a podium.** Built as asked, with the kit's podium. The winner's name is the heading, very large in its team colour under a small "Winner" label, with a confetti burst of gold and cream; three steps rise third, second, first in the team colours with the scores counting up, a shared place puts two names on one step, and teams past third are listed under. When first place is shared the heading says "Tied for first" with the names, the Tie-breaker button is the primary one, and pressing it plays a short drum roll before the tie-breaker clue. The scoreboard strip is hidden on this screen. The live youth-group quiz already uses this podium, so the two games share one.
6. **Reveal the Final question bets one at a time.** Built as asked. The host's number box stays. When Right or Wrong is pressed the row lands large in green or red with "Right, +300" or "Wrong, −300" and the team's score counts to its new value, and the next team's row deals in under it, lowest score first as before. A team at zero shows "Bets nothing" instead of a box it could get wrong, and its landed row reads "Bet nothing".
7. **Deal the board at the start.** Built as asked. The category titles drop in one by one and the tiles fill column by column, about two seconds in all, on the first board of the night (also when carrying on a saved game). The team to choose is already raised on its chip. It does not replay after each clue.

**My own additions.** The steal invitation (after a wrong answer in Take turns, the Right buttons of the teams that may still answer lift twice). The phone chips double as a board summary, saying how many tiles each category has left. The four choices are cards that deal in with a stagger and tick on the answer (the audit asked for a tick under Accessibility). The right choice's letter turns green too, so the mark is not colour alone. Every button, pill and tile shows focus as a lift and a gold or coloured edge (the site has no focus outline), and the pills on the setup page are 44 pixels tall.

## 3. Faults fixed

- **The phone board** (the one real defect): one category at a time at phone width, chosen by chips that follow the focused column; the title is large, the tiles are 56 pixels tall with the value in big type, and nothing scrolls sideways. Arrow keys still move through the whole board.
- **After Build board the page landed at the bottom:** the kit's scroll-to-top runs when the page changes screen (setup, preview, stage) and when the stage changes screen, so the preview and every stage screen open at the top.
- **The preview was a very long page on a phone:** at phone width the value slabs are left out and each category is one block with its title, its line and its tools.
- **The clue with the four choices needed scrolling to mark on a phone:** the choices are one column of compact cards, the clue type is sized for the phone, and the row of mark buttons sticks to the bottom of the stage, which now fills the phone's height.
- **The scoreboard wrapped to three lines on a phone:** it is a two-column grid with the score on the right of each chip. The adjuster, which the first play-through found running off the left edge of the phone with its Done button out of reach, now floats the full width of the phone just above the strip.
- **"Board link copied." lingered** into the next game: the status clears when Play again or New board deals a board.
- **The score delta lingered** beside a team's score across screens: the float fades within a second and a half.
- **"Wrong, −0" and the "from 0 to 0" error** for a team at zero: the row says "Bets nothing" and has no box; the landed row says "Bet nothing".
- **The stage theme menu item did nothing on first press:** it now offers the opposite of what the host sees (Stage: light on the dark site), read from the page's own theme.
- **Replay a tile gave no cue:** while it is on, the played tiles take a gold edge and tint and the unplayed ones fade.
- **"TO CHOOSE" and the delta were tiny on a projector:** both scale with the screen's height, in gold.
- **Daily Double, timer and the right choice were colour or position alone:** the splash names the team, the timer has its number and the stamp, the right choice has a tick and a green letter.
