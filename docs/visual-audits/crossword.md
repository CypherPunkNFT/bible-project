# Visual audit: Bible crossword (/games/crossword)

Audited 10 November 2026 on the local preview. I played the game through from a fresh start at desktop size (1440 wide) and again at phone size (390 wide): the start screen with its theme, book, level and size choices; a Whole Bible, Easy, Medium puzzle solved honestly from the clues, with a wrong letter checked, a whole wrong word checked, a letter and a word revealed, the "reveal all" confirmation, Clear, Tab between clues and the on-screen keys; a reload mid-puzzle and after solving; a Hard, Large puzzle to see the widest grid; a Small puzzle solved with no help; "New puzzle" and "Change puzzle"; and the light theme. No code was changed. The screenshots are in [crossword/](crossword/). Names starting `desktop-` or `phone-` give the screen size and the number gives the stage.

My step's own brief said only "audit the crossword", so there was nothing to reconcile with the shared brief, and I followed the shared brief as written.

## What it looks and feels like now

The start screen is a clean settings page ([desktop-01](crossword/desktop-01-start.png), [phone-01](crossword/phone-01-start.png)). A serif "Bible crossword", one line of invitation, then four groups of pill chips and a drop-down: eleven themes, a "Choose a book" list of 29 books, Easy, Medium or Hard, and Small, Medium or Large with "About 7 words, on a grid of up to 9 by 9 squares" underneath. A gold "Make my crossword" button finishes it, and after a win a grey "Crosswords solved on this device: 1" appears beside it ([desktop-23](crossword/desktop-23-start-after-solved.png)). Chips that cannot be offered grey out; with Genesis at Hard only Small is left ([desktop-05](crossword/desktop-05-start-book-chosen.png)). There is no picture, no preview of a grid, and nothing that says "puzzle" before the puzzle.

In play, the desktop is two columns ([desktop-07](crossword/desktop-07-play-first-open.png)). On the left a grey bar carries the current clue, "1 DOWN · Where did the Lord tell Paul he must also tell people about Him? (4)", under it the grid, under that a faded QWERTY keyboard, and under that, below the fold on a 900-tall screen, two rows of small pill buttons: Check Letter, Word, Puzzle; Reveal Letter, Word, Puzzle; Clear, New puzzle, Change puzzle ([desktop-16](crossword/desktop-16-play-mid-play-fullpage.png)). On the right, Across and Down lists with the current clue tinted gold and a gold left bar. The grid is drawn as outlined dark squares floating on the page background inside a thin rectangular frame; there are no solid blocks, so a medium puzzle reads as a scattered shape with a lot of dark emptiness around it rather than a crossword from a newspaper ([desktop-25](crossword/desktop-25-play-large-open.png) shows the Large grid, 17 squares wide). The selected square is a strong gold, the rest of its word a dim gold band.

Typing is immediate and well behaved. A letter appears and the selection steps on; the clue bar follows; tapping the selected square at a crossing flips from Down to Across; Tab moves to the next clue; arrow keys work. A checked wrong letter turns red with a diagonal slash ([desktop-11](crossword/desktop-11-play-check-letter-wrong.png), [phone-11](crossword/phone-11-play-check-letter-wrong.png)); a whole wrong word checked is a column of red Xs ([desktop-12](crossword/desktop-12-play-check-puzzle-wrong.png)). A revealed letter gets a small purple corner ([desktop-13](crossword/desktop-13-play-reveal-letter.png), [desktop-14](crossword/desktop-14-play-reveal-word.png)). Reveal Puzzle turns into a red-outlined "Yes, reveal all" with no cancel beside it ([desktop-19](crossword/desktop-19-play-reveal-all-confirm.png)). Finished clues go grey in the list; clues that cross the current word get a faint grey bar. Nothing on the screen moves at any point: no letter pops in, no word lights up when complete, no count of words done.

The end arrives silently. The moment the last right letter goes in, the grid tints faintly green, the clue bar vanishes, and the right column becomes a serif "Solved!", a grey line such as "4 letters revealed. 2 checks used." or "Every letter yours, with no checks and nothing revealed.", a gold New puzzle and an outlined Change puzzle, and then "The answers", a long list of every word with its clue and a gold link to the verse or person page ([desktop-20](crossword/desktop-20-solved.png), [desktop-33](crossword/desktop-33-solved-clean.png), [phone-20](crossword/phone-20-solved.png)). The answer list with verse links is genuinely good and is the part that feels most like this site. But there is no time, no streak, no flourish, and the puzzle you just finished sits unchanged on the left.

Underneath, the game is solid: reloads resume mid-puzzle and after solving ([desktop-29](crossword/desktop-29-play-resumed-after-reload.png), [desktop-22](crossword/desktop-22-solved-after-reload.png)), Clear keeps revealed letters, the light theme is clean and paper-like ([desktop-37](crossword/desktop-37-light-play-checked-and-revealed.png), [desktop-36](crossword/desktop-36-light-start.png)), and there were no console errors in any run. It is a competent crossword engine wearing a settings page.

## Where it reads as a form or quiz instead of a game

- **The start screen is a preferences form.** Four labelled groups of toggles and a drop-down, then a submit button ([desktop-02](crossword/desktop-02-start-fullpage.png)). Nothing shows what you are about to get.
- **The grid does not look like a crossword.** Empty space is page-coloured, squares are thin outlines, and the frame draws a big rectangle round all of it, so the puzzle floats in a void ([desktop-07](crossword/desktop-07-play-first-open.png)). Every printed and app crossword uses solid blocks in a tight frame.
- **The tools are a toolbar.** Nine small pills in two rows with tiny grey captions "CHECK" and "REVEAL" ([desktop-16](crossword/desktop-16-play-mid-play-fullpage.png)). They look like a filter bar, and on desktop they sit below the fold where a player never sees them.
- **No feedback while playing.** A completed word looks the same as a half-done one until you ask for a check. The only progress signal is clues turning grey in a list.
- **Solving is a heading.** "Solved!" plus a stats sentence ([desktop-20](crossword/desktop-20-solved.png)). The grid does not celebrate, there is no time, and nothing distinguishes a clean solve from a revealed one except one grey line.
- **The clue lists are plain lists**, which is normal for a crossword, but the current clue is only a tint and the crossings only a faint bar, so the lists do not feel connected to the grid.

## How the best-known games of this kind do it

- **The New York Times Crossword and the Mini** (web and app): solid black blocks, a yellow current square inside a blue word band, a timer in the header, and on a phone the grid pinned at the top with the current clue on a bar with left and right arrows pinned just above the keyboard. Finishing the Mini pops a "Congratulations!" card with your time and a little animation; the squares flash. Check and Reveal are two menus, not nine buttons.
- **The Guardian's crossword** (web): the grid left and clues right like this site, but the grid is black-blocked, the current clue sits on a sticky bar under the grid, and "Check this", "Reveal this", "Check all", "Clear all" are grouped with a plain cancel. An anagram helper gives the page a toy.
- **Crosswords With Friends** (Zynga, phone): a daily puzzle on a card with a date and theme, squares that pop as you type, a word that flashes when it is correct, a confetti finish with stars and time, and a streak calendar. It is the clearest example of a crossword made into a game rather than a page.
- **Apple News+ Puzzles and LA Times (Arkadium)**: the grid auto-zooms so the current word is always visible on a phone, a completed word gives a soft pulse, and the end screen shows time, hints used and a share card.
- **Wordscapes and Crossword Jam**: not clued crosswords, but the models for letter feedback: tiles that bounce in, a gold wave across a finished word, and a short burst of sparkles for a finished level.

## Improvements, ranked by impact for the effort

1. **Rebuild the phone layout around the grid and the keyboard.** Pin the grid to the top, put the current clue on a bar with "‹" and "›" arrows directly above the keyboard, and pin that whole lower block to the bottom of the screen, as the NYT Mini does. The Across and Down lists move to a sheet or a tab you pull up, and the tools go behind one "⋯" button. On the phone today the player taps "Make my crossword" and lands looking at the keyboard with the grid and the clue off the top of the screen ([phone-38](crossword/phone-38-play-landing-after-make.png)), then scrolls through four screens of page to find the clue lists ([phone-16](crossword/phone-16-play-mid-play-fullpage.png)). This one change fixes most of the phone problems below.
2. **Make the grid look like a crossword.** Fill the empty squares with the ink colour so the puzzle has solid blocks, and crop the frame to the filled shape instead of the whole rectangle. Make the numbers a point or two larger, give the current square a gold ring as well as a fill, and show a tiny arrow in it for the direction. On the light theme the squares already look like paper ([desktop-37](crossword/desktop-37-light-play-checked-and-revealed.png)); the dark theme needs the same contrast. This is a stylesheet change.
3. **Let the finish be an event.** When the last letter goes in, run a gold wave across the squares from top left to bottom right, scale "Solved!" in over a quarter of a second with a short burst of confetti in the site's gold and cream, and show the time taken and "12 of 12 words" above the stats line. A clean solve ("Every letter yours") gets a gold laurel or star; a helped solve gets the plain version. The answers list stays as it is. One keyframe set and a timer that is already easy to keep in the saved game.
4. **Give typing and words feedback.** A typed letter scales in from slightly large to normal; the on-screen key dips on press; when every square of a word is filled, the word band pulses once, and if it is right the clue in the list gets a small gold tick instead of only going grey. This is where Crosswords With Friends and Wordscapes earn their feel, and it is a few lines of CSS plus one class on a completed word.
5. **Show progress.** A thin gold bar under the clue bar, "7 of 12 words", filling as words complete, and on the start screen a small "Solved 3 · best time 4:12" in place of the grey sentence. Players of Wordle-style games on this site already expect a tally.
6. **Tidy the tools and protect progress.** Collapse Check and Reveal into two small menus ("Check ▾", "Reveal ▾"), put a Cancel next to "Yes, reveal all", and ask before New puzzle or Change puzzle throws away a half-done grid; today both discard it with no warning ([desktop-30](crossword/desktop-30-play-new-puzzle.png)). Move the tools above the keyboard so they are visible on a desktop.
7. **Turn the start screen into a shelf of puzzles.** Each theme becomes a card with a tiny grid thumbnail and its name, the sizes are drawn as small, medium and large grids rather than words, and the chosen combination shows a preview of the grid shape before "Make my crossword". The drop-down of 29 books stays as it is behind an "Or one book" card.

## Phone problems

- **Landing in the wrong place.** Tapping "Make my crossword" keeps the page scrolled where the button was, so the puzzle opens with the clue bar and the grid above the top of the screen and the keyboard and tools filling the view ([phone-38](crossword/phone-38-play-landing-after-make.png)).
- **The selected square hides under the header.** Tapping a clue in the list scrolls the grid so the selected square sits at the very top of the page, behind the 61-pixel sticky header ([phone-39](crossword/phone-39-after-tapping-clue-in-list.png)). The player sees the dim word band but not the square they are typing into.
- **The medium grid is ten pixels too wide.** At 390 wide a 13-square grid comes to 368 pixels in a 358-pixel frame, so the right column is clipped and the frame scrolls sideways by a sliver ([phone-11](crossword/phone-11-play-check-letter-wrong.png)). The Large grid scrolls properly but shows about twelve of seventeen columns ([phone-25](crossword/phone-25-play-large-open.png), [phone-27](crossword/phone-27-play-large-grid-scrolled-right.png)).
- **The page is four screens tall.** Clue bar, grid, keyboard, three rows of tools, then the Across and Down lists, with a medium puzzle running to about 3,400 pixels ([phone-16](crossword/phone-16-play-mid-play-fullpage.png)). Reading a clue in the list means losing sight of the grid.
- **Squares and numbers are small.** Squares are 28 pixels, below the usual 44-pixel touch size, and the clue numbers are 7 pixels tall, which is hard to read on a phone.
- **The clue bar is not sticky**, so once the grid is scrolled the player has no clue in view.
- Done well: the on-screen keys are 46 pixels tall with a wide backspace, no phone keyboard is ever summoned so the page never zooms, tool buttons do not keep a hover ring after a tap, and a reload resumes the puzzle exactly ([phone-29](crossword/phone-29-play-resumed-after-reload.png)).

## Accessibility

- **No visible keyboard focus on the start screen or the tools.** Tabbing to a theme chip or "Make my crossword" shows nothing: the computed outline is none and there is no shadow ([desktop-03](crossword/desktop-03-start-primary-focused.png)). The grid squares do have a focus outline, which is right.
- **Greyed-out sizes explain themselves only on hover.** "Not enough clues for this size at this level" is a mouse tooltip, so touch and screen-reader users get a disabled chip and no reason.
- **The keyboard shortcuts are invisible.** Tab, Shift-Tab, Space to switch direction and the arrows all work, but nothing on screen says so.
- Done well: each square announces "3 Down, letter 2 of 7"; wrong and revealed squares carry a slash or a corner as well as a colour; the clue bar is a polite live region; "Solved!" takes focus and is announced; muted text is the site's usual grey at about 6.8 to 1 on the dark background; the only motion is a chip colour transition, which honours reduced-motion.

## Anything broken

Nothing broke. The only console warnings at either size were two site-wide font preload notices that belong to the page shell, not the game. Three things are worth knowing:

- The medium grid's ten-pixel sideways clip on a phone, above, is a sizing formula that stops at 28 pixels a square, which times 13 is wider than the space.
- "New puzzle" and "Change puzzle" discard a puzzle in progress with no confirmation, while "Reveal puzzle" asks. The two should agree.
- The "Yes, reveal all" confirmation stays armed until it is used; typing letters or checking does not disarm it, so the red button can sit there for the rest of the game ([desktop-19](crossword/desktop-19-play-reveal-all-confirm.png)).
