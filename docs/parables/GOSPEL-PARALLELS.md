# Gospel parallels: implementation step 5

Implemented 11 October 2026 after the owner authorized the next step. This addresses the two passage omissions reserved in the inventory/content reviews and improves account selection throughout the collection. It does not claim an exhaustive catalogue of every repeated Gospel saying.

## Passage changes

| Study | Added passage | Setting retained |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The lamp on its stand | Luke 11:33 | The crowd has heard Jesus answer the demand for a sign in 11:29–32. The lamp leads into the eye/body comparison in 11:34–36. Luke 8:16–18 remains a distinct selection following the sower. |
| The tree and its fruit | Matthew 12:33–37 | Jesus confronts his accusers following the exorcism controversy in 12:22–32. Speech expresses the heart and is subject to judgment. Matthew 7:15–20 retains its warning about false prophets; Luke 6:43–45 retains its heart/speech setting. |

The site's KJV text was checked for the added verses and the surrounding settings before writing. The relevant study audiences, overview paragraphs and comparison notes now acknowledge both occurrences within the same Gospel. No occasion is reconstructed from shared imagery alone. The separate eye/light study remains separate; the lamp note directs attention to its adjoining passage without merging the two images.

Contemporary Christian cross-checks, consulted directly on 11 October 2026: [Douglas Sean O’Donnell, Matthew, section 12:30–37](https://www.thegospelcoalition.org/commentary/matthew/) and [Peter G. Bolt, Luke, sections 11:29–33 and 11:33–36](https://www.thegospelcoalition.org/commentary/luke/). These are the registered online TGC commentaries; the notes rely on the observed passage sequence and wording, not on unverified historical reconstruction. Source roles and confessional limits remain those in SOURCE-REGISTER.md.

## Account presentation

Every selection button now gives the full passage reference rather than only a Gospel name. Distinct passage URLs supply stable keys, so two Luke or Matthew occurrences do not collide. The selected reference appears in the reading header and above its own setting note; the reader link follows the same selection. Buttons wrap on narrow screens and retain visible keyboard focus. The explanatory line identifies the upper study as an overview drawing on the listed passages, rather than implying that selecting an account rewrites all its story moments.

The collection remains 57 studies in seven themes, with the desktop four-by-two category selector preserved. Passage coverage increases from 94 ranges / 604 verse references to 96 ranges / 610 verse references. These are references counted with repetition across studies, not a count of unique Bible verses.
