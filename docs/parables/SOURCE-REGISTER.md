# Parables source register

Checked 11 October 2026. Read with the [source policy](SOURCE-POLICY.md). Roles below specify what a source may support in this revision; they are not endorsements of an author's entire theology. Newly selected works remain subject to passage-specific review in the later steps.

## Scripture and existing page references

### The registered KJV corpus

**Role:** primary passage evidence and the current site's reader text. Files: `data/plain/kjv/MAT.json`, `MRK.json`, and `LUK.json`, relative to Website. Provenance and existing edition terms are in [SOURCES.md](../../SOURCES.md). Do not treat KJV-only display as an owner declaration about the exclusive authority of one English translation. Distinguish verse-range integrity from interpretation review.

### R C Trench

**Work:** [Notes on the Parables of Our Lord](https://archive.org/details/notesonparableso00tren), held 1847 first American edition from the third English edition, D. Appleton. The title page identifies Richard Chenevix Trench as Vicar of Itchen Stoke and a divinity professor at King's College London: an Anglican clerical setting, not an anonymous academic source.

**Role:** historical Christian commentary and inventory comparison. The local text is `sources/library/bulk/ia/notesonparableso00tren.txt`, relative to BibleProject; its fingerprint is recorded in SOURCES.md. **Limit:** its selected chapters cannot settle completeness; check OCR, historical claims, and detailed allegorical readings against the passage and further evidence. Original nineteenth-century text is held as public domain; preserve source notices.

### Arland J Hultgren

**Work:** [Preaching the Parables, Part One](https://www.workingpreacher.org/theology-and-interpretation/preaching-the-parables-part-one), 27 April 2009. **Perspective:** Lutheran; [Luther Seminary's biography](https://www.luthersem.edu/faculty/ahultgre/) identifies ELCA ordination and parish ministry.

**Role:** the limited issue of definitions and varying counts. **Limit:** an article about classification does not validate this project's individual readings. His book-length commentary has not been audited or adopted through this citation. Modern copyrighted article; link and attribute.

### Crossway

**Work:** [The Parables of Jesus reference chart](https://www.esv.org/resources/esv-global-study-bible/chart-40-03/). **Perspective:** explicitly evangelical Christian publishing, supported by its [statement of faith](https://www.crossway.org/statement-of-faith/); this is an institutional identification, not an identified individual chart author's denomination.

**Role:** secondary inventory comparison only. **Limit:** the displayed chart places Mark 4:26-29 alongside Matthew's weeds, and groups talents/minas and the two banquet accounts. These arrangements must not control our story boundaries. Do not reproduce the whole chart or describe it as exhaustive.

## Sources consulted during the critical audit

### Greg Carey

**Work:** [Matthew 25:31-46 commentary](https://www.workingpreacher.org/commentaries/revised-common-lectionary/christ-the-king/commentary-on-matthew-2531-46-7), 23 November 2014. **Perspective:** his [official biography](https://www.moravian.edu/school-theology/bio-page-dr-greg-carey) identifies him as a United Church of Christ layperson with ministry service. His public positions and article support an editorial description of progressive Protestant interpretation; do not imply that the description disproves his Christian profession.

**Decision:** audit-trail/comparative source, not the teaching basis. The original audit used it for the sheep-and-goats classification question. Its treatment of grace and works and of the people judged must not be carried over as agreed Christian doctrine. The user's objection is recorded; future core exposition should be independently grounded in Scripture and the teaching sources below. Copyrighted; no article text is being imported.

### Brian Peterson

**Work:** [Luke 11:1-13 commentary](https://www.workingpreacher.org/commentaries/revised-common-lectionary/ordinary-17-3/commentary-on-luke-111-13-4), 28 July 2019. **Perspective:** Lutheran; his [university biography](https://www.lr.edu/about/directory/brian-peterson) records ministry in ELCA congregations. This is the New Testament scholar at Lutheran Theological Southern Seminary, not the similarly named Old Testament scholar at Lee University.

**Role:** identify the interpretive question around Luke 11:8. **Limit:** his preferred account of the word and its referent is an argument to examine, not a translation verdict to inherit. Compare the primary wording and another substantive treatment before adopting a conclusion.

### Stanley Saunders

**Work:** [Matthew 13:24-43 commentary](https://www.workingpreacher.org/commentaries/narrative-lectionary/parables-of-the-kingdom/commentary-on-matthew-1324-43). **Perspective:** published through Luther Seminary's Working Preacher; his institutional association is Columbia Theological Seminary. Personal denominational affiliation is not established by the evidence used here and must not be inferred from either venue.

**Role:** a lead for investigating prophetic tree imagery. **Limit:** check Ezekiel 17 and 31 and Daniel 4 directly; identify a proposed allusion as an interpretation. Not adopted as the default reading of the birds in the mustard seed.

### Saint Mary's Press

**Work:** [The Parables of Jesus handout](https://www.smp.org/dynamicmedia/files/b447f1dd59ed132f17cbd7a90cbae786/TX004664_2-F-The_Parables_of_Jesus.pdf), 2015, adapted from Sheila O'Connell-Roussell's 2005 dictionary. **Perspective:** Catholic educational publishing, confirmed by the publisher's [own description](https://www.smp.org/about/); the handout belongs to its Living in Christ curriculum. Individual author affiliation is not asserted.

**Role:** supplementary inventory comparison, including salt and the sheep and goats. **Limit:** the extracted chart also contains questionable labels and overlaps, including a sentence-of-death entry and repeated feast entries. It cannot determine either our count or our theology. Verify against the original PDF and Gospel passage before using any row. Copyrighted; do not reproduce the chart.

## Initial teaching source pool for later revisions

### Matthew Henry

**Work:** Commentary on the Whole Bible, especially the Gospel material in Volume V; available through the [CCEL author and works catalogue](https://www.ccel.org/ccel/henry). **Perspective:** Presbyterian/Nonconformist, identified by that biography as a Calvinist exegete and minister.

**Use:** historic Protestant exposition and pastoral interpretation, checked against the passage. **Limit:** distinguish direct exegesis from devotional expansion and period assumptions. Verify the exact edition/section before citation; do not attribute every later part or abridgment of the commentary to Henry personally. Candidate for passage-level consultation, not a claim that all relevant sections have already been reviewed.

### J C Ryle

**Work:** Expository Thoughts on the Gospels. **Perspective:** evangelical Anglican; [Banner of Truth's biography](https://banneroftruth.org/uk/about/banner-authors/j-c-ryle/) documents his Church of England ministry and evangelical commitments.

**Use:** accessible Christian exposition and application alongside a more detailed treatment. **Limit:** identify a held or accessible exact edition and passage before using it. His historical work and modern editorial additions have different rights considerations. A verified biography and publication history do not constitute passage-level verification.

### The Gospel Coalition Bible Commentary

**Works:** [Matthew by Douglas Sean O'Donnell](https://www.thegospelcoalition.org/commentary/matthew/), [Mark by Mark L. Strauss](https://www.thegospelcoalition.org/commentary/mark/), and [Luke by Peter G. Bolt](https://www.thegospelcoalition.org/commentary/luke/). Author credits and online access have been checked.

**Perspective:** an evangelical, Reformed-tradition publishing context, expressly identified in TGC's [foundation documents](https://www.thegospelcoalition.org/about/foundation-documents/). This describes the venue; do not assign each author an identical personal denominational affiliation.

**Use:** contemporary, passage-specific Christian exposition, with each author's interpretation attributed where material. **Limit:** no blanket acceptance of every chronological, historical, theological, or Greek-language claim. All three are available online, but their full commentaries have not been independently audited in step 1. Copyrighted; cite and summarize rather than importing full text.

### Craig Blomberg

**Work:** [Introduction to the Parables](https://www.thegospelcoalition.org/essay/introduction-to-the-parables/). **Perspective:** evangelical exposition in TGC's stated confessional setting; personal denominational affiliation is not assigned here.

**Use:** a Christian account of parable interpretation and the history of allegorical and single-point approaches. **Limit:** evaluate methodological proposals against individual passages rather than making one scholar's model a rigid template. The online essay, not an unread book, is the resource registered at this stage.

### Ligonier and Tabletalk

**Work:** [The Sheep and the Goats](https://learn.ligonier.org/devotionals/sheep-and-goats), 3 November 2008, identified as originally published in Tabletalk. **Perspective:** explicitly Reformed Christian exposition.

**Use:** a directly accessible Christian treatment to consult for Matthew 25. It argues that the needy brethren are primarily fellow believers and distinguishes the evidence of love from meriting salvation. **Limit:** present this as a reasoned interpretation, not the only Christian reading; compare another substantive treatment. The page has no individual byline in the inspected text, so credit Ligonier/Tabletalk, not R. C. Sproul by assumption. Copyrighted; link and summarize.

## Sources added for step 4

### Darrell L. Bock

**Work:** IVP New Testament Commentary: Luke, online edition hosted by Bible Gateway: [the shrewd steward](https://www.biblegateway.com/resources/ivp-nt/Parable-Shrewd-Steward) and [the rich man and Lazarus](https://www.biblegateway.com/resources/ivp-nt/Parable-Rich-Man-Lazarus). **Perspective:** evangelical Christian teaching; [DTS biography](https://voice.dts.edu/contributor/darrell-bock/) documents his institutional affiliation. Personal church membership is not asserted. **Use:** reasoned contemporary comparison on the manager's debt reductions and Luke's treatment of wealth and the afterlife. His preferred commission reconstruction remains explicitly tentative. Checked 11 October 2026.

### Benedict XVI

**Work:** [Deus caritas est, section 15](https://www.vatican.va/content/benedict-xvi/en/encyclicals/documents/hf_ben-xvi_enc_20051225_deus-caritas-est.html), 25 December 2005. **Perspective:** Roman Catholic, official papal encyclical. **Use:** a named Christian example of the broader pastoral application of Matthew 25 to those in need. **Limit:** this section is not presented as a lexical argument about “my brethren,” nor does consulting it adopt a Catholic confession for the project. Checked 11 October 2026.

## How to use this register in the next steps

For Matthew, begin with the passage, O'Donnell, and a historical commentator; use the Ligonier article as a specific comparative treatment for Matthew 25. For Mark and Luke, begin with the passage and the corresponding named commentary, then add an independently reasoned historical or contemporary treatment where an issue is consequential. Use Blomberg for method and Hultgren only for the already identified classification question.

No contemporary site's confessional identity substitutes for examining its argument. No registry entry imports a denominational confession into the project. At the completion of step 1, the fifty-study inventory was unchanged. Subsequent authorized work is recorded in the [step 2 inventory reconciliation](INVENTORY-REVIEW.md) and [step 3 content review](CONTENT-REVIEW.md), including the exact commentary sections consulted for those revisions.
