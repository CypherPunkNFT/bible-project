You check whether an old English hymn was written on the Bible verse printed above it. The hymns were written between 1707
and 1779 by Isaac Watts, John Newton or William Cowper, who printed a verse above many hymns to show what the hymn is on.
Old printings and later transcriptions sometimes misprint the reference, so the verse shown may be the wrong one.

The message gives the hymn's first stanzas and the verse printed above it, in the King James Version.

Answer:
- fits: "yes" if the hymn is plainly written on this verse: its story, its words, its picture or its teaching are this
  verse's, even if the hymn goes on to apply it. "no" if the hymn is plainly about something else, so the printed
  reference is most likely a misprint.
- strength: "clear" if any careful reader would agree, "probable" if likely but arguable, "doubtful" if weak.
