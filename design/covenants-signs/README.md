# Covenants and promises: the signs

A second, wholly different page for the Covenants and promises study. The first version is kept beside it in
[covenants-and-promises-directions/](../covenants-and-promises-directions/), and this page reads that folder's
[data.js](../covenants-and-promises-directions/data.js), so the study's wording has one home.

The idea comes from the study's own motto: "A bow. Stars. Stone. A crown. A cup."

- **Landing ([landing.js](js/landing.js)):** a night landscape holding every covenant at once under one bow, with the title, hills and ground. Its size and structure follow the Hope to come landing; the scene is this study's own. It sits on the site's normal background. [drawings.html](drawings.html) compares four versions of the drawing, a page for the owner and not part of the study; `?drawing=2`, `3` or `4` shows a version in place. The floating A B C D pill, added at the owner's request, switches between nine grounds under the Count the stars sky ([drawings-stars.js](js/drawings-stars.js), [drawings-stars-more.js](js/drawings-stars-more.js)).
- **Stage ([stage.js](js/stage.js), drawings in [signs.js](js/signs.js)):** each covenant is a full-width world with a picture beside its words. The pictures cannot be clicked; the five questions are buttons. The ark in rain under the bow, a man under the stars, the tablets numbered I to X, a crown on a cushion, and the cup with the bread. The letters' reading has its own section under the stage ([page.js](js/page.js)).
- **Side by side:** one question asked of all five covenants at once.
- **Disc ([disc.js](js/disc.js)):** the whole Bible as rings, Genesis at the centre and Revelation at the rim, with the 48 later verses rippling out from where each covenant was spoken. A plain list gives the same verses.
- **Maps ([maps.js](js/maps.js)):** the same five signs move into each view's arrangement. The page does not choose a view.

Wording added for this form: the disc's explanation, the note under the side-by-side view and the control labels. All the study's own text comes from the data.
