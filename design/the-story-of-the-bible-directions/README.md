Study 55, an Explore the Bible study and the shelf's map: the whole Bible as one story in eight movements, from the garden to the city. Its one habit: **before you read any verse, ask where you are in the story.** So the page is a map with a *you are here* pin. Eight stations stand on one road, each in the site's colour for the part of the Bible its readings come from. Six promise threads run under the road, and choosing a station answers the study's three questions for that point: where am I, what has God promised by this point, what is still waiting.

## How it is built

[build-data.py](build-data.py), with [scripture_text.py](scripture_text.py) (a byte-identical copy of the model's reader, sha256 `9f674d78…109480e8`, the same as in [the-good-news-directions/](../the-good-news-directions/)), reads the study's content.md and [study_lists.py](study_lists.py), checks them against the site's Berean text, and writes [data.js](data.js) through a temporary file and a rename. Run it from the Website folder:

- `python design/the-story-of-the-bible-directions/build-data.py` builds.
- `--check` runs every check and fails if data.js is not what the inputs build now. If the only change is a study link (a sister study's mock-up appearing), it says so: "study links changed … rebuild needed".
- `--selftest` ([build_selftest.py](build_selftest.py)) feeds wrong inputs in memory and fails unless each one is refused with its own expected message; it compares data.js's bytes before and after, and checks no temporary file is left.

It writes no bytecode caches. The page is [index.html](index.html), [js/sb.js](js/sb.js), [js/art.js](js/art.js) and [css/sb.css](css/sb.css), with the frame borrowed from the Authors and Manuscripts mock-ups, as in the model. All text goes into the page as text; only the fixed drawings in art.js and the shared icons are markup. `?at=` (the chosen station) and `?ref=` (an open verse) are read only against known values. Nothing under `src/` was touched.

One direction was built, as for The good news, because the text already existed.

## Its shape, and why it is not a sibling

The shape is in the text: the study calls itself "the shelf's map", gives three questions to ask of any passage, names one thread to follow (the nations), and in its last movement says the Bible's last pages answer its first ones, naming the promises that run between them. The page draws exactly that: a road, a pin, threads, and the three answers.

- **Could be confused with [The hope to come](../the-hope-to-come-directions/)** ("What was lost, what Revelation answers": rows from the first pages to Revelation). That page pairs a loss with its answer in two end verses. This one walks *forward* through eight points in the story, and its subject is the reader's position: the answer changes as the pin moves, and promises that are kept later are shown as *still waiting* at earlier stations.
- **Could be confused with [Covenants and promises](../covenants-and-promises-directions/)** (a whole-Bible line with arcs to later verses). That page measures where each covenant is quoted later, by verse count. This one has no verse axis at all: the axis is the story's eight movements, and each mark is a state (promised, kept in part, kept, lost from view), not a quotation.
- **The same category** ([Genesis 1–11](../creation-to-babel-directions/), [Reading Daniel and Revelation](../reading-daniel-and-revelation-directions/), [The shape of Scripture](../the-shape-of-scripture-directions/)) uses chapter blocks with arcs, a scroll of seals, and passages set in their literary forms. None has a pin, stations or threads.
- The parts are not the model's dotted road nor the Kingdom page's margin notes: each part opens with its station (number, name, section, a rule in its colour). Where the study gives a Jewish reading and then a Christian one, the two stand side by side as "Two ways of hearing it". Each part ends with where the map stands at that point and signposts to the studies that read it closely.

## What the checks prove, and what they do not

- **By script on every build:** every non-blank line of content.md is placed on the page; every block quotation is word for word the Berean at its reference; every reference exists, with no gap in a range; a quotation followed by its own reference is in that reference; any other quotation is in a verse its paragraph cites, its part's main readings, or the passage printed just above it, or is on a named list in study_lists.py with its reason. "In outline" readings name chapters the Berean has. The three confession phrases (articles III, IV, XI) are word for word in that article of the Baptist Faith and Message 2000 held on this PC ([baptist-know-the-truth-catechism.json](../../content/questions/sources/extracted/baptist-know-the-truth-catechism.json)), and the paragraph names that article or "the confession". A note labelled "In Jewish tradition" must say "Jewish"; "Where Baptists differ" must say "Baptists". An unknown note marker, an unclosed `[check:` note, an italic name that is neither a known study nor a known book title, or a link off the site stops the build.
- **The map, by script:** every mark's words are in its verse; every mark's verse is in a chapter the study itself points to; every mark carries words of the study's own (checked word for word in content.md) that say what the mark claims, so the map cannot say a promise was kept where the study does not; marks run forward, one to a movement; the land thread ends where the study stops following it (movement 05), and the page says "the study follows it no further" rather than calling it kept or waiting. Marks the study gives as readings (Genesis 3:15 and Galatians 4:4 for the first promise, John 1:14, Luke 2:32) are flagged and labelled "a reading" everywhere they appear. Each station's colour is checked: the part has a main reading in that section's books. The station names must be the concepts line; the three questions must be in the frame and the Way; "eight movements" and "sixty-six books" must still be in the text.
- **Refused on purpose:** 23 wrong inputs, tried in memory without writing data.js, were all refused, each with its own message: a changed word in a block quotation; a block quotation under the wrong reference; a changed word in a quoted phrase; a quotation cited to the wrong verse; a reference that does not exist; a link off the site; a link that only looks local; a line the page cannot place; an unknown note marker; an unclosed check note; a Jewish-tradition label on a note that is not; a confession phrase under the wrong article; a changed word in a confession phrase; an unknown study name; a part removed; a thread phrase not in its verse; a "kept" mark with no warrant in the study; a mark on a chapter the study never points to; marks out of order; a station coloured by a section none of its readings is in; a station name not in the concepts line; a question the frame no longer asks; a study link to a page that does not exist.
- **Not checked by script:** the readings in the outside sources the study names (Targums, Irenaeus, the Mishnah, the Amidah, The Drama of Scripture, the Second London Confession, Kingdom through Covenant); none is held on this PC. The study marks five of them for checking; the page lists them under "Notes still to be checked". Whether a thread's state ("kept in part" for Ruth, for instance) is the best word is a reading aid chosen by this page from the study's own sentence, which is shown beside it; the script proves the sentence is the study's, not that the label is the best one.
- **By browser against the always-on preview** (Playwright, 2026-11-07): 1440, 390 and 320 wide, light and dark. At 390 and 320 the page does not scroll sideways; the only elements past the right edge are inside the shared site header's own sideways-scrolling menu, as on every mock-up. No console errors or warnings at any width. A verse link opens its verses in a sheet and puts `?ref=` in the address; Escape closes it, removes it from the address and returns focus to the link. On the map, arrow keys, Home and End move the pin and update `?at=`; pointing at or focusing a mark writes its line once into a polite live region. `?at=05` opens with the pin at the exile. The strip of stations stays above the reading on wide, tall screens, and moves its "You are here" as the reader scrolls; on phones it, the six threads and the four-part figure are left out and the stations wrap into two rows of four, with every mark still listed in words. Screenshots were looked at by eye.
- **Not tested:** 200% zoom, a screen reader, a real phone. Colour contrast was not measured.

## Findings for the editor

- content.md line 25 quotes Genesis 1:26–27 as "in His image"; the Berean has "in His own image" (1:27). The build lists it in `LOOSE_QUOTES` (its words must still be in order in that verse) rather than changing the study's words. Restoring "own" would let it be removed.
- The Reflect question in part 04 ("what is right in your own eyes") turns Judges 21:25 ("what was right in his own eyes") to the reader. It is listed as the study's own words, with that reason.
- Eight words the study defines in quotation marks ("The fall", "Sin", "covenant", "Torah", "judge", "witness", "first gospel", "going out") and the hook's "obey Me and I will save you" (what God does *not* say) are on the named list of the study's own terms.
- No `[check: …]` note in content.md is a link note, so content.md was not changed.
- Two named studies are not linked because they have no mock-up on this PC yet: *The Ten Commandments* and *Grief and dying* (the second is being designed beside this one). The run's last step adds their links; `--check` will then report "study links changed … rebuild needed".
