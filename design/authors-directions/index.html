Authors · four directions

Four ways to show the preachers and writers in the library, all built on the library's own catalogue.

  • A · LifelinesFive centuries of lives on one timeline: drag through the years, compare two lives, follow who taught whom.
  • B · Their worldThe map is the page: lives play out across Europe and America, cities gather their people, missionaries cross the world.
  • C · The preached BibleEvery chapter of Scripture lit by the sermons preached on it; pick a preacher to repaint the Bible.
  • D · RearrangeOne set of people that rearranges itself: by century, by tradition, by what they left, by who knew whom.
