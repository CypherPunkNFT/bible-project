Study 06, a Scripture & Theology study in Living the Christian Life. Its one habit: **when you hear a claim, put three questions to it, in order: what does it say about Jesus; is it so, in the passage read whole; and, turned on yourself, what is driving me as I judge it?** The three are the study's own frame (the letters test the message, test it against Scripture, and test the person doing the testing), each anchored in a verse: 1 John 4:2 with Galatians 1:8, Acts 17:11, and James 3:14–17. The page draws them once as a claim's line through three stations, the third bending back toward the reader, and then a rail beside every part shows, with three dots, which question that part trains.

## What is built

- **Opening.** The standard heading: trail, *Test · Search · Weigh* in gold capitals, *Learning* in ink with *to discern* in gold italic, the lead, Acts 17:11 in full. Beside it, in a faint circle and dotted orbit, an open book on a reading stand with a small lamp, and three lines of sound arriving from the left; the lines keep flowing and the flame keeps breathing. Motto: *Hear the claim. / Open the book.* No person is drawn. Four counts with what each means (5 passages walked, 15 verses printed, 7 qualities of wisdom from above, 5 questions to listen for) and links to every part.
- **00 One habit, three questions.** The frame paragraph, the claim's line (point at a station to read its verses on the line below; choose a number to go to its part), the note that part 05 asks the first and third questions together, and the selection note.
- **01–06**, each with its rail (number, three-dot mark, the question it trains, a small drawing) and one figure from its own passage: 01 John's test as two clauses with verse 4 under it; 02 "we", "an angel from heaven" and "anyone" above one fixed line, the gospel already received; 03 Acts 17 widened in three steps (the verse, its paragraph 17:10–12, the account in two towns 17:1–15), with "eager", "careful" and "the result" marked in the Berean words; 04 two wisdoms, James's words from below beside the seven qualities from above in his order, each quality found in the verse when pointed at; 05 Peter's five marks beside 2 Peter 2:1–3, each lighting its words in the passage; 06 the five questions to listen for, each linked back to the part it comes from.
- **Questions readers ask**, four, opening in place between rules. **Close**: the habit, the three questions again, the close and invitation, the reader's question, "Read the passage again before you answer", 1 John 4:1–6 whole, the sources, a list of every outside quotation with whether it was checked, a list of every point still marked "to confirm", and the way back to Living the Christian Life.
- **Every reference** opens its verses in a sheet that slides in from the right, in the Berean, with the verse in the address (`?ref=`).

Colour: Prophets blue, because the study is about telling true prophets from false (1 John 4:1, 2 Peter 2:1, Matthew 7:15). The gold accent marks the concepts, the title's turn and the care notes.

## How it is built

[build-data.py](build-data.py) reads the study's content.md and [study_lists.py](study_lists.py) (this page's own lists), checks them against the site's Berean through [scripture_text.py](scripture_text.py) (a copy of The good news mock-up's reader), checks the drawings through [drawn.py](drawn.py), and writes [data.js](data.js). Run from the Website folder: `python design/learning-to-discern-directions/build-data.py`. `--check` runs every check and fails if data.js is stale, writing nothing; `--selftest` feeds wrong inputs in memory. Exit 1 on a failed check (data.js unchanged), exit 2 if a file cannot be read or written. The page is [index.html](index.html), [js/ltd.js](js/ltd.js), [js/art.js](js/art.js) and [css/ltd.css](css/ltd.css), with the site frame borrowed from the Authors mock-ups. All text goes into the page as text, never as markup; the only markup strings are the fixed drawings. Nothing under `src/` was touched, and content.md was not changed (it has no "[check: …]" note that is a link on this site).

## What the checks prove, and what they do not

- **Recorded run, 2026-11-07:** `build-data.py` then `--check`: "all checks pass; data.js is current (9 points in the text still marked to check before publishing)", exit 0. `--selftest`: "19 of 19 wrong inputs refused as expected; nothing written", exit 0.
- **By script on every build:** every non-blank line of content.md is placed on the page or the build stops; every block quotation is word for word the Berean at its reference; every reference exists, with no gap in a range; a quotation with its own reference beside it is in that verse; any other quotation is in a verse its paragraph cites, its part's main readings or the passage printed just above; a quotation under three words must be pinned to its verse; a line with an odd number of quotation marks is refused; links go only to this site; a named study links only to a mock-up whose index.html exists. Every phrase in the drawings is in its verse: the three stations' verses, the two clauses of 1 John 4:2–3, the messengers and the fixed line of Galatians 1:8–9, the marked words of Acts 17:11–12, James's seven qualities in his order, Peter's five marks. Each wider view of Acts 17 holds the one before it. Each station's question is in content.md word for word. Part 05's five marks and part 06's five questions match the study's own lists, and each question sent back to a part cites a verse inside that part's main readings.
- **Refused on purpose (`--selftest`), 19 wrong inputs, each for the expected reason:** a changed word in a block quotation; a block quotation under the wrong reference; a changed word in a quoted phrase; a quote cited to the wrong verse; a reference that does not exist; a link that leaves the site; a line the page cannot place; an unbalanced quotation mark; an outside quotation edited away from its named wording; a renamed mark in part 05; a short quote with no verse; and, patched in study_lists: a station verse phrase not in its verse; a station question the study does not ask; a clause's verdict moved to the wrong verse; a wider view that does not hold the verse; James's qualities out of order; a listening question sent to the wrong part; an outside quotation looked for in the wrong held copy; a held source outside the allowed folders.
- **Outside quotations, 15.** Three were checked by script, against the site's own [DOCTRINAL-FOUNDATION.md](../../../DOCTRINAL-FOUNDATION.md), which quotes the Baptist Faith and Message 2000 (articles III, V and XIII); that proves agreement with the site's copy, not with the published confession. The other twelve have no copy on this PC: three more confession phrases (article II), the Latter-day Saints' Articles of Faith 1 and 8, Doctrine and Covenants 130:22, the "Godhead" entry title and Lorenzo Snow's saying, the New World Translation at John 1:1, "God's active force", the Seventh-day Adventist Fundamental Belief 2, and the Nicene Creed phrase. They are listed on the page as "not held here; compare by eye".
- **The Nicene Creed wording differs from the site's own copy.** The study quotes the creed as calling Christ "true God from true God" (a modern English wording). The site's creeds collection ([nicene-creed-381.b1.json](../../data/creeds/doc/nicene-creed-381.b1.json)) holds the older wording, "very God of very God". The meaning is the same, but the words cannot be checked against what is held. Needs the owner's word: keep the modern wording and name its translation, or quote the held wording.
- **Nine points in the text are still marked to check before publishing:** the Latter-day Saints' "Godhead" wording, Lorenzo Snow's saying, the Jehovah's Witnesses' wording on Jesus and Michael, "God's active force", and the five crisis lines (988, Línea de la Vida, 024, other Spanish-speaking countries, CVV 188). The page shows each as a small "to confirm" mark where it stands and lists them all at the close; none is hidden.
- **By browser against the always-on preview, 2026-11-07:** 1440, 390 and 320 wide, light and dark: the page is never wider than the window (scroll width equal to window width at all six), no console errors or warnings. A verse link opens its sheet from the right and puts `?ref=GAL.1.8` in the address; Escape closes it, clears the address and returns focus to the link. A station takes keyboard focus and shows its verses; the Acts steps are buttons with `aria-pressed`, and a focused button draws no outline. Screenshots were looked at: the hero, the claim's line, each part's figure at 1440 light, the hero and the claim's line at 390, part 05 at 320, the hero and the sheet in dark.
- **Not tested:** 200% zoom, a screen reader, a real phone, colour contrast by measurement, and the reduced-motion version in a browser (the stylesheet stops every animation, including the sheet's slide, under `prefers-reduced-motion`, but this was not watched). A verse check proves the words are in the verse, not that the reading of them is right.

## Not to be confused with

It uses Epistles gold, boxed figure cards, a pill row of three reading modes and six place-cards. This page is blue, has no cards or pills, and is shaped by one ruled line of three questions and a rail beside every part.
- **[Sexuality, singleness and divorce](../marriage-sexuality-and-singleness-directions/)**, also in this category, whose code this page's build follows. Its look is a seven-turn conversation over a ground line and a three-station rule repeated inside each part, in History orange. Here the three stations appear once, as a claim's path that turns back, and each part carries only a three-dot mark in a side rail; the part figures (two clauses, a fixed line, a widening passage, two wisdoms, lit marks) are all different.
