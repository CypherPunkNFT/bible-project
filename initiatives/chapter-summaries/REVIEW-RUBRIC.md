# Chapter-summary review rubric

Used for every draft in the 2026-10-13 pilot, whoever wrote it. The reviewer sees the BSB text and drafts labelled A/B/C, never the writer's identity.

## Issue categories

Each issue names the field, quotes the exact phrase, gives the verses, and takes one category:

| Category | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| factual error | The draft states something the text contradicts: wrong subject or object, wrong sequence, altered number or time, reversed negation, changed speaker |
| lost qualifier | A condition, qualifier or pronoun scope is dropped or changed ("after" → "at", "us" → "humanity", "if anyone slays Cain" dropped) |
| unsupported interpretation | An evaluation, motive, cause or theological conclusion the chapter does not state ("misplaced", "arrogantly", "forever") |
| imported detail | A fact, name or label from outside the supplied text |
| citation mismatch | A claim sits in an outline range, or a context note cites verses, that do not contain its support |
| material omission | A main movement, the conclusion or a defining qualification of the chapter is missing |
| genre | A proverb treated as a guarantee, a character's speech as endorsed teaching, a question turned into an assertion, lament flattened |
| style | Readability, length, padding, verse-by-verse cataloguing, awkward wording. Never material |

Severity: **material** (a reader would come away with something the text does not say, or miss something it plainly says) or **minor** (imprecise but not misleading). Style is always minor.

## Criteria (0 = fails, 1 = partly, 2 = meets)

1. Factual fidelity and correct agency
2. Conditions, qualifiers and pronoun scope preserved
3. Every substantive claim supported by the text
4. Context and outline citations accurate
5. Main movements and the closing point present
6. Genre-sensitive presentation
7. Readable without padding (target 80–140 summary words; shorter for brief chapters)
8. Invitation points to something observable in the chapter, not personal application

## Verdict

**No material issue found**: zero material issues. This records the result of one inspection, not approval and not proof of accuracy. Otherwise **revise**. Only the owner approves a summary.
