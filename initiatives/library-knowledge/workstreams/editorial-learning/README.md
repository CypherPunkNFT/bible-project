[Initiative](../../README.md) · [Tasks LK-301–302 and LK-401–403](../../TODO.md)

Turn reviewed evidence into answers, doctrinal comparisons, lessons, courses, practical-life collections and reusable editorial packets.

Inputs are W1 collections and W2 topic/passage coverage. Deliver attributed answer drafts, comparison records, learning sequences, source-linked reading assignments and publication-ready materials after review. Proposed pilot: 100 questions, with 20 held out before retrieval tuning.

Execution checklist: define reader question and audience; retrieve evidence; inspect surrounding context; draft with explicit attribution; verify quotations and qualifications; review theological/historical interpretation; reuse canonical evidence across formats.

Learning products can include introductory paths, advanced reading, discussion questions, revision cards and printable booklets. Editorial packets can support essays, narrated explainers, newsletters and presentations. These are derivative products, not additional acquired books.

Acceptance: published claims are inspectable, disagreement is represented fairly, the resource teaches something useful and multiple formats remain synchronized. Record model-assisted review separately from human review.

Catholic philosophy and documents remain available under the owner's direction. Identify particular doctrinal concerns with evidence; do not let a general caution substitute for describing what the source actually says.

Boundary: no automatic mass publication of model drafts, fabricated citations or public release of private source bodies. W7 owns the release assembly.
