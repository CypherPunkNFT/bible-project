[Initiative](../../README.md) · [Tasks LK-404–406](../../TODO.md)

Develop distinctive research resources from church history, archaeology, inscriptions, original languages, fathers, philosophy and textual relationships.

Deliver source-backed historical dossiers, place/period evidence bundles, contextual terminology, justified translation alignments, quotation/parallels registers and permitted research datasets. Begin with bounded high-quality source sets rather than claim uniform coverage across languages.

Execution checklist: distinguish composition/delivery/edition dates; preserve uncertain identities and chronology; identify source genre and perspective; use available language annotations; verify alignments and numbering; detect candidate parallels; separately review claims of dependence, influence or disagreement.

The same dossier can feed a scholar page, academic subject, Apologetics case, timeline and atlas layer. Preserve the distinct purposes of theological teaching, historical research and argument assessment under existing plans.

Acceptance: every historical or linguistic claim has suitable source support, dates and uncertainty survive presentation, and unsupported connections remain candidates. Similar wording is not proof of borrowing; a Strong's identifier alone is not a contextual sense analysis.

Boundary: no silent automatic translations presented as original texts, invented linguistic annotation, or new acquisition campaigns. Feed genuine gaps to the existing backlog through W7.
