## Recommended inspection sequence

1. Read Hodge’s opening three complete question-and-answer units on 32. His third answer includes the communion explanation and a final breaking-of-bread item missing from the saved candidate span. This is an attributed historical treatment, not an independently validated answer for every tradition.
2. Read Henry’s complete section 2 on 1 and its edition notice. Compare the two authors’ identified explanations, while retaining the compiler’s role and the distinct editions.
3. If using Hodge beyond those opening units, inspect the whole local question and distinguish his reported opposing positions, his replies, embedded quotations and the separate closing church standards.
4. Keep a source-specific Reformed/Protestant label and the existing Catholic cautions. Hodge’s descriptions and criticism of Catholic/Lutheran positions are his historical account; a neutral comparison requires directly checked witnesses from those traditions.
