# P2 mapping and question design

12 October 2026. **25 topics: 21 scoped existing-ID matches; 4 unresolved mappings.** This maps planning subjects to website-topic IDs, not to vector rows or graph entities. Planning keys and the five display groups are deliberately noncanonical. All topic parents are null; related-topic links express association rather than an invented doctrinal hierarchy.

The 5,603-entry generated website topic index is the identity registry for this task. Its source taxonomy, Nave placement metadata, route contract and selected entry headers were checked. English enrichment rule labels belong to a separate namespace; six observed rule labels are not a universal topic ontology or a graph-readiness claim. Exact input and selected bundle hashes are recorded privately in input-versions.json.

## Unresolved mappings

- **Covenant:** The Covenant and Covenants divide theological and general-agreement material; no single accepted parent for this broader proposed scope is verified.
- **Suffering:** Suffering centres on suffering for/of Christ and vicarious suffering; Afflictions covers a different scope. A broad umbrella mapping remains unresolved.
- **Christ's person:** Jesus the Christ is a broad multi-part container; humanity/divinity/incarnation are narrower entries. No exact parent topic for this scope is verified.
- **Interpretation:** Existing Interpretation concerns dreams and foreign tongues, not a verified general hermeneutics topic; title equality is insufficient.

## Scope choices

Justification maps to `justification-before-god`; the broader `justification` record stays distinct. The Supper maps to `communion-of-the-lord-s-supper`, with the separate Eucharist entry retained as a candidate parallel. Providence maps to `providence-of-god`, not a merge of two existing entries. Christian hope narrows the `hope` entry. Faith, repentance, sanctification and church retain their IDs with explicit sense exclusions. These are reviewed planning matches, not automatic title matches or production changes.

## Counts and split

Four independently worded questions per topic: concept, textual reading, attributed comparison and source criticism. Each has an intent, evidence requirements, difficulty and no answer/relevance judgment. Total: 50 introductory and 50 advanced; 25 questions of each type. Development: 80 (40/40 difficulty; 20 of each type). Reserved: 20 (10/10; 5 of each type).

Reservation uses the documented pilot order, excludes every fifth topic, and fixes five source-criticism selections to cover the hard cases. The remaining eligible topics rotate across concept, textual reading and comparison. It covers 20 topics; the Supper, perseverance, Christ's person, worship and Christian hope have development coverage only in this first split. This limitation is explicit, not evidence that those topics need no evaluation. Add a separate fresh evaluation wave later if 25-topic coverage is required.

## Handling evaluation honestly

Reserved wording is confined to the private evaluation directory and its private authoring tool, never this overview or fixtures. Separation is by workflow, not ACL. Both sets were authored by this assistant; no independent human or blind evaluation is claimed. Future tuners must exclude the evaluation directory and freeze configuration before access. The older LK-106 question texts were not opened. Validation may inspect structural fields without printing reserved wording; that does not constitute relevance evaluation.

No corpus or services were consulted. Source availability, answer quality, multilingual sufficiency and relevance remain unassessed for all 100 questions. Thin language coverage, attribution, ambiguous terms, missing text and independent-work diversity are explicit future evaluation cases. Final mining still waits for intake, vector parity and graph gates. Canonical mappings must be rechecked if the registry changes.
