# Archaeology scholar catalogue: source notes

Updated 2026-10-12. The Scholars page lists **selected publications**, not a complete bibliography or downloadable holdings. A null `site` value means no on-site copy or live use has been verified. Work titles and dates refer to the cited edition or excavation report. Place markers indicate a significant working site, not a person's birthplace or permanent residence. Archaeological interpretations are described as interpretations, especially at Jericho, Qumran, and in the chronology of ancient Israel.

The selection includes scholars who reach different conclusions from the same material. The catalogue records their contribution and points readers to their work; it does not treat an excavation, an identification, or an interpretation as blanket proof of a biblical narrative.
