 "title": "Judgment in the Valley of Jehoshaphat",
 "summary": "The LORD says that when He restores Judah and Jerusalem, He will gather all the nations to the Valley of Jehoshaphat and judge them for scattering His people, dividing His land, and selling a boy and a girl for a prostitute and wine. He accuses Tyre, Sidon and Philistia of taking His silver, gold and treasures and selling the people of Judah and Jerusalem to the Greeks, and warns that He will return their recompense on their heads. The nations are summoned to prepare for war, beating plowshares into swords, and to come to the valley where the LORD will sit to judge them, for their wickedness is great. The Day of the LORD is near in the valley of decision; sun, moon and stars darken, and the LORD roars from Zion, yet He will be a refuge for His people. In that day the land will flow with wine, milk and water, Egypt and Edom will become desolate for their violence against Judah, and Judah and Jerusalem will be inhabited forever, for the LORD dwells in Zion."
