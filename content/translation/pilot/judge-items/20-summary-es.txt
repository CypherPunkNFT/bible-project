 "summary": "The word of the LORD came to Jonah a second time, telling him to go to the great city of Nineveh, and this time he went. The Ninevites believed God, proclaimed a fast and put on sackcloth, from the greatest to the least. The king left his throne for sackcloth and ashes and decreed with his nobles that neither man nor beast eat or drink, that all wear sackcloth, call out earnestly to God and turn from evil and violence, in case God might relent so that they would not perish.
