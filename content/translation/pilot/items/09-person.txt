These men were part of an elite group of soldiers who served David faithfully and were renowned for their military prowess (2Sa.23.8-39; 1Ch.11.10-47). \nThe mention of the Ithrites in these passages highlights the importance of family and clan connections in Israelite society and the role that certain groups played in the military and political life of the nation.
