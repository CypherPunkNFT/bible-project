{
 "title": "Sins That Separate and the LORD Who Redeems",
 "summary": "The chapter opens by insisting that the LORD's arm is not too short to save; rather, the people's iniquities have built barriers between them and their God, so that He does not hear. Their hands are stained with blood, their lips speak lies, and they do not know the way of peace. A 'we' voice then confesses that justice is far from them, that they grope like the blind, and that their transgressions testify against them. The LORD saw that there was no justice and no one to intercede, so His own arm brought salvation. Clothed in righteousness, salvation and vengeance, He will repay His enemies according to their deeds, and His name will be feared from west to east. The LORD declares that the Redeemer will come to Zion, to those in Jacob who turn from transgression, and that His Spirit and words will not depart from them or their descendants."
