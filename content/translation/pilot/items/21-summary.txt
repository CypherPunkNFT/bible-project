 "title": "Jacob Blesses Ephraim and Manasseh",
 "summary": "When Jacob falls ill, Joseph comes with his sons Manasseh and Ephraim, and Jacob recalls God Almighty's blessing at Luz and claims the two boys as his own, just as Reuben and Simeon are his. Nearly blind with age, Israel kisses and embraces the boys, saying he never expected to see Joseph's face again, but God has let him see his children as well. Joseph brings them so that Manasseh, the firstborn, is toward Israel's right hand, but Israel crosses his hands, placing his right on Ephraim, and asks the God of his fathers to bless the boys. When Joseph objects, Israel refuses, saying Manasseh too will be great but his younger brother will be greater, and he puts Ephraim before Manasseh. Saying he is about to die, Israel tells Joseph that God will bring him back to the land of his fathers, and gives him a ridge of land taken from the Amorites."
