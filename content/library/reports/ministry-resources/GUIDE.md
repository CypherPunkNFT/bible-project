# Ministry reading guide

Start with a responsibility or audience below. Each link identifies the source and the exact span assessed. These are suggested entry points, not complete-book reviews. For every claim and limitation use [treatments.json](treatments.json); for edition and rights boundaries use [the report](REPORT.md).

Theological principle means a claim grounded in Scripture or doctrine. Historical practice means a dated description. Practical advice means guidance for carrying out ministry; some sources treat their practical directions as duties. A work can contain all three.

## By responsibility

### Sermon Preparation

- [Lectures to My Students: First Series](https://archive.org/details/lecturestomystud00spurrich) — Introduction, printed pp. 5–9; PDF pp. 11–15. **historical practice**. Spurgeon locates these addresses in Friday meetings at his college and describes its entry requirements and support of students. A particular nineteenth-century training institution. Its admissions and funding practices are not universal church commands.
- [Sermons—Their Matter](https://archive.org/details/lecturestomystud00spurrich) — Lecture V, printed pp. 112–113; PDF pp. 118–119. **theological principle**. Preaching needs substantive doctrinal content; presentation cannot compensate for an empty message. Apply to teaching preparation without making every small-group leader an ordained preacher.
- [On Spiritualizing](https://archive.org/details/lecturestomystud00spurrich) — Lecture VII, printed p. 156; PDF p. 162. **practical advice**. Spurgeon permits some spiritualizing while opening with a warning to use it sparingly. Do not present this as a blanket rejection of allegory or a fully reviewed hermeneutical method. Compare the passage’s actual argument before adopting an illustration.
- [On the Voice](https://archive.org/details/lecturestomystud00spurrich) — Lecture VIII, printed p. 178; PDF p. 184. **practical advice**. Delivery matters, but vocal ability without meaningful content does not supply a sermon. Historical speaking advice requires adaptation to microphones and current settings; the opening does not validate every later vocal exercise.
- [The Faculty of Impromptu Speech](https://archive.org/details/lecturestomystud00spurrich) — Lecture X, printed p. 227; PDF p. 233. **practical advice**. Develop the capacity to speak when unexpectedly called upon. The opening explicitly distinguishes this ability from the debate about written sermons and from routine preaching without preparation.
- [To Workers with Slender Apparatus](https://archive.org/details/lecturestomystud00spurrich) — Lecture XIII, printed p. 282; PDF p. 288. **practical advice**. Churches should consider the preacher’s need for books and sustained intellectual nourishment. A ministry support recommendation, not a mandatory library size or spending formula.
- [Use and Application](https://banneroftruth.org/uk/resources/book-excerpts/2019/how-to-use-and-apply-doctrines/) — The Art of Prophesying, chapter 7; 2019 publisher excerpt, application paragraphs. **practical advice**. Application attends to hearers and circumstances; Perkins distinguishes the law’s diagnosis from the gospel’s promise and the Spirit’s enabling work. Selected modernized excerpt. This is not a transcription of the 1592 edition or a summary of the entire book.
- [How is the word of God to be preached by those that are called thereunto?](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NonlinearFruit/Creeds.json/2ae21a4c5387ecc91f474c9d3d67c826d2a6b9d5/creeds/westminster_larger_catechism.json) — WLC Q159, answer; acquired Creeds.json /Data/158/Answer. **theological principle**. The answer calls for diligent, plain, faithful preaching, adapted to hearers, with love and a purpose of conversion and edification. Q158 separately concerns approved and called preachers; applying teaching wisdom in a group does not confer that office.

### Worship

- [Of the Sacraments](https://www.ccel.org/ccel/calvin/institutes.vi.xv.html) — Institutes IV.14.1–4; Beveridge translation. **theological principle**. A sacramental sign confirms God’s promise and assists weak faith; the accompanying word matters to its meaning. Later editorial footnotes in the digital edition are not Calvin’s own statements.
- [The Lord’s Supper](https://www.ccel.org/ccel/calvin/institutes.vi.xviii.html) — Institutes IV.17.1–4; Beveridge translation. **theological principle**. The Supper assures believers of nourishment in Christ and directs them to the benefit of his once-offered sacrifice. Only these opening sections assessed. They do not establish how often any historical congregation actually celebrated communion.
- [A habit of prayer](https://www.monergism.com/duties-parents) — Duties of Parents, counsel VI. **practical advice**. Parents are urged to cultivate a habit of prayer in their children. Selected counsel in an unidentified printed witness; no blanket endorsement of all historical disciplinary advice elsewhere in the work.
- [Of Religious Worship and the Sabbath Day](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NonlinearFruit/Creeds.json/2ae21a4c5387ecc91f474c9d3d67c826d2a6b9d5/creeds/westminster_confession_of_faith.json) — WCF 21.1, 21.5–6; acquired Creeds.json /Data/20/Sections. **theological principle**. The confession grounds worship in God’s revealed will and includes Scripture, preaching, hearing, praise and sacraments, with family, private and public worship. This is a confessional prescription, not evidence that every church historically practiced it identically.
- [What is required of those that hear the word preached?](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NonlinearFruit/Creeds.json/2ae21a4c5387ecc91f474c9d3d67c826d2a6b9d5/creeds/westminster_larger_catechism.json) — WLC Q160, answer; acquired Creeds.json /Data/159/Answer. **practical advice**. Hearers prepare, pray, test teaching by Scripture, meditate, discuss and practice it. Practical direction presented by this standard as a duty; practical-advice does not mean the source treats it as optional.
- [Of the Lord's Supper](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NonlinearFruit/Creeds.json/2ae21a4c5387ecc91f474c9d3d67c826d2a6b9d5/creeds/westminster_confession_of_faith.json) — WCF 29.7; /Data/28/Sections/6. **theological principle**. Worthy receivers spiritually receive and feed upon Christ by faith. The same paragraph denies a corporeal presence in the elements; retain both parts.
- [Of the Lord's Supper](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NonlinearFruit/Creeds.json/2ae21a4c5387ecc91f474c9d3d67c826d2a6b9d5/creeds/london_baptist_1689.json) — Second London 30.7; /Data/29/Sections/6. **theological principle**. Second London also affirms real spiritual feeding on Christ by faith. Do not recast this Baptist statement as a merely mental memorial to manufacture a contrast.

### Evangelism

- [Continuing obligation of the commission](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/11449/pg11449-images.html) — Enquiry, section I, argument concerning the continuing commission. **theological principle**. Carey argues that the commission to teach all nations continues rather than expiring with the apostles. Preserve the argument for an obligation separately from his proposed organizational means.
- [Duty of Christians and means to be used](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/11449/pg11449-images.html) — Enquiry, section V, prayer, cooperation and financial support. **practical advice**. Prayer, cooperative effort and material support are proposed as means for missionary work. An eighteenth-century proposal; contemporary strategy needs contextual knowledge and local Christian participation.
- [The Soul-Winner](https://www.spurgeon.org/sermons/the-soul-winner) — Sermon 1292, opening and holy-life discussion; Proverbs 11:30. **theological principle**. Spurgeon connects seeking others’ salvation with a holy life rather than mere argumentative skill. Selected sermon reading; do not confuse this numbered sermon with the later collected book The Soul Winner.

## Comparisons to keep explicit

- **Who should receive baptism, and by what mode?** Read both numbered statements in their own confessional setting; do not blend the positions. See [WCF 28.3–4; /Data/27/Sections/2–3](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NonlinearFruit/Creeds.json/2ae21a4c5387ecc91f474c9d3d67c826d2a6b9d5/creeds/westminster_confession_of_faith.json), [Second London 29.2, 29.4; /Data/28/Sections/1 and 3](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NonlinearFruit/Creeds.json/2ae21a4c5387ecc91f474c9d3d67c826d2a6b9d5/creeds/london_baptist_1689.json).
- **Do these Presbyterian and Baptist standards affirm spiritual feeding on Christ?** Agreement here does not erase their differences on baptism or establish identical wording throughout. See [WCF 29.7; /Data/28/Sections/6](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NonlinearFruit/Creeds.json/2ae21a4c5387ecc91f474c9d3d67c826d2a6b9d5/creeds/westminster_confession_of_faith.json), [Second London 30.7; /Data/29/Sections/6](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NonlinearFruit/Creeds.json/2ae21a4c5387ecc91f474c9d3d67c826d2a6b9d5/creeds/london_baptist_1689.json).
- **What authority does an interchurch assembly possess?** Compare authority to determine complaints with advice lacking jurisdiction; keep the historic WCF edition visible. See [Historic WCF 31.2–4; /Data/30/Sections/1–3](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NonlinearFruit/Creeds.json/2ae21a4c5387ecc91f474c9d3d67c826d2a6b9d5/creeds/westminster_confession_of_faith.json), [Second London 26.7–8, 26.15; /Data/25/Sections/6–7 and 14](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NonlinearFruit/Creeds.json/2ae21a4c5387ecc91f474c9d3d67c826d2a6b9d5/creeds/london_baptist_1689.json).
- **Is a biblical duty identical with a particular group program?** The same sermon contains an argued duty, a dated implementation and a proposed practice; those are separate claims. See [Sections on Hebrews 3:12–13 and 10:23–25: mutual encouragement](https://www.desiringgod.org/messages/small-group-life-in-the-power-of-gods-promises), [Connection Between Hebrews’ Motivation and Small Groups; September 14, 1997](https://www.desiringgod.org/messages/small-group-life-in-the-power-of-gods-promises), [Dynamics of Small Group Ministry According to Hebrews](https://www.desiringgod.org/messages/small-group-life-in-the-power-of-gods-promises).
