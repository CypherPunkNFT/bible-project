# Church, preaching and missions — L12

Collected and checked 2026-10-05. This first collection covers all eight requested responsibilities and offers entry points for pastors, elders, small-group leaders, families and individual Christians. It is a selected library, not an exhaustive bibliography or a complete review of every book.

Start with the [reading guide](GUIDE.md). The [inventory](inventory.json) preserves editions and component relationships; [scoped assessments](treatments.json), [responsibility map](topic-map.json), [audience map](audience-map.json) and [comparisons](comparisons.json) separate source arguments from editorial navigation.

## How readers can use it

| Responsibility | Useful starting material | Evidence boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Sermon preparation | Spurgeon’s lecture inventory, Perkins’s application excerpt, WLC 158–159 | Distinguish doctrinal substance and pastoral calling from delivery techniques. Spurgeon’s opening on spiritualizing permits some use; it is not a blanket prohibition. |
| Pastoral ministry | Spurgeon on self-watch, discouragement and books; Calvin IV.3; Piper’s pastoral book | Source-read openings supply scoped insights. Piper’s book remains a bibliographic lead. Historical care advice is not a complete contemporary ministry manual. |
| Worship | WCF 21; Second London 22; Owen’s catechism lead | Keep the standards’ prescriptions separate from descriptions of what churches historically did. |
| Sacraments | Calvin IV.14 and IV.17; WCF 27–29; Second London 28–30 | Baptism subjects and mode disagree. Both confessions affirm spiritual feeding on Christ; no artificial Presbyterian-versus-Baptist contrast on that point. |
| Church government | WCF 30–31; Second London 26; Calvin IV.3; Owen lead | Interchurch authoritative determinations and non-jurisdictional advice remain distinct positions. Historic WCF magistrate wording is preserved. |
| Discipleship | Piper’s small-group sermon; Ryle’s selected counsels; WLC 160; Whitefield lead | The duty of mutual encouragement, Bethlehem’s 1997 setting, and a group method are separately classified. |
| Evangelism | Spurgeon 1292 and 2423; Packer’s four-chapter inventory | One Spurgeon sermon has a scoped body assessment; the other sermon and Packer chapters are further study leads. |
| Missions | Carey’s five sections; Piper’s missions book; Zwemer’s contextual lectures | Continuing commission, proposed means and historic descriptions are distinct. Old demographic estimates do not describe today’s world. |

The audience recommendations are **entry points**, not an L14 curriculum or blanket endorsements. Pastors receive material on character and preparation; elders on authority and support; group leaders on mutual care and application; families on prayer, example and confessional differences; individual Christians on hearing, fellowship, the Supper, witness and missions. Small-group teaching wisdom does not itself confer the office of preacher.

## Rights and source decisions

The [Spurgeon witness](https://archive.org/details/lecturestomystud00spurrich) was acquired as an identified historical edition and classified public domain in the United States; worldwide clearance is not asserted. Public packaging and OCR review remain separate from acquisition.

[Banner of Truth’s edition](https://banneroftruth.org/us/store/church-ministry/the-art-of-prophesying/?attribute_pa_binding=paperback) and [its application excerpt](https://banneroftruth.org/uk/resources/book-excerpts/2019/how-to-use-and-apply-doctrines/) supply bibliographic and selected-body evidence. Modernized language was not copied as a corpus. [IVP’s Packer record](https://ivpress.org/evangelism-and-the-sovereignty-of-god) supplies edition and contents metadata only.

[Desiring God’s permissions](https://www.desiringgod.org/permissions) and [Monergism’s permissions](https://www.monergism.com/monergism-copyright-permissions) do not supply blanket clearance to rehost these collections. The Piper and Ryle assets remain source links. Free availability is not treated as an open license.

[Project Gutenberg’s automation policy](https://www.gutenberg.org/policy/robot_access.html) distinguishes ordinary browsing from permitted harvest/mirror routes. Carey was linked and manually inspected; no ordinary-site bulk retrieval was attempted. The text and catalog display differing digital update dates, so the record preserves the release date and leaves revision reconciliation open. CCEL’s linked Owen works were not downloaded or assumed to be wholly reviewed.

Carey’s initial eligibility entry follows the broader Calvinist evangelical charter, supported by his [primary Enquiry](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/11449/pg11449-images.html) and [Southern Seminary’s historical discussion](https://www.sbts.edu/news/patterson-mohler-calvinism-shouldnt-divide-southern-baptists/). Selection of a work does not endorse every historical claim or practice. All other new teaching authors already had eligible registry entries.

They do not certify theological conclusions or substitute for human source review.
