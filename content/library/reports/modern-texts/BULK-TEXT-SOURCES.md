## Remaining authors and limits

- **Criswell:** [official sermon library](https://wacriswell.com/) is a credible HTML corpus. The existing first sitemap yields 2,000 URLs; later sitemap failures and the lack of a verified bulk-copy grant remain unresolved. No English full-book bundle was verified. Same-title sermon pages do not supply the published book edition.
- **Graham:** [official library downloads](https://billygrahamlibrary.org/free-downloads/free-downloads/) and BGEA text features are credible, but no complete openly downloadable book/transcript corpus was verified. [Open Library's author catalog](https://openlibrary.org/authors/OL29976A/Billy_Graham) helps locate editions; catalog export is metadata, not full books.
- **Packer, Ferguson, Murray, Lloyd-Jones:** Monergism supplies specific usable editions, not their complete copyrighted works. Publisher ebook collections and legitimate library lending establish that many texts already exist; neither is an automatic bulk export license.
- **Internet Archive/Open Library, OverDrive, Logos:** distinguish catalog metadata, borrowed editions, platform-bound purchases and openly downloadable full text. Do not describe all four as equivalent acquisition sources.

## Reuse and efficiency

[Monergism](https://www.monergism.com/monergism-copyright-permissions) permits its hosted files to be downloaded for study while restricting republication of its curated files on other apps/platforms. [Desiring God](https://www.desiringgod.org/permissions) and [Love Worth Finding](https://www.lwf.org/about-us/contact-us/copyright-information) have source-specific conditions. Keep private reading acquisition separate from public app hosting.

For each batch, enumerate once, retain a resumable manifest, prefer EPUB/HTML/XML or existing PDF text, skip verified local duplicates, and record genuine missing-text cases separately. No model calls are needed per file. Do not queue transcription merely because a preferred source is unavailable or a work is commercially licensed.
