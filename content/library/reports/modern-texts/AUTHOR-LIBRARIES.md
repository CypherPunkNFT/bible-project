# Author-by-author downloadable libraries

Checked October 5, 2026. Owner direction: find already readable text first, acquire permitted editions, and consider transcription only for an identified work genuinely unavailable as text. Existing EPUB, HTML, TXT and searchable PDF are suitable; scanned pages are not the starting point. Files acquired here are private reading copies, not a claim of permission to publish a public full-text library.

## Distinctions that affect acquisition

- [Monergism's permission](https://www.monergism.com/monergism-copyright-permissions) expressly covers downloading its hosted ebooks for personal study. It restricts reposting curated files on other apps/platforms. Its old 1,063-book ZIP announcement currently exposes no ZIP hyperlinks in the retrieved page, so do not claim that bundle was downloaded. The live author index provides working individual EPUB links.
- Open Library and OverDrive are valuable for locating already-existing ebook editions. A catalog's ebook count or Borrow button is not a freely exportable full-text library.
- A search result promising a free PDF is not enough: preserve the author, edition, official offer or source permission, original URL, hash, and actual downloaded-file result.
- No absence conclusion here means that no ebook exists anywhere. It means no permitted direct download was verified in this pass. Record the exact title and completed text-source searches before any future transcription decision.

## Files and repeatability

The resumable worker is `scripts/collect-ready-libraries.py`. Logs are `.local/library/run-modern-texts-2026-10-05/ready-libraries.stdout.log` and `ready-libraries-resume.stdout.log`; errors are recorded in the result ledger. Failed URLs are not retried on resume; the worker stops on throttling, authentication requirements or repeated access denials. Original EPUB/PDF files and provenance are under `BibleProject/sources/library/`; extracted reading text is under the existing `.local/library/.../text/` directory. Each record retains its actual author/group so it is not silently added to Piper or Spurgeon.
