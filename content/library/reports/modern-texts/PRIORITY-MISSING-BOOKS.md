Established 2026-10-05. **65 acquisition tasks: five works per author across the 13 recent-preacher authors discussed.** These are editorial priorities for theological breadth, sermon preparation, apologetics and pastoral usefulness, not an objective ranking of literary quality or sales. Multi-volume works count as one task; retain their volume relationships.

Checked 5,917 existing-file asset records across the canonical catalog and modern acquisition ledgers. All 65 targets lack an identified local complete book edition. Sermons bearing the same title, source links, previews and excerpts do not satisfy these tasks. [Audit data](priority-book-holdings-check.json).

## First five across the whole list

1. J. I. Packer ? Knowing God: broad theological and devotional foundation.
2. R. C. Sproul ? The Holiness of God: foundational doctrine with pastoral force.
3. John Murray ? Redemption Accomplished and Applied: concise account of salvation.
4. Martyn Lloyd-Jones ? Preaching and Preachers: directly serves the sermon/ministry priority.
5. D. A. Carson ? Exegetical Fallacies: strengthens interpretation across the entire library.

These first five are my recommended acquisition order, not a claim that other selected authors agree with every argument.
