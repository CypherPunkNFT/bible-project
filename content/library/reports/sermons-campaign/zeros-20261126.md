# Who still has no sermon on a passage, and why (2026-11-26)

After the historic-sermons second round ([results](historic-splitting-20261126/RESULTS-steps-6-13.md)), **29 of the 53 people** on the Preachers & authors page have no sermon whose own printed text gives a Bible passage (37 before). Each has a recorded reason; the same list with counts is [zeros-20261126.json](zeros-20261126.json).

**Blocked by source terms**
