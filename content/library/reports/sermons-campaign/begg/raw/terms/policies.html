Copyright Policy

Truth For Life distributes Alistair Begg’s teaching material. As a ministry, we desire to see the Word of God proclaimed through all means. Therefore, we are excited to partner with you to give you easy access to much of our content for your personal use and to share with your friends. We are able to make these resources available because of financial support from listeners. As you use and share our content, please abide by the following guidelines to help us ensure the quality of the materials being distributed.

ESV Bible

Scripture quotations are from the ESV® Bible (The Holy Bible, English Standard Version®), © 2001 by Crossway, a publishing ministry of Good News Publishers. Used by permission. All rights reserved. The ESV text may not be quoted in any publication made available to the public by a Creative Commons license. The ESV may not be translated in whole or in part into any other language.

Audio and Video Content (DVD’s, CD’s, MP3 CD’s, & Digital Downloads)

You may duplicate and distribute copies of Alistair’s sermons without prior permission as long as you:

  • Do not alter or edit the content in any way
  • Do not charge for the material (even to recover your fee)

  • Make a label or print on a CD:

    Copyright [message year] by Truth For Life. Used with Permission. https://www.truthforlife.org 1-888-588-7884

  • Do not include personal or organization contact information with the copy.
  • Do not reuse Truth For Life’s logo.
  • You are welcome to show DVDs or streaming video of Alistair preaching in group settings.
  • If you would like to include a link to an audio or video clip on your site, you may embed a link to the file on our site. You must include a link back to our site at www.truthforlife.org.

Your use of the Audio and Video Content constitutes to an agreement by you and Truth For Life to be bound by these policies. Your use of this Content is provided under an irrevocable, nonexclusive and royalty-free right to use such Content subject to these terms. Please be aware that while all of our Audio and Video Content is free to use for non-commercial purposes in accordance with these terms, depicted items in the Content, such as identifiable people, logos, brands, etc. (other than of Alistair Begg or Truth For Life) may require the consent of a third party or the license of these rights. We do not represent or warrant that such consents or licenses have been obtained and expressly disclaim any liability in this respect. You agree to indemnify and hold harmless us from and against any and all losses, expenses, damages and costs, including reasonable attorney fees, resulting from your use of such Content and any violation of these terms.

Print Content

To use an excerpt from one of Alistair’s books or articles, please identify the publisher and contact that company directly. Truth For Life can only grant permission for re-use on items that we have published.

Unfortunately, we cannot grant permission to reprint the Morning & Evening devotional. For permission to reuse this content, please contact Crossway Books directly.

Web Content

You may post written content from www.truthforlife.org on your website as long as you:

  • Do not edit the content in any way
  • Include a link to the original article and our homepage (www.truthforlife.org)
  • Provide the appropriate credit for the content: “Copyright Truth For Life. Used with Permission. www.truthforlife.org.”

Provided that your use of the material falls under the guidelines of fair use, you are welcome to quote from Alistair’s sermons. If the quotation is represented in a published transcript, please use that as the basis for the quotation. Otherwise, you may use the audio, transcribing as reasonably as possible. In either case, please ensure that the quotation is an exact representation of the source, from the wording itself down to the capitalization, punctuation, etc., for written content. Please also provide an appropriate citation of the source in accordance with the style manual with which you are working. For example, a Chicago-style footnote for a sermon by Alistair would read as follows, per The Chicago Manual of Style, 14.217: Alistair Begg, “Getting Our Spiritual Bearings” (sermon, July 4, 2021), https://www.truthforlife.org/resources/sermon/getting-our-spiritual-bearings.

If you would like to post a web banner for Truth For Life on your site or blog, please visit our promotional resources page. We have a number of banners we have created for this purpose.

Truth For Life and Alistair Begg reserve all copyright protections under applicable law. We reserve the right to revoke or modify these permissions at any time.

If you would like to use content from Alistair Begg or Truth For Life in a manner that is not specified above, please contact us separately with the details of your request.

Guarantee & Returns

We offer a 100% satisfaction guarantee on all our ministry materials. If you’re not completely satisfied, you can return your purchase or resource request within 30 days of receipt. Returned items should be carefully packaged and include the original packing slip along with an explanation for the reason for the return. Truth For Life does not cover shipping costs for returns. In the event of an incorrectly delivered order, please call Truth For Life Customer Service at 1-888-588-7884.

Refund Policy

All refunds will be provided as a credit to the credit card used at the time of purchase within five (5) business days upon receipt of the returned merchandise.

Donor Privacy Policy

We never sell  or trade the names of any donors or listeners to other organizations, nor do we rent our mailing list to other organizations. No mailings will be sent on behalf of other organizations. All other donor requests related to mailing lists (either print or digital) will be fully honored.

Security

All information provided to Truth For Life is transmitted using SSL (Secure Socket Layer) encryption. SSL is a proven coding system that lets your browser automatically encrypt, or scramble, data before you send it to us. We are compliant with the strictest industry standards for credit card security and processing, and our team regularly evaluates our systems and processes for additional safeguards.

Unfortunately, however, no data transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot ensure or warrant the security of such information. To increase the security of your transactions, please protect your usernames and passwords, be sure to log-out of the site when completing your transaction, and use a secure Internet connection when completing a financial transaction.

Whistleblower Policy

Truth For Life is committed to operating in furtherance of its tax-exempt purposes and in compliance with all applicable laws, rules and regulations, including those concerning accounting and auditing, and prohibits fraudulent practices by any of its board members, officers, employees, or volunteers. This policy outlines a procedure for employees and volunteers to report actions that an employee or volunteer reasonably believes violates a law, or regulation or that constitutes fraudulent accounting or other practices. This policy applies to any matter which is related to Truth For Life's business and does not relate to private acts of an individual not connected to the business of Truth For Life.

If an employee or volunteer has a reasonable belief that an employee, volunteer or Truth For Life has engaged in any action that violates any applicable law, or regulation, including those concerning accounting and auditing, or constitutes a fraudulent practice, the employee or volunteer is expected to immediately report such information to Alistair Begg or Robert H. Butts. If the employee or volunteer does not feel comfortable reporting the information to either of the above, he or she is expected to report the information to any board member.

All reports will be followed up promptly, and an investigation conducted. In conducting its investigations, Truth For Life will strive to keep the identity of the complaining individual as confidential as possible, while conducting an adequate review and investigation.

Truth For Life will not retaliate against an employee or volunteer in the terms and conditions of employment because that employee or volunteer: (a) reports to a supervisor, to the executive director, the Board of Directors or to a federal, state or local agency what the employee believes in good faith to be a violation of the law; or (b) participates in good faith in any resulting investigation or proceeding, or (c) exercises his or her rights under any state or federal law(s) or regulation(s) to pursue a claim or take legal action to protect the employee's rights.

Truth For Life may take disciplinary action (up to and including termination) against an employee or volunteer who in management's assessment has engaged in retaliatory conduct in violation of this policy.

In addition, Truth For Life will not, with the intent to retaliate, take any action harmful to any employee or volunteer who has provided to law enforcement personnel or a court truthful information relating to the commission or possible commission by Truth For Life or any of its employees or volunteers of a violation of any applicable law or regulation.

Supervisors will be trained on this policy and Truth For Life's prohibition against retaliation in accordance with this policy.

Conflict of Interest Policy

Every member shall avoid conflicts of interest. Transactions with related parties may be undertaken only if all of the following are observed: 1) a material transaction is fully disclosed in the audited financial statements of the member; 2) the related party is excluded from the discussion and approval of such transaction; 3) a competitive bid or comparable valuation exists; and 4) the member's board has acted upon and demonstrated that the transaction is in the best interest of the member.
