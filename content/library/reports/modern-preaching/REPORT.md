# L04 — modern preaching source inventory

Verified 2026-10-05. This batch maps **five ministries to six eligible modern-era authors** and completes **two bounded biblical sermon inventories: 16 sermons, 16 archive editions, 20 link-only format records**. All 16 have a source-assigned main text, ordered series position, archive sermon date, verified recording duration and official destination. No sermon text or media files were acquired, transcribed, indexed or published by this batch.

The completed boundary is this source/access review and its two pilots. It is not a census of every recording by these preachers. [Machine-readable inventory](sermon-inventory.json), [ministry decisions and permission queue](ministry-inventory.json), [counts](summary.json) and [resumption checkpoint](checkpoint.json) accompany the canonical catalog records.

## Remaining work and quality boundaries

- No missing members against either bounded source inventory. Author-wide missing-sermon counts remain unknown.
- Publication and upload dates are unknown for all sixteen. Archive-assigned sermon dates are recorded as delivery witnesses with that qualification; no independent service-record collation is claimed.
- Source passage ranges span Ruth and 2 Peter. This is bibliographic coverage, not certification that every verse receives substantial exposition. No full recording or theology audit was performed.
- [Ferguson’s twelve-part Philippians course](https://learn.ligonier.org/series/my-joy-and-crown-pauls-letter-to-the-philippians) is an adjacent teaching resource, excluded from sermon totals. [Packer’s Ligonier archive](https://learn.ligonier.org/teachers/j-i-packer) mixes formats and speakers; a complete biblical sermon series was not established there.
- Carson and Begg remain provisional in the existing author registry. No new core-teaching records bypass that review.
- Every permission request is documented but **not sent**. The queue specifies retrieval route/export, rate limits, retention, metadata, transcription, full-text search, public use, attribution and withdrawal scope. Unknown terms remain unknown rather than being described as express prohibitions.

Rebuild the catalog offline with `python -X utf8 scripts/catalog-modern-preaching.py`; validate with `node scripts/validate-library.mjs`. The source-registration helper applies the recorded decisions without contacting ministries. These scripts reproduce the dated review; they do not refresh policies or enable crawling. The public website manifest is unchanged.
