# RB05: distinctions for church study

This is a bounded, AI-assisted work/edition review. Scripture and the working 1689 Baptist reference govern selection; these authors' applications do not silently become the project's church constitution. [The source map](READING-MAP.md) gives exact pages and identities. [Primary evidence](theological-evidence.json) distinguishes institutional standards, attributed argument and unresolved contributor checks.

| Question | Source comparison | Preserve in later tables and answers |
|---|---|---|
| Who belongs to a local church? | Dagg ch.II; Keach reception and covenant; Living as a Church lesson2; Nettles, Church Purity | Regenerate membership means receiving credible professions and nurturing holiness. It does not give officers infallible access to another person's regeneration. Attendance statistics do not by themselves prove spiritual condition. |
| Where are the keys? | Keach section6; Dagg local churches/ministry; Living as a Church lesson5 | Congregational responsibility and elder leadership are complementary claims in these Baptist treatments. Do not turn elder oversight into independent episcopal or Presbyterian jurisdiction. |
| Are elders and deacons interchangeable? | Merkle, Dunlop, Schmucker and Newton in Deacons; Lay Elders | Elders teach and shepherd; deacons facilitate service and unity. A task-based structure is an argued practical application, not an additional universal biblical office qualification. Titles, roles and selection processes need separate columns. |
| May women be deacons? | Merkle's interpretation of 1 Timothy3:11; current Del Ray leadership includes deaconesses | Merkle's article assumes the wives reading; that does not settle the alternate women-deacons reading. Del Ray's practice is recorded as a distinct church application. Its common gospel confession does not erase this difference. Neither view becomes the owner's policy through acquisition. |
| What does Acts6 establish? | Deacons issue | A service/teaching distinction and useful pattern do not prove that every detail is the formal deacon office or a universal organizational prescription. Keep the author's exegetical argument visible. |
| How should discipline begin and end? | Dever/Gilbert, Discipline II; Dagg ch.IX; Keach sections7–12; Boyce1852 article | Dever's title warns against immediate implementation in an untaught church; it does not reject biblical discipline. Distinguish teaching, private correction, reliable witnesses, congregational action, repentance and restoration. |
| Who is a non-attender? | Schmucker, Those Toxic Non-Attenders/Cleaning Up the Rolls | Willful abandonment differs from illness, disability, age or another providential inability to attend. The article itself exempts and urges care for the sick/elderly. Do not convert a pastoral distinction into an indiscriminate attendance score. |
| Can resignation avoid discipline? | Leeman, Preemptive Resignation; multi-pastor forum | Record the author's covenant and authority reasoning as an attributed position. Procedures and legal assumptions remain contextual; these articles are not an automated disciplinary workflow. The forum contains different contributors, including Presbyterian Philip Ryken, requiring individual scopes. |
| How broad is communion? | Dagg ch.V; earlier RB02 ordinance maps | Historical baptism/communion boundaries must retain their own claims. Core gospel confidence does not make every Baptist author identical on open, close or closed communion. This batch does not choose a new boundary. |
| What does Fuller add? | Reused 1846 collection: church-polity, ordination and communion bands; instrumental-music and singing essays | Fuller's letters argue for strict communion while recognizing conscientious disagreement. His instrumental-music argument and practical singing counsel are distinct. Do not flatten those into the modern music articles or the owner's default policy. Running page headings are not reliable essay starts; the exact body boundaries were reviewed. |
| What regulates worship? | Dagg ch.VII; held Keach Breach Repaired; Founders90 | Scripture's authority over gathered worship is distinct from each writer's application to music, instruments, style and congregational participation. Keach's historic defense of congregational singing is not a modern music policy. |
| Can Presbyterian music counsel help? | Kevin DeYoung, Ten Principles for Church Music | Use a conservative Reformed comparison on lyrics, love, intelligibility and congregational singing. Preserve his Presbyterian affiliation; do not transfer his baptism, covenant or government conclusions to Baptist default teaching. |
| What makes preaching faithful? | Broadus1898, Spurgeon Lectures, Scheiderer in Founders129 | Exposition and gospel truth govern technique. Broadus's 1898 edition includes Dargan's editing; Spurgeon's combined witness has two introductions and 28 numbered lectures. Do not call either a first-edition autograph or count introductions as lectures. |
| Are all early Baptists doctrinally interchangeable? | Founders129: Collins, Puritan origins and John Smyth | Historical biography and quoted positions have separate roles. Smyth's changing positions are historical evidence, not approved Particular Baptist teaching. Marsh's Collins/covenant interpretation remains his interpretation, not proof that all Baptist covenant models are identical. |
| Who wrote Dagg's introduction and appendix? | RB01 correction; recovered Obedience to Christ; Dagg preface | The old Founders “Introduction” is Stan Reeves's modern confession editorial. Dagg's recovered introduction is a different asset. Dagg credits Samson with the chief Appendix article; segment authorship before admitting that mixed component. Preserve originals and disclose transcription errors. |

No global author eligibility changed. Uncertain contributors remain in the decision queue; source-first bibliography entries do not override it.
