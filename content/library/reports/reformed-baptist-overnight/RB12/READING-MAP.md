| Historical question | Start here | What to preserve |
|---|---|---|
| Where did Particular Baptists emerge? | [Belyea: The JLJ Church, Southwark](https://www.thegospelcoalition.org/themelios/article/origins-of-the-particular-baptists/) / derivative-lines:21-44 | Distinguish Independent beginnings, uncertain 1633 rebaptism and later explicit believer baptism; compare the held First London 1644/1646 witnesses. |
| What can a local church record actually prove? | [Broadmead account of the five-person 1640 gathering](https://archive.org/download/cu31924029452277/cu31924029452277_djvu.txt) / derivative-lines:4680-4754 | Read Terrill as an edited retrospective record. Preserve mixed/open beginnings, later doctrinal development and unidentified continuators. |
| How do editions change historical evidence? | [Hayden on editions, Underhill insertions and lost correspondence](https://archive.org/download/bristol-record-society-27/bristol-record-society-27_djvu.txt) / derivative-lines:151-239 | Compare 1847 modernization/insertions with the officially offered 1974 edition; one underlying record corpus, two witnesses. |
| What did a persecuted Baptist say about his own life? | [Kiffin: Religious changes](https://www.onthewing.org/user/Kiffin%20-%20Remarkable%20Passages.pdf) / derivative-lines:579-707 | Compare self-report with Orme interpretation, the held Sober Discourse, and the primary London letter; never infer every political judgment is doctrine. |
| What happened to Knollys’s later autobiography? | [Kiffin continuation / lost remainder notice](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/textcreationpartnership/A47565/master/A47565.xml) / derivative-lines:326-339 | The edition states the remainder cannot be found. Treat continuation, legacy and publisher advertisements as different voices. |
| How did churches cooperate without identical institutions? | [Philadelphia association record 1707](https://baptisthistoryhomepage.com/phila.minutes.1707-1768.html) / derivative-lines:5-7 | Start with early associational practice and the 1749 doctrine/order essay; compare held Second London chapter 26 without asserting direct dependence. |
| How were confessional doctrines taught by associations? | [Philadelphia 1785 circular: Justification / confession chapter XI](https://baptisthistoryhomepage.com/1785.cl.phila.html) / derivative-lines:1-68 | William Rogers justification and Thomas Ustick adoption (1786) explicitly name confession chapters; retain supplied titles and signatures. |
| How did war affect church cooperation? | [Gillette note: no association meeting in 1777](https://baptisthistoryhomepage.com/philadelphia.minutes.1776.html) / derivative-lines:41-43 | No meeting is reported by a later editor. The repeated note is one editorial claim, not two contemporary meeting records. |
| How did confessional Baptists move into organized missions? | [RB07 existing document map](../RB07/DOCUMENT-TABLE.md) | Reuse RB07 Fuller, Carey Enquiry, Serampore Agreement, letters, Judson and Rice; distinguish mission primary documents from later biography. |
| Which origin stories need stronger corroboration? | [Ivimey preface](https://www.reformedreader.org/history/ivimey/volume1/contents.htm) / derivative-lines:34-114 | Ivimey’s ancient continuity program and Belyea’s Puritan-roots argument are historiographical claims. Do not turn a narrative into genealogical or doctrinal certainty. |
