# RB02: covenant and ordinance distinctions

The working reference remains the **1677/1689 Second London Baptist Confession**. This acquisition does not choose one detailed covenant model for the owner, rewrite that confession or make a modern label a test of every Baptist's orthodoxy. The source texts and the author's arguments come first.

All these study paths affirm salvation through Christ by grace, not an alternative Old Testament way of earning eternal salvation. Their disagreements concern how particular covenants reveal, administer, typify or establish that salvation, and what follows for the church and its ordinances.

## Use in the later knowledge system

A private search result is not a judgement that a statement fits the chosen theology. Acquisition, full-text ingestion, embedding, author approval and normative answer use are separate states.
