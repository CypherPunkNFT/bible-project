## Differences retained for study

The [comparison table](COVENANT-MODELS.md) separates historical Coxe, Gill, Pink and Keach; contemporary Federalist retrieval; Waldron's current formulation; and the acquired confessional comparison of progressive covenantalism. **Progressive covenantalism is a conservative Baptist model**, not the progressive/liberal denominational category excluded by this campaign. Shared Christ-centred salvation does not erase differences about Abraham, Sinai, promise/accomplishment, law and Sabbath.

Separate ordinance rows address immersion, credible profession, circumcision, household passages, church constitution, spiritual feeding, Table admission and professing children. Quoted opponents are not approved teaching. Booth/Keach strict communion, Spilsbury's gospel-offer restriction and Waldron's child-baptism policy remain attributed positions. This mission does not choose one detailed model or church policy for the owner.
