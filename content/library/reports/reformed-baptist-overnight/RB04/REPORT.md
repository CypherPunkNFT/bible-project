## Source, access and quality exceptions

The cached response was then validated; these were resolved completeness-check heuristics, not repeated source failures. Acquisition manifest failures are now empty.

The public client and book API exposed the offered link; review then allowed the unauthenticated download. No purchase, login, private endpoint guess or workaround was used. The EPUB's internal 2017 abridgment/annotation notice is retained.

Monergism permits private study/reference/indexing under its recorded policy while restricting external file redistribution and public AI-corpus republication. Chapel's free download does not remove editorial copyright. Archive underlying historical works are public domain; later additions and notices remain recorded. No private bodies or derivatives are placed in Git; this report is metadata.

Murray's complete *Redemption Accomplished and Applied* remains a commercial full-book gap. [Banner's article](https://banneroftruth.org/us/resources/articles/2011/definitive-and-progressive-sanctification/) explicitly offers selected *Collected Writings* extracts, not a whole volume. These discovery pages were not counted as acquired full books. A held Sibbes `Back To Top` NCX target points to an absent anchor; the body member exists.
