## The six selection anchors

Scripture authority, Trinity, Christ's deity/humanity, substitutionary atonement, bodily resurrection, and salvation by grace through faith alone are supported by the primary Hinson/CHBC/Phoenix statements, Westminster references on the author/institution's own pages, Chapel's explicit 1689 guide, and the actual gospel/ethical arguments. A short ethics essay need not restate every doctrine; its teaching and wider source evidence are read together. The seven cached primary records have byte hashes. Phoenix and Chapel's full about text were verified through the web tool; a collector 403 and a React shell are not passed off as hashed full doctrine.

Chapel explicitly says that publishing an author on a subject does not approve every doctrinal position. This is applied to each issue. Catholic/progressive teaching resources were not collected as comparison material. A covetousness issue with unresolved contributor fit was left in the decision queue rather than automatically approved because Chapel hosts it.

## Reused ethical witnesses

Watson's Ten Commandments, Beatitudes, Divine Contentment and Godly Man; Plumer's Law of God; Ryle's Practical Religion/Forgiveness; Steele's Religious Tradesman; Perkins's Vocations; Piper's Living in the Light; Owen's Mortification; and John Murray's Definitive Sanctification remain twelve actual held witnesses. Their selected headings/anchors were checked. Anglican/Presbyterian polity, older social/economic conventions, pastoral rhetoric, editor modernization and nested quotations are attributable context rather than binding Baptist policy. Broad surname searches also found other Murrays/Watsons; those do not substitute for John Murray/Thomas Watson.

## What this review establishes

Targeted identity, edition, gospel/ethical reasoning, source doctrine and relevant sections were inspected. Integrity and structural extraction checks are not a claim to have critically read/collated all 146,763 new words or every word of 1,138 held originals. Reading routes establish useful contextual study, not comprehensive passage commentary, medical advice, a modern safeguarding manual or a single project position on every contested ethical case.

The [table-first reading map](READING-MAP.md) keeps forgiveness meanings separate and pairs duties with gospel grounds. Its justice route starts with fair dealing, truthful treatment and protecting neighbors, rather than political polemic. Conscience freedom does not make every choice morally indifferent; charity does not erase accountability or require concealment of harm.
