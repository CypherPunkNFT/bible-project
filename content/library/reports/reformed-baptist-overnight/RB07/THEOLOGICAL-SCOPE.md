# RB07 theological and contributor scope

The working reference remains Scripture and the 1689 Second London Baptist Confession. RB07 adds attributed historical evidence and gospel exposition; it does not replace the project's doctrinal standards or approve an author's complete output. All seven new parent witnesses and 69 prepared text slices remain evidence-only pending segmented aggregate intake. This table records source-supported fit and the boundaries that later intake must enforce.

## Gospel teaching: Christmas Evans's translated sermons

The actual 1857 witness names Evans's Baptist ministry and preserves substantial gospel preaching. Its 1846 Advertisement distinguishes two translation hands. Joseph Cross's memoir, editorial qualifications and notes are separate voices; Cross expressly declines to endorse every view. The English witness is neither the Welsh original nor a verbatim transcript of oral delivery.

The original editor disputes Evans's comparison of Christ's hours of suffering with the eternal punishment of the lost: body marker `{185}` occurs around line 6655 in Sermon XI, and the separate editorial note is at lines 12824-12826. Note `{132}` at 12818-12821 similarly questions the relationship between suffering and the dignity of the sufferer. These are real contributor disagreements, not silently harmonized teaching. Component metadata links sermon footnote markers to their source locations. Publisher advertisements (10847-12634) are excluded from author-teaching scope; nine extracts end at 10840, and notes begin at 12635.
