# RB08: theological and scholarly boundaries

The working reference remains Scripture and the 1689 Baptist confession. These acquisitions add selected Christian arguments, not new confessions or blanket author approvals. [Theological decisions](theological-decisions.json) identify the primary evidence for the six required doctrinal anchors and distinguish a suitable study subject from rights and importer readiness.

## Selected authors

**James White:** Alpha and Omega Ministries' own confession supports Scripture's authority, the Trinity, substitutionary redemption and justification by grace through faith alone. The selected long Christological essays defend deity and genuine humanity. White's separate [John 2 response](https://www.aomin.org/aoblog/jehovahs-witnesses/we-chatted-with-a-jw-last-night/) explicitly supports bodily resurrection; it was checked as doctrinal evidence, not counted as a substantial acquisition. His [1689 justification discussion](https://www.aomin.org/aoblog/reformed-baptist-issues/72704-an-interesting-expansion-in-the-lbcf-1689/) supplies additional Baptist confessional context. The selection covers written biblical apologetics; it does not endorse every ministry controversy, rhetorical judgment or historical generalization.

**Michael J. Kruger:** his [RTS faculty biography](https://rts.edu/people/dr-michael-j-kruger/) identifies the canon scholar and author site. [RTS's purpose](https://rts.edu/about/purpose/) affirms the inerrant sixty-six books and faculty teaching in the Westminster confessional tradition. Westminster chapters 2, 8 and 11 provide institutional evidence for the other orthodox anchors. This is conservative Presbyterian scholarship within canon and apologetics; Presbyterian baptism and church government do not become the project's Baptist default. The actual selected texts support the declared subjects. Neither a journal's entire contents nor every cited scholar is admitted by acquiring one Kruger essay.

## Canon definitions before graph relationships

The complete 2012 essay supplies this useful study distinction. These are Kruger's analytical categories, not three competing lists of biblical books. Exact derivative ranges and PDF pages are in [chapter-map.json](chapter-map.json); the named sections appear in the [reading map](READING-MAP.md).

| Definition | What it addresses | What it does not establish alone | Source section |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Exclusive | A fixed and closed collection and agreement on its boundaries. | Earlier authoritative use and substantial agreement over core books can be obscured if this is the sole definition. | 2. The Exclusive Definition of Canon |
| Functional | Books serving as Scripture and an authoritative norm. | Use alone does not explain what makes the books God's word. | 3. The Functional Definition of Canon |
| Ontological | Books God gave his corporate church; authority precedes church recognition. | It must not erase the historical process through which the church recognized the books. | 4. The Ontological Definition |

## Existing holdings and attribution corrections

Warfield's inspiration, Christology and atonement collections and Piper's *A Peculiar Glory*, *Fifty Reasons* and suffering anthology already have readable bodies. They were reused, not downloaded again. Warfield's conservative Presbyterian context remains distinct from Baptist ordinance/polity teaching. Overlapping essays in different Warfield collections require underlying-work deduplication.

Actual chapter authors include Piper, Mark Talbot, Stephen Saint, Carl Ellis, David Powlison, Dustin Shramek and Joni Eareckson Tada; its cancer appendix is jointly Piper/Powlison. The map records actual bylines. It does not turn an editor label into authorship or approve all contributors globally.

Carson's held *Trinity* PDF is a slice of dictionary pages. The signed Carson entry occupies derivative lines 86–308; surrounding entries by other authors are excluded from that reading unit. His held Romans 3 essay is one chapter from *The Glory of the Atonement*, not the whole anthology. The desk's *A Christian Perspective on Islam* is actually **Chawkat Moucarry**, not Carson. Its author/work eligibility remains unresolved here; it is recorded as a correction and is not selected as approved teaching. Existing database rows were not silently relabeled.

Fifteen HTML bodies and two text-layer PDF essays were acquired. No scans, separate image assets, OCR, media or transcription jobs were used. The scholarly canon article's HTML/XML contains no full body, making its clean offered PDF a justified text route. Other modern author-offered texts are limited private reading witnesses with corpus-use rights review still required. No public text hosting is authorized.
