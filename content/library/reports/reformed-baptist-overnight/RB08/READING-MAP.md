Authority is distinguished from reception, manuscript copying and apologetic method.

Piper argues for recognition of divine glory; Warfield for biblical inspiration; Kruger for presuppositional authority. Do not merge them into one anonymous claim.

Scripture starting points: 2 Timothy 3:16, 2 Peter 1:20-21. These are study routes, not exhaustive verse-coverage claims.
