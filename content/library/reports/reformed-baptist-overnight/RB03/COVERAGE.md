Henry volumes I–IV together cover the Old Testament chapter sequence. Only II–IV are new. His New Testament volumes were not acquired. Gill is a deliberately selected book subset, not a whole-Bible acquisition.

Exact chapter IDs, original paths, SHA-256 values, verse-entry numbers and omissions are in [chapter-coverage.json](chapter-coverage.json). Selected but unacquired Gill chapters are listed in [book-coverage.json](book-coverage.json); bodyless and reference-only responses are not filled exposition gaps. Keach’s volumes and individual sermon TOC locators are in [volume-coverage.json](volume-coverage.json); passage labels need the separate exception review.
