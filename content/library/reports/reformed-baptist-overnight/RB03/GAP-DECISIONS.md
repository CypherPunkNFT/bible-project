# RB03: genuine gaps versus stale or misleading counts

The October 5 sermon map records source-assigned main texts. Its low percentages and 354 chapters without mapped sermon texts are useful discovery signals, not proof that readable commentaries are absent. RB03 checked 186 actual originals and 37 input documents/ledgers before acquisition. It also checked every one of the 45 numbered Calvin XML volumes and the specific structure of the held Pink, Gill, Keach, Bridges, Manton and Henry witnesses.

| Area | Existing evidence | Acquisition decision | Benefit and limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ezra, Nehemiah, Esther, Chronicles and broader history | Ezra’s sermon main-text coverage is 0.36%; Henry’s held broad-title ebook contains only Genesis–Deuteronomy. Calvin’s held set does not supply these books. | Henry II, plus Gill’s selected historical books | Sustained readable exposition replaces relying on thin sermon metadata. Gill’s sparse Chronicles chapters remain explicit exceptions. |
| Ezekiel 21–48 | Held Calvin volumes have chapter divisions 1–20; later translation/appendix references are not sustained treatment of the remaining visions. Sermon main-text coverage is 6.36%. | Henry IV and Gill’s entire offered 48-chapter sequence | Complete chapter availability for later judgment, restoration and temple visions; interpretive claims remain author-specific. |
| Judges | Sermon main-text coverage is 4.21%; no full Gill witness held in the audited originals. | Henry II and Gill’s 21-chapter sequence | Continuous historical and theological reading, rather than scattered character sermons. |
| Leviticus and Numbers | Calvin’s topical Harmony of the Law and Henry’s held Volume I already exist. | Gill’s complete offered sequences | A distinct Baptist verse-labelled reading companion. This is an author/method gap, not proof that all commentary was missing. |
| Job and wisdom | Bridges’ Proverbs and Ecclesiastes are held; Pink’s Gleanings are selective. Henry’s wisdom volume was absent. | Henry III; Gill Job, Proverbs and Ecclesiastes | Continuous Job exposition and a Baptist comparison for wisdom. Bridges is reused, not reacquired or counted as a gap. |
| Minor prophets | Calvin’s actual offered volumes are held despite stale earlier discovery/index summaries. Full Gill sequences were not held. | Henry IV and all twelve offered Gill minor-prophet books | Two additional sustained reading witnesses, including Baptist coverage; not a claim that Calvin’s text was unavailable. |
| Hebrews and James | Pink Hebrews has all 126 exposition chapter headings, through Hebrews 13. Manton James includes all five biblical chapter divisions; Calvin’s relevant witnesses are held. | Reuse; no new duplicate download | Existing readable depth is substantial. Remaining editorial/verse-level quality review is separate from acquisition. |
| Gospel parables | The held Keach ebook contains offered Book III only: four parable divisions and 32 sermon headings. | Missing I, II and IV; reuse III | Completes the offered four-book set: 47 parable/similitude divisions and 146 sermon headings, of which 114 headings are in new volumes. It is not a full Gospel commentary. |
| Ruth | Existing sermon report has 100% main-text range coverage; new Henry II also includes all four chapters. | No extra Gill expansion during this budget | Avoids using the final minutes on an already strong area. The sermon percentage still does not certify exegetical quality of every verse. |
| Philemon, 2 John, 3 John | Earlier sermon coverage identifies sparse short-letter witnesses; the selected Gill book sequences were not held. Calvin covers Philemon but his Catholic Epistles volume does not cover 2/3 John. | Three complete offered Gill single-chapter books | Adds bounded contextual reading; not an indiscriminate NT sweep. |

The decisive additions are two completions within declared scope—Henry’s Old Testament and Keach’s offered four-book set—and one selected Gill subcorpus. They are not hundreds of newly discovered independent books. [Book/chapter table](COVERAGE.md), [actual held-file audit](holdings-audit.json), [Calvin volume identities](calvin-held-volumes.json), [Ezekiel boundary evidence](calvin-ezekiel-boundary.json), and [reused exposition witnesses](reused-expositions.json) preserve the evidence.

No dictionary, lexicon, Josephus text, audio, new OCR or isolated generic sermon sweep is counted as a new commentary acquisition here. Further whole-Bible breadth and modern biblical-theology books remain separate future tasks.
