# Grace and the beginning of Christian life

Learn to receive salvation as grace, respond in repentance and faith, and grow with other believers.

**For:** New believers and Christians revisiting the foundations

No prior theological reading required. Work through difficult words with a trusted Christian or church leader.

Three stages, usually one stage per week; take longer where useful. This is a suggested pace, not measured reading time.

Read Scripture first, then the assigned sermon selection and substantial reading. Annotations and exercises are this library’s editorial guidance. Sources open at their hosts; page references distinguish assigned excerpts from whole works.

## 1 Receive grace

Begin with what God gives before asking what you must achieve.

**Scripture:** [Romans 3:21–31](https://bibleproject.io/read/kjv/ROM/3?v=21). Places faith, Christ’s redemption and boasting within Paul’s argument.

**Sermon:** Charles Haddon Spurgeon, [Justification by Grace](https://www.spurgeongems.org/sermon/chs126.pdf#page=4). Sermon excerpt, PDF page 4.

The courtroom explanation makes justification easier to distinguish from self-improvement.

**Reading note:** The main text is Romans 3:24. Connections to other passages are editorial doctrinal companions.

**Substantial reading:** Charles Haddon Spurgeon, [All of Grace](https://ccel.org/ccel/spurgeon/grace/grace.iv.html). All of Grace, complete chapter God Justifieth the Ungodly.

A complete chapter addresses the fear that an unworthy person cannot come to Christ.

**Reading note:** Historic English digital witness; no full-text copy or new critical edition is supplied.

**Reflect:** What is the ground of acceptance, and what place does faith have?

**Put it into practice:** Write a two-sentence account of grace without making your progress its price.

## 2 Respond with repentance and faith

Consider how turning to Christ changes your relation to sin.

**Scripture:** [Mark 1:14–15](https://bibleproject.io/read/kjv/MRK/1?v=14). Read Jesus’ call in the setting of his announcement of God’s kingdom.

**Sermon:** Charles Haddon Spurgeon, [Faith and Repentance Inseparable](https://www.spurgeongems.org/sermon/chs460.pdf#page=1). Sermon excerpt, PDF pages 1–2.

Explains why the call to repent and believe also gives the troubled hearer warrant to come.

**Reading note:** Main text Mark 1:15. Connections to other biblical books are thematic companions, not exposition of those books.

**Substantial reading:** Charles Haddon Spurgeon, [All of Grace](https://ccel.org/ccel/spurgeon/grace/grace.xvi.html). All of Grace, complete chapter Repentance Must Go With Forgiveness.

Shows why forgiveness and repentance belong together without treating repentance as a payment.

**Reading note:** Historic English digital witness; no full-text copy or new critical edition is supplied.

**Reflect:** How does repentance differ from trying to buy forgiveness?

**Put it into practice:** Name one concrete wrong to confess and one faithful response to practice.

## 3 Grow in new life and fellowship

Move from an isolated decision toward dependence on the Spirit and mutual care.

**Scripture:** [John 3:1–21](https://bibleproject.io/read/kjv/JHN/3?v=1). Read new birth and believing together within Jesus’ conversation.

**Scripture:** [Hebrews 10:19–25](https://bibleproject.io/read/kjv/HEB/10?v=19). Connect access to God with drawing near, hope and encouragement.

**Sermon:** John Piper, [Small Group Life in the Power of God’s Promises](https://www.desiringgod.org/messages/small-group-life-in-the-power-of-gods-promises). Written sermon sections on mutual encouragement and the dynamics of group ministry.

Shows how Christian encouragement can become practical care while leaving room for different church arrangements.

**Reading note:** Main text Hebrews 13:1–6. His Bethlehem group structure is an application, not a format prescribed by Hebrews. Audio not reviewed.

**Substantial reading:** Charles Haddon Spurgeon, [All of Grace](https://ccel.org/ccel/spurgeon/grace/grace.xiv.html). All of Grace, complete chapter Regeneration and the Holy Spirit.

Keeps the new birth grounded in God’s work and directs the reader to Christ.

**Reading note:** Historic English digital witness; no full-text copy or new critical edition is supplied.

**Reflect:** Which duties are in Hebrews, and which group arrangements are proposed ways of practicing them?

**Put it into practice:** Arrange one conversation about Scripture and one act of encouragement in your church.

## When you finish

Explain the difference between receiving Christ and earning acceptance, then name one practice of mutual Christian care.
