# Draft — Ligonier Ministries (R. C. Sproul's sermons)

**Status:** draft only. Not sent. Nobody has been contacted.

**Recipient:** Ligonier Ministries, copyright permissions (an organisation, not a person).

**How to send:** Ligonier's [copyright policy](https://www.ligonier.org/copyright-policy) sends permission questions to its
**Copyright Permissions Request Form**, a Microsoft Forms page linked from that policy under "Permission Requests"
(read 2026-11-27). Paste the message below into the form. The policy gives a postal alternative instead of an email
address: Ligonier Ministries, Contract Administrator, 421 Ligonier Court, Sanford, FL 32771. No email address is
published for permissions, so none is guessed here.

**Why this one is worth sending:** Ligonier's Terms of Use (read 2026-11-27) forbid using "any robot, scraper, crawler,
or other automated means to access the Ligonier Services for any purpose without our express written permission". So
today Sproul's sermons can only be listed by hand: 12 so far, the 2 Peter series. With permission the site could list
every Sproul sermon by the passage it treats, each linking back to Ligonier. Ligonier already encourages linking and
publishes this form, so a yes is realistic.

**Subject:** Permission request — listing R. C. Sproul's sermons by Bible passage, with links to Ligonier

---

Hello,

I run Bible Guide (bibleguide.io), a free, non-commercial Bible study site with no advertising. Its sermon library shows
which sermons expound which passages, so a reader studying, say, Romans 8 can see who has preached on it and go to the
source to listen.

We would like R. C. Sproul's sermons from Saint Andrew's Chapel to be part of it. For each sermon we would store only the
title, the date and the Bible passage it treats, and link to its page on learn.ligonier.org. We would not host, embed,
transcribe or re-upload any audio, video or text: every listen would happen on your site.

So far we have listed a dozen sermons by hand. Your Terms of Use ask for written permission before any automated
access, so I am asking rather than assuming. May we read your sermon listings with a slow, clearly identified crawler
for this purpose, or is there an export you would prefer we use? We will keep to any rate limit, credit wording or
scope you set.

Thank you,
[your name]
Bible Guide · bibleguide.io

---
