FAQs

Can I study remotely online at CBTS?

Absolutely. CBTS is built for distance learners:

  • Live‑streamed semester courses for real‑time interaction.
  • Self‑paced online courses.
  • Modular classes – In-person courses where lectures are delivered over a few days during holiday weekends like Memorial Day.
Is the seminary accredited?

Yes. Since 2019, CBTS has been an accredited member of the Association of Reformed Theological Seminaries (ARTS), recognized by the Council for Higher Education Accreditation (CHEA). ARTS accreditation assures that CBTS meets rigorous academic and theological standards without compromising our confessional commitments. Credits are widely transferable among accredited evangelical institutions.

What are the tuition and fees?
  • Tuition: $100 per credit hour
  • Semester Enrollment Fee: $450 each January & August
  • Application Fee: $100 (one‑time)
  • Graduation Fee: $250
  • Auditing: Free onsite evening classes; $20 per online course
When can I apply for studying?

Our rolling enrollment system means you can apply and start learning at any time. Aim to submit your application, transcripts, and interview materials at least a month before you wish to start.

Can I use a payment plan?

Yes. Contact the registrar to arrange an interest‑free installment plan that spreads your tuition and semester‑fee costs across the term.

Is financial assistance available?

Need‑based financial assistance for tuition and semester fees are awarded by the Student Assistance Committee upon application. Funds are limited, so apply early.

What scholarships does CBTS offer?
  • Andrew Fuller Scholarship – for high‑performing students (≥8 credits/semester & 3.5 GPA); provides full tuition and fee coverage for only $100/month.
  • William Carey Scholarship – for international students (≥10 credits/year & 3.0 GPA); may waive tuition, lower fees, and grant Logos resources.

Scholarship awards are determined each July for the following academic year.

What is the Church Partnership Program?

Churches that commit at least $250/mo receive tuition‑free study for their members (semester fee still applies) and streaming access to all lectures. A $500/mo tier also covers our ThM program tuition.

Do you accept the GI Bill?

No. While CBTS is accredited by ARTS, we do not participate in federal Title IV financial aid or the VA’s GI Bill programs.

Can I transfer credits from another institution?

Yes. Graduate credits (C or higher) that match CBTS program requirements may be transferred up to two‑thirds of your degree. At least one‑third must be completed at CBTS.

How quickly can I finish an MDiv?

Full‑time students often complete in 4 years (24–25 credits/yr). Part‑timers can extend to 8 years (12.5 credits/yr) to fit around work and church commitments.

What is the step‑by‑step admissions process?

Online application + $100 fee → official transcript → pastoral recommendation (if required) → phone/Zoom interview → admission decision.

May I study part‑time while working full‑time?

Yes. Evening live streams and self‑paced courses accommodate bi-vocational schedules.
