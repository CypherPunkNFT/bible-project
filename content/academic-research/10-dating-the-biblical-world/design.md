# Dating the biblical world: design record

The page: `/study/dating-the-biblical-world` (not yet built). Its evidence and limits: [the research record](../10-dating-the-biblical-world.json). Its verses and counts: a data build still to be written, which must fill every verse phrase from the site's held Berean text.

## What this page is for

A reader sees "853 BC" printed beside Ahab, or hears that radiocarbon has redated Solomon, and has no way to ask where the number came from. They leave able to tell a relative date from an absolute one, to follow the chain from one eclipse to a king of Israel, to see why one event has been given three dates, and to read a radiocarbon date as a range. The question it carries: what fixes this date, and how wide is it really?

## The material's own shape

The evidence already has a shape: one fixed point and a chain hung from it. The eponym lists are a run of named years that would float without the eclipse of 15 June 763 BC. Fix that one day and two and a half centuries of Assyrian years are fixed with it. Kings of Israel and Judah are then tied to that chain wherever an outside record names them. Further from the chain, at the division of the kingdom and in the ground, dates widen. The page draws exactly that: sharpness at the nail, widening away from it.

## Directions, and one honest gap

The study standard asks for three or four directions built as mock-ups, then a stop for the owner to choose. That stop is not possible inside a remote job, because the owner cannot answer mid-job. So three directions were weighed in writing, one is recommended, and the choice is the owner's to overturn on sight.

1. **One nail, and what hangs from it (recommended).** A single vertical line of years with one bright point at 763 BC. Eponym years are fine ticks above and below it, from about 910 to 649 BC, ending where the order of names stops being certain. Kings of Israel and Judah hang from it on short ties at each synchronism. Every mark is drawn as wide as its evidence: a hairline for the eclipse and 597, a year-wide bead for Qarqar, three separate marks for the division of the kingdom, a broad soft band for Iron Age IIA. The central comparison, how sharp each date is, is visible at once (rule A5).
2. **Lanes by method, on one horizontal line of time.** Clear, but it is the manuscripts study's dot field with bars instead of dots, and it hides the chain: the reader sees widths but not what each width hangs from. It survives as section 04's precision rows.
3. **A map of the archives,** Nineveh, Babylon, Samaria, Jerusalem, Karnak, with lines between them. Rejected: dating is not a matter of place, and lines between cities would look like influence the evidence does not show.

## Keeping it apart from its neighbours

Egypt beside Israel uses threads between two rulers; this page must not. Its ties are short, vertical, and run from one line to a name, never across between two columns. Manuscripts uses a dot field; this page uses widths. Israel among the empires keeps the story of the empires; here they appear only as records that carry a year. The exodus, Jericho, Genesis 1–11 and Daniel are handed to their own studies, and the page says so where a reader would look for them.

## Behaviour

The view lives in the address: `link` (the chosen chain link), `rule` (the counting rules switched on), `sync` (the chosen synchronism), `open` (the record sheet). Unknown values fall back to defaults. The counting-rule switches are pressed buttons; the moving mark is announced in words ("Division: 931/930 BC under these rules") so the change is not carried by motion alone. Motion is the slow orbit, a faint shadow crossing the sun disc and returning, and the nail's glow; all of it stops for reduced motion. The single glow on the page is the nail.

## Where the evidence cannot go

- **No date narrower than its record.** Each mark has a minimum drawn width so it can be pressed; the key says so.
- **The division is never one mark.** Three schemes, three marks, each labelled with who reckoned it.
- **No exodus date, no Solomon gate assigned.** Both are stated as handed over or open.
- **Radiocarbon is always a band.** No single year is printed for a radiocarbon result.
- **Widths are this study's summaries**, marked as such, until each is checked against its source.

## Doctrine

Academic study, so the reckonings are reported without ranking. The site's own voice holds Scripture as "truth, without any mixture of error" (Baptist Faith and Message 2000, article I) and says so at the stance after section 02. The confession fixes no chronology, so the page chooses none. Where a scholar amended a reign length or judged a synchronism an editor's error (Albright, Thiele on 2 Kings 18), the page reports it as theirs and does not adopt it; it shows the coregency reading that keeps both numbers, as a reconstruction.
