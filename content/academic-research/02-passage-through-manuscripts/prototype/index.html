The decision is still an argument

What could make an editor choose ο?

NET favors “we have” because Paul describes the consequences of justification. Yet it acknowledges strong early support for “let us have”. Context weighs in the decision; it cannot supply missing autograph ink.

  1. Attestation

    Which witnesses preserve each reading? Two selected codices cannot stand for the whole tradition.

  2. A copying explanation

    Similar sounds could explain either vowel replacing the other. A possible mechanism does not establish its direction.

  3. The argument in context

    Does Paul describe peace already possessed or urge his readers to embrace it? Historical editors have disagreed.

The editor still has a separate question

The prevailing critical judgment is that the earliest attainable text ends at 16:8. That does not settle whether Mark intended this ending or an earlier ending disappeared. The Longer Ending circulated early; circulation and original authorship are different claims.
