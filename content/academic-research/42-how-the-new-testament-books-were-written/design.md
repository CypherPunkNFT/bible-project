## What this page is for

A reader hears that the Gospels contradict each other, that Q is a lost Gospel, that half of Paul's letters are forged, or that the Jesus Seminar found what Jesus really said. They have no way to test any of it. They leave able to set three tellings side by side and say what shared wording shows; to name four answers to the Synoptic question with their holders; to tell what a book says of its writer from what a later witness says; to tell the quest's history in its own terms and give the evangelical answer; and to find any book's writer, witness and date in one line. The question it carries: who says so, how early, and what could they have known?

## The material's own shape

The Synoptic question has an object of its own: the synopsis, the Gospels printed in parallel columns so a reader can see what is shared. Griesbach printed one in the 1770s [check: year], and every student of the question still works from one. So the page's central view is that object. The authorship question also has a shape of its own: a chain of witnesses, each quoting the one before, most of them surviving only inside Eusebius. The page draws that chain as it really is, with the lost books shown as lost.

## Directions, and one honest gap

The study standard asks for three or four directions built as mock-ups, then a stop for the owner to choose. That stop is not possible inside a remote job. So three directions were weighed in writing, one is recommended, and the choice is the owner's to overturn on sight.

1. **A synopsis, then four maps (recommended).** Three columns of one event, the shared narrator's aside tied across all three; then four small arrow-diagrams of the same size, one per Synoptic view, each labelled with who holds it. Equal size is the argument: no view is drawn as the main one. The pattern is visible at once (rule A5), and it is the subject's own form.
2. **A line of composition, AD 30 to 100.** Clear and familiar. Rejected as the lead: every book's date is a range, and the evangelical and critical ranges overlap and cross. A single line would pin what the evidence leaves open. It survives as the reference table's range bars, each book drawn twice where views differ, and never as points.
3. **A family tree of sources.** Rejected: any tree draws one hypothesis, and this page is bound not to choose. The four equal maps replace it.

Against the site's other studies: How the canon took shape sets three writers' lists side by side, and a synopsis also uses three columns. The difference is kept plain on the page. The canon's columns are lists of books, each cell a judgement. Here the columns are running text, read line by line, with ties drawn between the same words; the comparison is inside a sentence, not between lists. The witnesses' chain is a vertical thread of named people with gaps marked, which no study uses. The quest is told as stages on one track, but its bars carry no point dates either.

## Behaviour

The four maps are pressed buttons; whatever opens a sheet is marked as opening a dialog, and focus returns to the opener. ## Where the evidence cannot go

- **No direction on the synopsis ties.** A tie shows shared words, never an arrow.
- **No point dates.** Every date is a range; where evangelical and critical ranges differ, both are drawn and labelled.
- **No witness quoted unread.** Witnesses are restated until the editor reads the translation; the sheet says which.
- **Shared wording in English is labelled as English.** The Greek comparison waits on the build.

## Doctrine

Academic study, so history is reported without ranking. The site's voice holds Scripture as "truth, without any mixture of error" (Baptist Faith and Message 2000, article I), and states it once, in the stance. Where scholars who hold that differ (the Synoptic question, Hebrews, Revelation's date, the beloved disciple), the page does not choose. Letters written in another's name and a historian's Jesus set against the Gospels' Christ are described in their holders' terms as others' views, with the evangelical answer. Why Scripture is true is linked to the theology study Scripture (the word of God).

## What is checked

The build will prove that every verse exists in the Berean with its phrase, that the synopsis passages are the ranges named, that the reference table has all twenty-seven books once each in canonical order, and that every source id is used. None of this proves a reading is right or a date is correct.
