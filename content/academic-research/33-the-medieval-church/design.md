# The medieval church: design record

The page: [/study/the-medieval-church](../../../src/pages/study/medieval/MedievalPage.tsx). Its evidence and limits: [the research record](../33-the-medieval-church.json). Its verses and coast: [the data build](../../../scripts/build-medieval-study.py).

## What this page is for

A reader hears that the pope ruled Europe, that everyone believed, that the Bible was forbidden, or that people thought the earth was flat. They leave able to ask of any medieval sentence who wrote it and how far it reached, to keep a ruler's baptism apart from a people's faith, to read a disputed question in its own form, and to walk a parish naming the rule at each step and how little shows whether it was kept.

## Directions, and one honest gap

The study standard asks for three or four directions built as mock-ups, then a stop for the owner to choose. That stop is not possible inside a remote job, because the owner cannot answer mid-job. So three directions were weighed in writing, one was built, and the choice is the owner's to overturn.

1. **A line of years with decrees on one side, and an instrument for each field (built).** The gap between the order and the village is the whole argument, so it is drawn first.
2. **One timeline with four lanes, a century at a time.** Rejected: other studies already use bars, and lanes show sequence, not the gap.
3. **A narrative, century by century.** Rejected: it is a long scroll that hides the argument.

## Keeping it apart from its neighbours

How the church has read the Bible keeps the four senses and medieval Bible reading. The East-West split, monastic spirituality, doctrine as defined by councils, the meaning of worship, the first generations, Wycliffe onward, and Jews and Christians all go to the outlines and planned studies named on the page. Aquinas appears for the form of one article, and his system is left to Works that shaped Christianity. Crusades to the Holy Land and the inquisition have no study yet. The page says so, and does not cover them.

## What the three plan reviews changed

Review before code ran three times, as a security and correctness reader, a reliability reader, and a principal engineer. What they changed:

- **Quotations.** The Rouen visitation and Gregory's letter to Serenus had been cited without a fetched text behind them. Both were then fetched and read, and every quotation was checked against the fetched texts.
- **Practice read off a rule.** Sentences such as "most people received once a year" were the very mistake the page teaches against. They are now marked as this study's inference, or removed.
- **The word "baptised".** It is defined once as reporting the medieval rite, because the site's own confession uses it of believers only. The fourth claim's headline was changed to match.
- **Threads implied cause.** The lower side is called "set beside it", and pairs with no connection have dashed threads.
- **"Written N years later" could be false.** Where a story was written during its own events, the page now says so, and the build refuses a story dated before its events.
- **The tilt.** Each round now gives its reason and its limit, and the note says nothing adds up to a score.
- **Marks.** The ways of conversion have their own set of marks, separate from the fields. Force is an X, so it is not read as a cross.
- **Address values.** A year is parsed against a pattern and held inside the period. A field beats a pair it hides, and an open record beats both.
- **The slider** writes the address when let go, and not on each tick.

Not done: a reviewer suggested moving the article and the ladder to a second page, as the standard asks of overflow. They stay on this page, because they are the only place the schools are taught. Powers links back to the record for pairs it shares, and tells each round short.

## Form and motion

- The header drawing is new and ours: a sealed parchment on a cord above a small parish church. It is labelled illustrative. Motto: "Decreed at the top. / Lived in the parish."
- The tone is the Study hub field's colour for this category, and "chosen" is carried by ink, weight and underline as well.
- Motion is a slow orbit and two sparks in the header, and the balance easing to its tilt. All of it stops under reduced motion.
- Each drawing is one described image. The lists are the interface, at a 44-pixel target, and the drawings repeat them for a pointer, out of the tab order. Every reply says in words which objection it answers.
- The line runs along the page when wide and down it on a phone. Marks in one year stack outward and keep their date. Tests hold them apart at both shapes.
- The map uses real coast from Natural Earth. A close mark moves aside on a leader line and its true place keeps a dot. Poland's mark is a dashed ring because the place is disputed.

## What these checks prove, and what they do not

The build proves that each verse phrase is in the Berean, that every reference resolves, and that dates run forward. It does not prove a reading. A one-off check found the page's quotations in the texts fetched for this study, apart from the four popular claims, which are nobody's quotation, the Berean verse the build checks itself, and Harald's stone, whose words the source sets out across table rows. It does not prove those texts are well translated. Each tilt and each pair is a judgement, and the page says so.
