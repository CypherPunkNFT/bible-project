# God and Christian Faith study ideas

Develop studies that trace Christian teaching through passages and arguments. Separate what a passage explicitly says, how Christian traditions synthesize passages, and where those traditions disagree.

These are content proposals for review. Subcards are working content divisions, not site components. Passage lists are starting points for later reading and verification, not completed research.

## 21 Knowing God

**Purpose:** Explore how Scripture speaks of God’s character and his making himself known.

**General direction:** Connect names, descriptions and actions without reducing God to an isolated list of attributes.

**Possible subcards:** God makes himself known; holiness; mercy and faithfulness; justice; wisdom; love.

**Starting passages or sources:** Exodus 3:1–15; Exodus 34:5–9; Isaiah 6:1–8; Romans 11:33–36; 1 John 4:7–21.

## 22 Father Son and Holy Spirit

**Purpose:** Examine the passages behind Christian teaching about Father, Son and Spirit.

**General direction:** Distinguish the biblical wording from later doctrinal formulations, and explain disputed interpretations accurately when relevant.

**Possible subcards:** One God; the Father; the Son; the Spirit; passages naming all three; how Christian doctrine brings the texts together.

**Starting passages or sources:** Deuteronomy 6:4–5; Matthew 28:16–20; John 1:1–18; John 14–16; 2 Corinthians 13:14.

## 23 The Holy Spirit

**Purpose:** Trace the Spirit’s work across the New Testament.

**General direction:** Study presence, new life, assurance, gifts and fruit in context; identify differing Christian readings of contested passages.

**Possible subcards:** The promised Spirit; new birth; indwelling and assurance; gifts; fruit; walking by the Spirit.

**Starting passages or sources:** John 3:1–8; John 14:15–26; Acts 2; Romans 8; 1 Corinthians 12–14; Galatians 5:13–26.

## 24 Grace faith and salvation

**Purpose:** Explore how the New Testament explains God’s saving work and the response of faith.

**General direction:** Follow complete arguments instead of defining salvation through isolated proof texts.

**Possible subcards:** Grace and gift; repentance and faith; justification; new birth; assurance; faith and works.

**Starting passages or sources:** Romans 3:21–5:11; Ephesians 2:1–10; Titus 3:3–7; James 2:14–26; 1 John 5:1–13.

## 25 What the cross accomplishes

**Purpose:** Study the language Scripture uses for Christ’s saving death.

**General direction:** Compare sacrifice, redemption, reconciliation, victory and example, noting overlap and differences among theological interpretations.

**Possible subcards:** Sacrifice; redemption; reconciliation; bearing sins; victory; love revealed.

**Starting passages or sources:** Romans 3:21–26; 2 Corinthians 5:14–21; Colossians 2:8–15; Hebrews 9–10; 1 Peter 2:21–25.

## 26 Covenants and promises

**Purpose:** Follow the promises and covenant language that connect biblical books.

**General direction:** Distinguish the text’s named covenants from later theological systems and explain how later writers appeal to earlier promises.

**Possible subcards:** Noah; Abraham; Sinai; David; the promised new covenant; covenant language in the letters.

**Starting passages or sources:** Genesis 9; Genesis 12:1–3; Genesis 15; Exodus 19–24; 2 Samuel 7; Jeremiah 31:31–34; Hebrews 8.

## 27 Scripture and its witness

**Purpose:** Explore what biblical writers say about Scripture and how they use it.

**General direction:** Distinguish claims about inspiration and authority from the historical questions of canon, manuscripts and translation.

**Possible subcards:** Inspired Scripture; teaching and correction; reading in context; Jesus and Scripture; the apostles’ use of earlier texts; the question of canon.

**Starting passages or sources:** 2 Timothy 3:14–17; 2 Peter 1:16–21; Luke 24:25–49; Romans 15:1–6.

## 28 The church and its calling

**Purpose:** Study the identity and shared practices of the Christian community.

**General direction:** Focus on the church as a people; leave detailed interpersonal application to Life together. Identify differences over church order and practices.

**Possible subcards:** Body and members; household and temple; gathering and worship; leadership and service; baptism and the Lord’s Supper; witness and mission.

**Starting passages or sources:** 1 Corinthians 11–14; Ephesians 2:11–22; Ephesians 4:1–16; 1 Timothy 3; 1 Peter 2:4–12.

## 29 Humanity sin and renewal

**Purpose:** Explore what Scripture says about human dignity, wrongdoing and renewal.

**General direction:** Distinguish shared textual foundations from differing interpretations of the fall, inherited sin and human freedom.

**Possible subcards:** Made in God’s image; the fall; sin and accountability; Adam and Christ; renewal in God’s image; embodied life.

**Starting passages or sources:** Genesis 1:26–31; Genesis 3; Romans 3:9–26; Romans 5:12–21; Colossians 3:1–11; James 3:9–10.

## 30 The hope to come

**Purpose:** Study resurrection, judgment and the promised renewal of creation.

**General direction:** Begin with central passages, then compare disputed questions of sequence and imagery rather than treating one timeline as settled.

**Possible subcards:** The return of Christ; resurrection; judgment; creation renewed; new heavens and earth; living in hope.

**Starting passages or sources:** 1 Corinthians 15; 1 Thessalonians 4:13–5:11; Romans 8:18–25; 2 Peter 3; Revelation 20–22.
