# Grace faith and salvation

**Content pass status:** item 24, first full draft ready for review, 2026-10-13. [Content draft](content.md); [passage and editorial notes](passage-notes.md).

## Purpose and scope

Follow the New Testament's arguments about God's saving work and faith's response. Keep grace, repentance, justification, renewal, assurance, and active faith distinct enough to read carefully, while showing their connections. Selected confessional summaries are attributed, not presented as direct Bible quotations or all Christian positions.

## Content divisions

1. It is the gift of God — Ephesians' movement from death to life and good works.
2. Repent ye, and believe the gospel — Jesus' announcement and Paul's testimony.
3. Justified freely by his grace — Romans, Abraham, peace with God, and attributed formulations.
4. According to his mercy he saved us — Titus on regeneration, renewal, and conduct.
5. That ye may know — the Son, testimony, eternal life, and assurance.
6. Faith without works is dead — James' needy neighbour, Abraham, Rahab, and comparison with Paul.

Two subdivisions per section organise content only. Preserve the accepted outline in `../ideas.md`. No site design or infrastructure is involved.
