# Humanity sin and renewal working notes

**Item 29 · God and Christian Faith · First draft · 2026-10-13**

## Purpose and central question

How does Scripture hold together human dignity, wrongdoing, dependence on grace and renewal? Trace the accepted themes without equating sinfulness with worthlessness or treating dignity as innocence.

## Content divisions

1. In the image of God — Genesis and James connect creation with dignity and speech.
2. I was afraid and I hid myself — disobedience, hiding, responsibility and consequences in Genesis 3.
3. All under sin — Romans 3's universal accountability and turn toward grace.
4. By the obedience of one — Adam and Christ, with attributed accounts of inherited sin and inability.
5. Renewed in knowledge — the Creator's image and the concrete pattern of putting off and putting on.
6. The redemption of our body — bodily conduct, resurrection and patient hope.

Each section contains two subdivisions, readings, consideration and reflection. No layout or site implementation is proposed.

## Boundaries and reuse

See [content.md](content.md) and [passage notes](passage-notes.md). A new creation already develops the practical putting-off/putting-on material; this study explains its place within the wider account of humanity. Grace faith and salvation develops justification, while The hope to come will develop resurrection and final renewal.

Inherited guilt, corruption, freedom and grace are presented through named sources where compared. Do not make this brief introduction settle creation chronology, human origins, every reading of Genesis 3:15, or the salvation of infants. Keep dignity and accountability explicit without importing unrelated political debates.
