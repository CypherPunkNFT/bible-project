# Humanity, sin and renewal working notes

**Item 29 · God and Christian Faith · Rewritten to the study standard · 2026-10-30.** The first draft of 2026-10-13, in the King James, is in git history.

## Purpose and central question

Every person is made in God's image, has gone wrong, and can be made new only by Christ. How do you hold all three at once, without flattering people or writing them off? Read any person with three questions: what does this passage honour, what will it not excuse, and where does it point them? The [reader-facing text](content.md) is the source of the [mock-up](../../../../design/humanity-sin-and-renewal-directions/README.md).

## Content divisions

1. In the image of God: Genesis 1 and James 3.
2. I was afraid, so I hid: Genesis 3.
3. All under sin: Romans 3.
4. The obedience of the one man: Romans 5 and 1 Corinthians 15:22.
5. Renewed in the image: Colossians 3.
6. The redemption of our bodies: 1 Corinthians 6 and Romans 8.

Then two questions left open between named sources (how sin reaches us, and what the image is and whether it was lost), and a close with the gospel invitation.

## What changed from the first draft

- King James to Berean, every verse.
- Westminster and the Catholic Catechism as the two examples gave way to Baptist answers first, because the site speaks under the Baptist Faith and Message: Boyce, the Second London Confession, the Abstract of Principles and a 2012 Southern Baptist statement. Westminster, Trent and the Orthodox Longer Catechism stand after them, labelled as other traditions.
- Article 3 is now the site's voice on the nature inherited and on condemnation. How Adam's own guilt reaches us is left open, because Baptists differ and the confession does not say.
- Added: the image thread (Genesis 5:1 and 5:3, Genesis 9:6, Psalm 8:5, Romans 1:23, Hebrews 2:8–9, Colossians 1:15, 2 Corinthians 3:18 and 4:4, Hebrews 1:3, Ephesians 4:24, Romans 8:29, 1 Corinthians 15:49, 1 John 3:2); Romans 3:13–18 with the verses Paul draws on; Romans 6:13; 1 Corinthians 15:47; Genesis 3:15 as a labelled reading, with Matthew Henry; John 9:3; Romans 16:20.
- Added care notes on self-harm, on what was done to someone's body against their will, on illness and disability, on Genesis 3:16, on "worthless" in Romans 3:12, and on "all men" in Romans 5:18.

## Boundaries and reuse

[A new creation](../../christian-life/a-new-creation/content.md) develops the practical putting off and putting on. [Grace faith and salvation](../grace-faith-and-salvation/content.md) develops justification, [What the cross accomplishes](../what-the-cross-accomplishes/content.md) the saving death, and [The hope to come](../the-hope-to-come/content.md) the resurrection. This study does not settle how long ago or by what steps God made the world, what becomes of those who die before they can act morally, or what "all" and "many" mean in Romans 5 beyond what the page says.
