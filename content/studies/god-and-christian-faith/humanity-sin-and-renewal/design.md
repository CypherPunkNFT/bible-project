## Direction

Teach one habit: read any person with three questions. What does this passage honour, what will it not excuse, and where does it point them? Only one direction was built, as with the earlier mock-ups, because this was an existing draft.

## The page

- **Opening.** Study trail, the four concepts in gold, the title, the lead, Genesis 1:27, and a plain human outline made of dust motes inside a faint ring. They gather, hold and loosen. The outline and ring never go away, because the page does not teach that the image disappears. A visible button pauses it. With reduced motion it is a still figure. No face, and never God or Christ. Below it, four computed figures, each saying what it means and what it does not.
- **01 Six passages, one line.** Six points on one line, whose height says what the passage is mostly about: the wrong, or God's answer. The page says height is not worth, and the last stretch is dashed because it is not finished. Choosing a point opens that passage in place and in the address. The passage is set first, with the three questions in its margin, each with a small mark. Then what the text says, with this study's readings labelled where they come, then Look closely and Your response. Genesis 3 has a small figure of who is asked and who is spoken to. On a phone the line is a list on a side thread.
- **02 One body, two uses.** The signature, on a dark band dusted with points. One line drawing of a body with fine rules to its words. Romans 3:11–18 as Paul lists it, each with the verse he draws on. A switch from Romans 6:13 shows the same body with a verse where each part is used well. Each row says whether it is the same English word, a different word, or empty, and only Romans 6:13 is Paul's own joining.
- **03 One man, One Man.** Romans 5 as he wrote it: two sides and the word between them. One fixed step larger on the right where he says the two are not equal, and the caption says it is no ratio. Then 1 Corinthians 15. Then "How does it reach us?", with the confession first and the sources side by side, none chosen.
- **04 Not yet seen, but we see Jesus.** The image thread: eight stages and nineteen places, each stage with the same small drawing and one named mark. The Christ stage has no figure, only light. Then "What is the image?" and "Was it lost?".
- **Close.** The way of reading, the invitation, a question for the reader's own life, the passage again, back links, a line-art foot, and the sources with how far each was read. One sheet from the right for every verse.

## Doctrine and care

The site's voice follows the Baptist Faith and Message 2000, Articles 3, 4 and 15. What it does not settle, the page reports and leaves open: how Adam's guilt reaches us, what the image is, and what becomes of those who die before they can act morally. Care notes on self-harm, abuse, illness, Genesis 3:16, Romans 3:12 and Romans 5:18 are the site's own words, and the owner has not yet read them.

## What makes it unlike the other studies in this category

The line with a named height, the marginal questions beside the open passage, the dark body band with its switch, and the two-sided Romans 5. Covenants and promises and The hope to come use cards, tables and strips instead.
