# Passage notes: God: who He is and what He does (21)

Rewrite of draft 21 (Knowing God), widened to the row's three subjects: God's character, creation out of nothing, and providence. All Scripture re-quoted from the supplied Berean Standard Bible text; the draft's King James headings and quotations are removed.

## Owned passages

### Exodus 3:1–15 (part 01)
- **Context:** Moses in Midian, after fleeing Egypt (Exodus 2:15). The call at Horeb and the giving of the divine name, before the return to Pharaoh.
- **Main readings:** (1) "I AM WHO I AM" as God's self-existence and independence: long standing in Christian theology (classical theism). (2) As a promise of presence ("I will be who I will be"), tied to 3:12: found in the Babylonian Talmud (Berakhot 9b, as cited, to be checked) and followed by many Jewish and Christian readers. The study presents these as complementary. The angel of the LORD (3:2) is read by some as a created messenger speaking for God, and by many Christian interpreters as a pre-incarnate appearance of the Son; the study does not walk this and links 53.
- **Jewish practice:** described first (the name not pronounced; *Adonai*, *HaShem*), then the Christian echo in John 8:58 (referred to, not quoted).
- **Links:** Angels, demons and spiritual powers (53), which keeps 3:2–6 for the angel of the LORD (pair; 53 should link back here). Trinity articles.

### Isaiah 6:1–13 (part 02)
- **Context:** Isaiah's vision dated to Uzziah's death (2 Kings 15:2 gives his reign as fifty-two years). Commission to a hardened generation; the "holy seed" stump.
- **Main readings:** "Us" in 6:8 as the heavenly court (Jewish interpreters and many Christians; compare 1 Kings 22:19) or as a hint of the Trinity (many Christians from early times); the study shows both and does not rest the doctrine on it. John 12:41 links the vision to Christ's glory (referred to, not quoted).
- **Jewish practice:** 6:3 in the *Kedushah*; Christian *Sanctus*; Revelation 4:8 echo.
- **Care:** New Testament uses of 6:9–10 (Matthew 13:14–15; John 12:39–40; Acts 28:26–27) framed so that no verdict falls on the Jewish people; Romans 11:1–2 referred to.
- **Links:** Trinity articles; part 10 (Romans 11).

### Exodus 34:1–9 (part 03)
- **Context:** After the golden calf (Exodus 32) and Moses' intercession (Exodus 33). New tablets; the proclamation of the name.
- **Main readings:** 34:6–7 as the Bible's own summary of God's character, repeated through the Old Testament (Numbers 14:18; Nehemiah 9:17; Psalm 86:15; 103:8; Joel 2:13; Jonah 4:2). On 34:7b, the study reads it with Exodus 20:5 ("those who hate Me") and Ezekiel 18:20 as describing the spread of sin's consequences, not punishment of the innocent; this is the common evangelical reading.
- **Jewish practice:** the Thirteen Attributes of Mercy in festival, fast-day and *Selichot* prayers (to be checked).
- **Links:** Jonah's home is Reading the Bible well (referred to only). Covenants (26) owns Exodus 19–24.

### Deuteronomy 10:12–22 with Psalm 96 (part 04)
- **Context:** Moses' sermon after the renewal of the tablets (Deuteronomy 10:1–5). Psalm 96, a call to the nations, also found in 1 Chronicles 16:23–33.
- **Main readings:** God's greatness defined by impartial justice and care for the vulnerable; Psalm 96's judgment as cause for joy.
- **Links:** Every life in God's image (62), which quotes 10:17–19.

### 1 John 4:7–21 (part 05)
- **Context:** John's letter, after the test of spirits (4:1–6, owned by 06).
- **Main readings:** "God is love" defined by the sending of the Son; "atoning sacrifice" (traditionally "propitiation") as the turning aside of judgment; the site's reading follows article IV (salvation). 4:18 read as fear of judgment, not general anxiety.
- **Links:** The cross, the empty tomb and the throne (25), which quotes 4:9–10; Fear, anxiety and despair (56); Learning to discern (06) for 4:1–6.

### Psalm 33:6–9 and Isaiah 40:12–31 (part 06)
- **Context:** Psalm 33, a hymn of praise to the Creator. Isaiah 40, the opening of the comfort chapters to exiles.
- **Main readings:** creation by word; creation out of nothing (with Hebrews 11:3 quoted; Westminster Shorter Catechism Q9 and the Baptist Catechism cited). Isaiah 40's incomparability of God against idols, turning to pastoral comfort in 40:27–31. Romans 11:34 quotes Isaiah 40:13 (shown in part 10).
- **Open question shown:** age of the earth and days of creation; confession silent; views listed, not chosen.
- **Quotes:** Genesis 1:1 and Hebrews 11:3 (home: 50); Colossians 1:16–17 (home: 11).
- **Links:** Genesis 1–11: the questions readers bring (50); Who do you say that I am? (11); apologetics answers on why anything exists and on science.

### Acts 17:22–31 (part 07)
- **Context:** Paul in Athens on his second journey; the Bereans (17:10–12) are owned by 06.
- **Main readings:** Paul's use of creation and Greek poets as common ground; the call to repent; judgment through the risen Jesus. The quotations from Aratus and (possibly) Epimenides as commonly identified, marked for checking.
- **Links:** 50 quotes 17:26; Trinity answer for Muslim neighbours; The good news (54) in the close.

### Psalm 104 and Matthew 10:29–31 (part 08)
- **Context:** Psalm 104, a creation hymn broadly following the order of Genesis 1. Matthew 10, Jesus sending the Twelve, with warnings of persecution.
- **Main readings:** providence as God's working through ordinary processes (104:21, 27–30); 104:35 as a prayer for the world put right. Matthew 10:29 as God's knowledge and will over all events, within a context that expects suffering. Hebrews 1:7 quotes Psalm 104:4 of angels (not mentioned in the text, to save space; editor may add a link to 53). Luke 12:6 has the parallel.
- **Sources:** Baptist Faith and Message article II (God the Father); Heidelberg Catechism Q27 (Reformed, labelled).
- **Links:** Fear, anxiety and despair (56) by theme.

### Genesis 50:15–21 (part 09)
- **Context:** After Jacob's death and burial (50:1–14, owned by Grief and dying, 57).
- **Main readings:** two intentions in one event (human evil, divine good); human responsibility kept. Romans 8:28 quoted (home: Chosen and kept, 58). Calvinist and non-Calvinist Baptist explanations of providence and free choice named without choosing.
- **Care:** crisis lines placed first; abuse and forgiveness note.
- **Links:** Chosen and kept (58); Grief and dying (57) for 50:1–14.

### Romans 11:33–36 (part 10)
- **Context:** The close of Romans 9–11 on Israel, the nations and mercy.
- **Main readings:** praise of God's unsearchable wisdom; quotations of Isaiah 40:13 and an echo of Job 41:11; 11:36 as the summary of creation, providence and purpose.
- **Care:** no verdict on the Jewish people; 11:29 referred to.
