# Knowing God

**Content pass status:** item 21, first full draft ready for review, 2026-10-13. [Content draft](content.md); [passage and editorial notes](passage-notes.md).

## Purpose and reader scope

Connect what Scripture calls God with his actions and the response of those addressed. This introduces the God and Christian Faith category through sustained passages, not a complete systematic catalogue of attributes or a comparative history of doctrine.

## Content divisions

1. I AM hath sent me unto you — God's initiative, name, presence, and deliverance.
2. Holy, holy, holy — Isaiah's encounter, cleansing, and difficult commission.
3. Merciful and gracious — the proclaimed name, forgiveness, accountability, and Moses' prayer.
4. He doth execute the judgment — impartial care and the hope of righteous judgment.
5. O the depth of the riches — wisdom, mercy, limits of understanding, and praise.
6. He first loved us — the sending of the Son and love of the brother.

Two subdivisions per section are content organisation only. Preserve the accepted outline in `../ideas.md`. No design or infrastructure decisions are made here.
