# The Holy Spirit

**Content pass status:** item 23, first full draft ready for review, 2026-10-13. [Content draft](content.md); [passage and editorial notes](passage-notes.md).

## Purpose and scope

Trace the Spirit's work through Jesus' promise, Pentecost, new birth, adoption, gifts, fruit, and conduct. Attribute selected disputed interpretations; do not let those disputes displace the passages' sustained arguments.

## Content divisions

1. The promise of the Holy Ghost — John 14 and Peter's explanation at Pentecost.
2. Born of the Spirit — Nicodemus, necessity of birth, water and Spirit, wind comparison.
3. The Spirit of adoption — indwelling, assurance, suffering, hope, and prayer.
4. Diversities of gifts, but the same Spirit — body, love, edification, order, and attributed disagreements.
5. The fruit of the Spirit — liberty, relational conflict, works and fruit.
6. Let us also walk in the Spirit — restoration, burdens, responsibility, and perseverance.

Each section has two subdivisions. These are content divisions, not website specifications. Preserve the outline in `../ideas.md`.
