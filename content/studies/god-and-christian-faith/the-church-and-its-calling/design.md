## Direction

Teach one habit: before you ask what a line says to you, ask who was in the room. The picture is one word in four sizes: every use of church or churches in the Berean New Testament, on four rings from a house to all who are Christ's. Only one direction was built, as with the earlier mock-ups of existing drafts.

## The page

- **Opening.** Study trail, the three concepts, "The church" and "and its calling" in italic, the lead, 1 Peter 2:4–5, a thin-line wall of stones with its top course open and one stone on its way, the motto, a pause control and the figures.
- **01 One word, four sizes.** The signature. 111 points on four rings, each coloured by its part of the New Testament, hollow where the ring is a close call. Four buttons choose a ring and show every verse on it as a list, with the close calls and why. That list is the full path on a phone. Pointing or arrowing along the points shows each verse in Bible order, and choosing one opens it on the sheet.
- **02 Six readings, and who was listening.** A cabinet of six small drawings. The chosen reading has a tinted plate beside it, "Who was in the room", with two short phrases from the passages in their own words.
- **03 Ten baptisms, read down the water.** One line of water with what the passage names first on the left and the baptism on the right. Lydia's household has a hollow point and a "Left open" note. On a phone the line moves to the left edge. The twelve other verses in Acts that use the word are listed closed beneath.
- **04 Six phrases, all of them older.** Six phrases of 1 Peter 2:9–10 as a row. One older verse opens beside the chosen phrase, with a plain statement of how close the wording is.
- **05 Three lines, tried in their rooms.** Matthew 18:20, 1 Corinthians 3:16 and Philippians 1:6, each with how it is often read alone and its answer closed, so the reader tries the habit first.
- **06 Six questions, faced.** A plain ruled list. Each opens the one sheet from the right, with the passage, then this study's reading, then what others have taught, labelled as theirs.
- **Close.** The way of reading, the invitation, the question, "Read the passage again before you answer" with 1 Peter 2:4–10, the way back to God & Christian Faith, one course of stones, and the sources.

## Choices made, and why

- **A counted word, not a concordance of ideas.** The rings count one English word as the Berean prints it, and the page says so and names "assembly" and "congregation" as not counted. A count that can be run again is worth more here than a wider one that cannot.
- **Hollow points for close calls.** Twelve uses in eleven verses could sit on another ring, and each says why. Marking them keeps the judgment in plain view.
- **The rings are a drawing; the list is the content.** At phone width the points are too close to choose, so the buttons and written list carry all the meaning, and nothing depends on colour.
- **Colour.** One page colour, the colour Acts has on this site. Only the points use the four fixed section colours.
- **The room plate comes before the text.** It is the habit, repeated six times in the same place, so the reader is doing it by the third reading.
- **Household baptism left open.** Lydia's verse says nothing about faith or age, and the page shows the hollow point and says that, while saying why Baptists baptize believers.
- **No face and no gathered figures.** Illustrative forms only, captioned as this study's.
