# The church and its calling passage and editorial notes

Working notes for [the reader-facing text](content.md). Rewritten 2026-10-30 from the Berean Standard Bible for the mock-up (the King James draft of 2026-10-13 is in git history). Built page: [the mock-up and its record](../../../../design/the-church-and-its-calling-directions/README.md).

## Source basis

Every passage was read in the site's Berean files, [data/plain/bsb/](../../../../data/plain/bsb/). The build checks every block quotation and every quoted phrase against the reference that belongs to it. That proves the words are there. It does not prove a reading is right. No Greek or Hebrew was compared.

## The rings

Every use of church or churches in the Berean New Testament: 111 uses in 108 verses (three verses use it twice: Matthew 18:17, 1 Corinthians 16:19, Revelation 1:20). The build proves the list equals a scan of the text.

| Ring | Uses | Rule followed | What the script enforces |
|---|---|---|---|
| A house | 4 | A church that meets at someone's house | Exactly the uses in those words |
| A place | 51 | One people in a town, or one congregation gathered or spoken of | Nothing beyond the rest being covered |
| Many places | 38 | Many congregations counted together | The plural, or one of three named singular uses (Acts 9:31, Acts 14:23, 1 Corinthians 4:17) |
| All who are Christ's | 18 | Christ's church, not any one town | Nothing. 11 of the 18 are in Ephesians and Colossians, and that count is computed |

Twelve uses in eleven verses are marked close calls, and each says why, in [study_lists.py](../../../../design/the-church-and-its-calling-directions/study_lists.py). The hardest are Acts 9:31, 1 Timothy 3:15 and the three verses on Paul harming the church, which name no town and were clearly felt in particular towns.

## The ten baptisms

Counted: each time Acts says a person or a group was baptized in Jesus' name, or tells it in the course of events: Acts 2:41, 8:12, 8:13, 8:38, 9:18, 10:48, 16:15, 16:33, 18:8, 19:5. Not counted: commands, John's baptism and retellings. The build proves every verse in Acts with baptize or baptism is either used or on the named list of twelve others. Saul's "he is praying" (9:11) and the Ethiopian's "told him the good news" (8:35) come from nearby verses, and choosing them is this study's judgment. Acts 8:37 is not in the Berean's numbered text.

## Interpretive limits

1. **Body:** 1 Corinthians 12 does not make all gifts identical. Whether tongues and prophecy continue is left open and to the planned study of the Holy Spirit, because the Baptist Faith and Message does not settle it.
2. **Ministry:** Ephesians 4 is read for its stated purpose of equipping. No modern staffing plan is drawn from the list.
3. **Household and temple:** these images refer to people joined in Christ. That is no judgment on buildings.
4. **Gathering:** 1 Corinthians 14's order is not equated with one modern service. How 14:34–35 sits with 11:5 is flagged open in the question on pastors.
5. **Office:** the "one office" reading follows Acts 20:17 and 28 and 1 Peter 5:1–2, and is Baptist teaching. Barnes, a Presbyterian, draws the same conclusion about elder and bishop. 1 Timothy 3:11 is not taken up.
6. **Who may be a pastor:** the page holds with Article 6, and says that other churches, some called Baptist, ordain women and that their reasons are not surveyed.
7. **Non-domination:** a title never excuses mistreatment. The care note says a crime belongs with the authorities.
8. **Baptism:** the page holds with believer's immersion and says plainly that no household verse settles infants for either side. Westminster, Barnes, and Jamieson, Fausset and Brown are shown on that.
9. **Does baptism save:** Acts 2:38, Acts 22:16 and 1 Peter 3:21 are quoted in full phrases, and Luther's answer is shown as his church's. The Baptist reading is labelled as Baptist.
10. **Supper:** remembrance is what Article 7 says. The page reports that Baptists differ on the Lord's nearness, without choosing. "Without recognizing the body" is shown with Barnes's reading and with this study's, and no writer holding the second was read for this page.
11. **Acts 1:8:** the promise was first spoken to those disciples. The page says the church since has taken the task as its own, with Matthew 28:19.
12. **Israel and the church:** the six phrases are said of Israel first, and the page does not choose among Baptist maps of how Israel and the church relate.
13. **The rooms:** "who was in the room" is the study's habit, drawn from each letter's own address. It does not deny that these lines also reach each believer. The page says so on Philippians 1:6, and the close says so too.

## Pairings

The older verses in the section on six phrases are this study's. Peter gives no chapter and verse. Two are close in wording (Isaiah 43:20, Exodus 19:6 for "a holy nation"), one shares a single word (Exodus 19:5), one is close in other words (Exodus 19:6 for priests), and two are echoes the page calls this study's pairing (Isaiah 43:21, Hosea 2:23). Older verses are not claimed to be the earliest uses.
