# The church and its calling working notes

**Item 28 · God and Christian Faith · First draft · 2026-10-13**

## Purpose and central question

What kind of people does the New Testament describe the church as being, and what shared responsibilities follow? Keep corporate identity and practices central; detailed interpersonal work remains in Life together.

## Content divisions

1. One body and many members — belonging, mutual need, Christ the head and equipping for ministry.
2. Of the household of God — shared access, household, temple, living stones and mercy.
3. Let all things be done unto edifying — intelligibility, participation, judgment and order in gatherings.
4. Feed the flock of God — character, teaching, care, example and differing forms of government.
5. Baptizing and remembering — commission, baptism, shared meal, self-examination and attributed differences.
6. Shew forth his praises — identity, conduct, proclamation and the Spirit-empowered witness in Acts.

Each has two subdivisions, readings, a consideration and a reflection. These are content divisions rather than a proposed site design.

## Boundaries

[Content draft](content.md) and [passage notes](passage-notes.md) are separate from planning. The Baptist and Westminster comparisons identify particular accounts rather than resolve all church-order or sacramental questions. No full survey of Catholic, Orthodox, Anglican, Lutheran or other positions is claimed.

Leadership qualifications and non-domination remain concrete. Women's offices and speech, continuing gifts, sacramental presence and church authority require dedicated comparison if expanded. The draft names relevant limits without turning its central purpose into a catalogue of controversies.
