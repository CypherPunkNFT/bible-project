# The church and its calling

**Status:** study 28, rewritten in the Berean with a mock-up for the owner's review, 2026-10-30. [Study text](content.md); [passage and editorial notes](passage-notes.md); [design](design.md); [the mock-up and its record](../../../../design/the-church-and-its-calling-directions/README.md).

## Purpose and central question

Help a general reader see what kind of people the New Testament calls the church, and what they are given to do together. Who was in the room when these lines were read? Detailed interpersonal work stays in Life together.

## Content divisions

The six readings of the 2026-10-13 draft stay, in the draft's order.

1. One body, many parts: belonging and mutual need; equipping for ministry. 1 Corinthians 12:12–31; Ephesians 4:1–16.
2. Members of God's household: no longer strangers; living stones. Ephesians 2:11–22; 1 Peter 2:4–10.
3. Everything in order: understanding and edification; order in the gathering. 1 Corinthians 14:1–19, 26–40; 1 Corinthians 11:17–22.
4. Be shepherds of God's flock: character; one office and God's own flock. 1 Timothy 3:1–15; 1 Peter 5:1–4; Acts 20:17–28.
5. Water and bread: baptism in the commission; the Supper in a divided meal. Matthew 28:16–20; 1 Corinthians 11:17–34.
6. Proclaim the virtues: conduct and speech; the disciples' task. 1 Peter 2:9–12; Acts 1:6–8.

Added around them: one word in four sizes, ten baptisms, six phrases beside older verses, three lines tried in their rooms, six hard questions, and a close with the way of reading, an invitation and the passage again.

## Boundaries

Continuing gifts belong to the planned study of the Holy Spirit. How Israel and the church relate is left to Covenants and promises. Other traditions are named only where a question needs them, each labelled as theirs, and no full survey is claimed.
