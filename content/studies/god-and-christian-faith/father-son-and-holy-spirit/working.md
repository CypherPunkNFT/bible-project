## Purpose and scope

Read the biblical passages before comparing doctrinal formulations. Distinguish quotations, contextual observations, and synthesis. The final section gives one explicit Trinitarian formulation and two named alternatives, using their own institutional statements; it is not an exhaustive history or adjudication of every dispute.

## Content divisions

1. The LORD our God is one LORD — confession, allegiance, one God and one Lord.
2. Shew us the Father — revelation, sending, prayer, glory, and the Father's greatness.
3. The Word was made flesh — John’s opening, creation, incarnation, and declaring God.
4. Another Comforter — sending, remembrance, witness, guidance, glorifying the Son.
5. In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost — baptism narrative, commission, apostolic blessing.
6. Bringing the passages together — explanatory vocabulary and attributed differences.
