# The hope to come working notes

**Item 30 · God and Christian Faith · Rewritten to the study standard · 2026-10-30.** The first draft of 2026-10-13, in the King James, is in git history.

## Purpose and central question

What is promised about the end, what does it ask of you now, and what does it leave open? Read each passage for what it plainly says before asking how it fits a chart. The [reader-facing text](content.md) is the source of the [mock-up](../../../../design/the-hope-to-come-directions/README.md); its [design](design.md) and [passage notes](passage-notes.md) sit beside it.

## Content divisions

1. The return: always with the Lord.
2. The body: sown and raised.
3. The judgment: the books were opened.
4. The creation: set free.
5. The new creation: God with them.
6. The waiting: what kind of people.

Then "One hope. Several maps." for the order of events, and a close with the gospel invitation.

## What changed from the first draft

- King James to Berean, every verse.
- Dallas Seminary and Westminster as the two examples gave way to Baptist maps, because the site speaks under the Baptist Faith and Message: Criswell, Piper and Akin, Waldron, the golden-age view through its critics, and Boyce.
- Hell is now stated plainly under Article 10, where the draft declined to resolve it. Pinnock's view stands beside it, labelled as not the site's.
- Added Acts 1:9–11, Matthew 25:31–46, John 5:24–29, Isaiah 65:17–19, and the note on the intermediate state from 2 Corinthians 5:8 and Philippians 1:23.
- Added a direct gospel invitation at the close.

## Boundaries and reuse

This study owns the teaching on Christ's return. Draft 20 keeps 1 Thessalonians only as a short reading centred on Christ, to be cut when it is designed; it was not edited here. Draft 19 owns the witness to Christ's resurrection, and this study the future body. Faith under pressure keeps the longer walk through 1 Thessalonians 4 for those who are grieving. Who God saves stays with the study of grace. The angels and spiritual powers need a study of their own.
