## Direction

Teach one habit: do not start with the chart. Ask of any passage about the end what is promised, what it asks of you now and what it leaves open. Only one direction was built, as with the three earlier mock-ups, because this was an existing draft.

## The page

- **Opening.** Study trail, the three questions in gold, the title *The hope to come*, the lead, Revelation 21:5, and a drawing of an empty tomb, a throne, a tree and a city on one dotted thread. No face or figure of God or of Christ. Below it, five computed figures, each with what it means.
- **01 Six readings, three questions each.** Return, body, judgment, creation, new creation, waiting, as six cards. A card opens its reading in place and in the address. Each has the passage first, its setting, the three questions, its parts, flagged notes, and Look closely and Your response.
- **02 The promise, then the therefore.** A real table, which stacks on a phone: a promise, the joining word from the verse, and the call. The page says the rows were chosen for the pattern, so they cannot prove it is everywhere, and names two passages without it.
- **03 What was lost, what Revelation answers.** Eleven rows from the first pages through the prophets to Revelation. A solid line means the same English word is in both end verses; a dotted line means this study's matching. So the kind of link is carried by the line and the words, not by colour.
- **04 One hope, several maps.** Four ordered lists lined up on the return, so a reader can see at once whether the thousand years come before or after it. On a phone each runs down the page. Each map has who holds it. None is chosen. Then where Boyce stood, and what no map disputes.
- **Close.** The way of reading, the invitation, a question for the reader's own life, the passage again, a back link, a landscape, and the sources with how far each was read.

## Doctrine and care

The site's voice follows the Baptist Faith and Message 2000, Articles 4 and 10. The return, the raising of the dead, the judgment of every person, Hell and life with God are stated plainly. The order of events is not, because the confession does not settle it. No dates and no current events. The judgment passage is not used to name who is lost.
