# Covenants and promises working notes

**Item 26 · God and Christian Faith · Rewritten to the study standard · 2026-10-30.** The first draft of 2026-10-13, in the King James, is in git history.

## Purpose and central question

Whose is this promise, and what does it actually say? Read each covenant where it was first spoken, then follow it forward. The [reader-facing text](content.md) is the source of the [mock-up](../../../../design/covenants-and-promises-directions/README.md); its [design](design.md) and [passage notes](passage-notes.md) sit beside it.

## Content divisions

1. Noah: every living creature.
2. Abraham: a family for the world.
3. Sinai: rescue first.
4. David: a house for a house.
5. The new covenant: on the heart.
6. Heirs: the letters read them.

Then "One text. Several maps." for how they fit together, and a close.

## What changed from the first draft

- King James to Berean, every verse.
- The Dallas Seminary statement and the Westminster-led comparison gave way to Baptist maps, with Westminster labelled as another tradition, because the site speaks under the Baptist Faith and Message.
- Added Psalm 89:38–39 (the psalm's lament), Jeremiah 34:18 on the walk between the pieces, and the case for believer's baptism from Genesis 17:12 and Jeremiah 31:34.
- The site now states its reading of Genesis 15, and reports Calvin's and Maclaren's different readings beside it.

## Boundaries and reuse

Baptism, the church's identity and end-times fulfilment get fuller attention in later studies. Sacrifice and justification stay with [What the cross accomplishes](../what-the-cross-accomplishes/content.md) and [Grace faith and salvation](../grace-faith-and-salvation/content.md). No study here claims to catalogue every covenant.
