# Master list of study ideas

This is the accepted development queue for the five Scripture and Theology categories. The owner authorized starting item 01 on 2026-10-13. Acceptance of the queue does not make individual drafts final copy.

## Reading and development order

Start with **A new creation**, then work through Christian Life, Jesus Christ, and God and Christian Faith. Explore the Bible and People of Scripture complete the neighbouring category inventory and come afterward. This is a proposed work order, not a change to the site category order.

Numbers identify the same study during both passes. Keep them stable after acceptance; record additions or removals rather than silently renumbering completed work.

## Overlap to manage

- **Jesus Christ** follows the person, narratives, teaching and witness about Jesus. **God and Christian Faith** develops doctrinal questions and how passages are brought together. **Christian Life** develops the resulting practice.
- **The cross and resurrection** follows the events and witness. **What the cross accomplishes** develops the theological language of salvation.
- **The Holy Spirit** studies the Spirit’s person and work. **A new creation** and **Growing in maturity** develop the life those passages call believers to live.
- **Life together** develops relationships; **The church and its calling** develops the community’s identity and shared practices.
- Existing Gospels, miracles, letters, names, people and Long Memory content should be inventoried and reused. Inclusion here is not a request for duplicate website features.
