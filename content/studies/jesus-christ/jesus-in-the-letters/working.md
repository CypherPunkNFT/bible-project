# Jesus in the letters

**Content pass status:** item 20, first full draft ready for review, 2026-10-13. [Content draft](content.md); [passage and editorial notes](passage-notes.md).

## Purpose and scope

Explore Christ’s person, work, and continuing significance in selected epistle arguments. Keep descriptions of Christ connected to the communities’ instruction and comfort. This is not an exhaustive collection of every reference to Jesus in every letter.

## Content divisions

1. He is before all things — creation, preeminence, God’s speech through the Son.
2. Let this mind be in you — incarnation, service, obedience, exaltation, shared humanity.
3. Having made peace through the blood of his cross — reconciliation and continuing in gospel hope.
4. We have a great high priest — sympathy, sinlessness, appointment, and approaching for help.
5. Who also maketh intercession for us — Christ’s present ministry and assurance amid suffering.
6. So shall we ever be with the Lord — the dead in Christ, coming, comfort, and watchfulness.

Two subdivisions per section organise the content; no website presentation is specified. The accepted outline is retained in `../ideas.md`.
