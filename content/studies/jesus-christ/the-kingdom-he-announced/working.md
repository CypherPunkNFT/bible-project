# The kingdom he announced

**Content pass status:** item 15, first full draft ready for review, 2026-10-13. [Content draft](content.md); [passage and editorial notes](passage-notes.md).

## Purpose and central question

Explore Jesus’ proclamation of the kingdom through announcement, reception, parables, present signs, wealth and status, and future expectation. What does each passage announce, reveal, or call its hearers to do?

## Content divisions

1. The kingdom of God is at hand — announcement and the first followers.
2. Receive the kingdom — children and the conversation about new birth.
3. The kingdom of heaven is like — hearing, growth, worth, and judgment.
4. The kingdom of God is come upon you — deliverance and the disputed source of Jesus’ works.
5. How hardly shall they that have riches enter — self-exaltation, mercy, possessions, and following.
6. Watch therefore — readiness, entrusted goods, and the King’s judgment.

Each section has two content divisions. They organise the argument, not a committed card or screen layout. The accepted outline remains in `../ideas.md`.
