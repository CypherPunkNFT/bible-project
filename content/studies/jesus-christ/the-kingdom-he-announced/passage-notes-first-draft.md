## Existing material and study boundaries

`src/data/parables.ts` already contains the mustard seed, leaven, treasure, pearl, and ten virgins among its entries. Its leaven entry cautions against imposing one fixed symbolic meaning everywhere; its treasure and pearl entries distinguish their costly responses from purchasing salvation. Preserve these useful distinctions. This draft selects comparisons for a kingdom study rather than replacing or duplicating that catalogue.

Item 14 has already developed individual encounters. Here Luke 18 is organised around receiving, standing, wealth, and entry. Item 16 develops following Jesus in greater depth; item 17 develops his teaching; item 18 develops signs and works. Keep this study’s central question the kingdom rather than reproducing each neighbouring study.

## Interpretive questions retained for review

- The draft uses present kingdom sayings and future judgment sayings together. It does not settle every eschatological system or translate them into a proposed political programme.
- Matthew’s “kingdom of heaven” and Mark/Luke’s “kingdom of God” remain in their respective wording. The draft does not infer two unrelated kingdoms merely from the different phrases.
- John 3:5 and baptism: traditions differ over the water-and-Spirit saying. The draft retains the wording and need for new birth without resolving sacramental interpretation.
- Luke’s children: avoid invented innocence doctrines or presenting this scene alone as settling infant baptism.
- Matthew 13: leaven and growth have received differing readings. The draft follows the comparison’s stated movement and neighbouring seed image; it does not make growth an unrestricted prediction about institutions or human progress.
- Luke 18: no speculative “needle gate” explanation. Preserve the difficulty of entry and Jesus’ explicit answer about God’s possibility. Do not invent the ruler’s later fate.
- Matthew 25:40: “my brethren” has narrower disciple-focused and broader readings. The draft identifies the question instead of declaring consensus. A detailed treatment would need the language of brethren and reception elsewhere in Matthew.
- The talents are money within the story. Broader applications to responsibility are labelled applications. They are not an investment strategy or a promise of financial success.
- Matthew 25’s judgment should not become either a formula for buying salvation through works or a scene in which concrete acts of mercy no longer matter. Preserve the text before attempting a wider doctrinal synthesis.
