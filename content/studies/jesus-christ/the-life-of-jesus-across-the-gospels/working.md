# The life of Jesus across the Gospels

**Content pass status:** item 13, first full draft ready for review, 2026-10-13. [Content draft](content.md); [passage and editorial notes](passage-notes.md).

## Purpose and central question

Follow broad movements of Jesus' life through the four accounts while distinguishing shared events, distinct selections and uncertain chronology. How does reading across accounts enrich attention to each Gospel without replacing them with a reconstructed fifth account?

## Content divisions

1. Birth and beginnings.
2. Baptism and wilderness.
3. Galilee.
4. The journey to Jerusalem.
5. The final week.
6. Resurrection appearances.

All six proposed themes are retained. The existing Gospel portraits and harmony were inventoried; their observations on baptism, temptation order, entry, anointing, burial and the empty tomb inform this overview. No duplicate event catalogue is created.
