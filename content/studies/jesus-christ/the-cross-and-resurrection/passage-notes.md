## Editorial and interpretive decisions

- **Meal wording:** retain the KJV’s “new testament.” Detailed sacramental interpretations and Passover-calendar reconciliation are outside this draft. Reading Mark and Luke together does not licence rewriting either quotation.
- **Gethsemane:** preserve anguish and submission without speculative psychology. Applications concern watchfulness and prayer; Jesus’ particular submission is not a blanket instruction to comply with another person’s harmful demands.
- **Responsibility:** describe the specific council, accusers, crowd, Pilate and soldiers in the narrative. No collective or inherited blame against Jews is supported by this retelling.
- **Cross sayings:** no composite sequence of last sayings is asserted. The draft neither supplies a medical reconstruction of death nor claims that one isolated saying exhausts the meaning of the cross.
- **Fulfilment:** John explicitly links events to Scripture. This draft does not add unverified claims about word meanings or identify every possible allusion as an explicit citation.
- **Tomb comparison:** Mark names a young man, Luke two men in shining garments, and John later two angels in Mary’s encounter. Do not harmonise number, movement, or timing by inventing scenes. The draft states each selected account’s details where used.
- **Mark scope:** the selected tomb reading ends at 16:8. This is a reading boundary, not a claim that the local KJV ends there. Appearances are developed from Luke and John. If Mark 16:9–20 is added in a later revision, document its textual-history question with appropriate sources rather than silently assuming all editions present it identically.
- **Emmaus:** Luke says Jesus expounds Scripture but does not list all texts. Suggested Old Testament connections must be labelled suggestions, not the recovered sermon.
- **Thomas:** John reports Jesus’ invitation and Thomas’s confession, not explicitly his touching the wounds. Do not illustrate or narrate touching as though John states it.
- **Appearances:** Paul’s list is his reported testimony, not an independently established roster produced by this study. No unsupported dating claim, interview reconstruction, or claim to have proved each witness independently is made.
- **Resurrection meaning:** Paul’s argument includes sins, testimony, those who died, and future resurrection. Preserve all of these rather than treating resurrection only as continued influence or inward renewal.
- **Firstfruits:** read 15:22 with the order in 15:23 and following. Detailed debates about the scope of “all,” final judgment, and kingdom chronology belong to fuller doctrinal treatment; no timetable is imposed here.
