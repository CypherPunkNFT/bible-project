# The cross and resurrection

**Content pass status:** item 19, first full draft ready for review, 2026-10-13. [Content draft](content.md); [passage and editorial notes](passage-notes.md).

## Purpose and reader scope

Follow the death and resurrection accounts and their interpretation. Distinguish narrated actions, witness reports, Jesus’ explanations, and apostolic teaching while showing their connections. This is a selected reading study, not a reconstructed exhaustive chronology or an apologetics proof catalogue.

## Content divisions

1. This is my body — Passover, betrayal, body and blood, remembrance and promise.
2. Nevertheless not what I will — Gethsemane, prayer, sleeping disciples, arrest and flight.
3. The King of the Jews — accusations, condemnation, mockery, crucifixion and death.
4. He is risen; he is not here — burial, observed location, discovery and announcement.
5. It is I myself — Emmaus, gathered disciples, Mary, Thomas, Scripture and witness.
6. According to the scriptures — received gospel, appearances, grace, resurrection and future hope.

Two subdivisions per section organise the content. They are not approved website components. The original outline remains in `../ideas.md`.
