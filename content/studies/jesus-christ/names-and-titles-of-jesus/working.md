# Names and titles of Jesus

**Content pass status:** item 12, first full draft ready for review, 2026-10-13. [Content draft](content.md); [passage and editorial notes](passage-notes.md).

## Purpose and central question

Study what Jesus is called, by whom and in what setting. How do the surrounding words and narrative develop a title's significance without making all uses interchangeable?

## Content divisions

1. Messiah and Christ — John's explicit equivalence and the mission following Peter's confession.
2. Son of God — announcement, witness, kingship and relationship to the Father.
3. Son of Man — authority, suffering, service and scriptural echoes of glory.
4. Lord — Thomas's confession and Philippians' movement through humility to exaltation.
5. Lamb of God — testimony, redemption and the slain Lamb honoured in Revelation.
6. Word and image — distinct descriptions in John and Colossians.

All six outline themes are retained. A short introduction also covers Jesus and Emmanuel through Matthew's own explanation. This is a selective study, not an exhaustive title dictionary or new site catalogue.
