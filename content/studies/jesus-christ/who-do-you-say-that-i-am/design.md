## Direction

Written for someone who does not yet believe. The Gospels kept what people said about Jesus, friendly and hostile, and He asked a question that has a blank in it. So the page shows the answers, leaves the blank open, and says plainly where this site stands. A reader can go through all of it without agreeing to anything.

## The page

- **Opening.** Study trail, the three concepts, the title, the lead, Matthew 16:15, and a ring: one tick for each line on the wall, sorted into its groups, with one gap and a ruled blank at the centre. A slow dot goes round it, and a visible button pauses it. The motto, the figures, where this page stands and what it selected.
- **01 The answers, and a blank.** First the way in, from Matthew 16:13–15. Then the signature: 47 lines under ten kinds of speaker, as serif type on fine rules. How a line was said is in the type: upright if stated, italic if asked, small capitals if thrown. Pointing or tabbing tries a line in the blank; choosing keeps it, and Read it opens the passage with who said it and what came next. Eight words light the lines that use them, hide none, and draw a fine rule down the margin. Then three pairs of the same words in two mouths.
- **02 Six scenes, read whole.** Six ruled entries that open where they stand, each with a form from its own text: three accounts side by side; the steps from "Blessed" to "Get behind Me"; the council's record beside the witnesses of John 5; the voice's two sentences word above word; Thomas' terms against Jesus' words; John's answers in order, ending on an empty stop.
- **03 The answer that changed.** Six rows, read across: first, between, after. Then words that were already old, each saying who draws the link.
- **04 Five questions, faced,** on the one sheet.
- **Close.** What was read, the way of reading, the invitation, the question, a ruled blank that is not a form, the passage again, links, a drawing of a road going into hills, and the sources.

## Choices made, and why

- **The title is the Berean's.** The listed title is not the Berean's wording. The page uses the Berean; the list, folder and hub keep the old title until the owner decides.
- **The blank is never filled for the reader.** It does not cycle, and nothing but the reader's own pointing puts words in it.
- **The blank at the end is a ruled line, and not a form.** Nothing can be typed and nothing is kept, so no one's answer about Jesus sits in a database.
- **The site says where it stands, at the start and at the end.** A seeker should not find out at the end that the page had a view. The invitation is plain, and Article 17 stands beside Article 4.
- **A question is not a claim.** The Son of God figure says how many state it. Asked and dared lines are shown as asked and dared.
- **Hostile words are kept, in their speakers' mouths.** "He is out of His mind" stands on the wall. The care note says a line is what its speaker said, and not what this page says.
- **Nothing is hidden by a word.** The others fade, so a reader still sees who did not use it.
- **No faces and no people drawn.**
- **Unlike the sibling studies.** Encounters with Jesus is a plate of meetings, and The signs and works of Jesus is cords on a brown band. This is type on rules, with no cards and no pills.
