## Purpose and central question

Written for someone who does not yet believe. People who met Jesus said many things about Him, and He asked a question nobody else can answer for you. Who is speaking, what exactly do they say, how does He answer, and what do they do next?

## Content divisions

1. The question on the road: what people say, and what you say.
2. Blessed, and then rebuked: the right title, and what it did not yet mean.
3. Before the council: what He said when it would cost Him His life, and the witnesses of John 5.
4. The voice from heaven: the same sentence twice, and three words more.
5. Thomas sets his terms: a man who refused, and what he was offered.
6. Written for you: the Gospel that says it was written so that you may believe.

All six units of the first draft are kept. Added for the seeker: the wall of answers, three pairs of the same words in two mouths, people who changed their answer, and five hard questions: whether He was simply a great teacher, whether He said He was God, who the rock is, whether writers who meant to persuade can be trusted, and what to do if you do not know what you think yet.
