# Who do you say that I am

**Content pass status:** item 11, first full draft ready for review, 2026-10-13. [Content draft](content.md); [passage and editorial notes](passage-notes.md).

## Purpose and central question

Explore questions and claims about Jesus' identity through named speakers and narrative settings. Who identifies Jesus, what is said, how does he respond, and how does each Gospel direct its reader?

## Content divisions

1. The question to the disciples — public opinions and the disciples' answer.
2. Peter's confession — recognition, further instruction and the path of suffering.
3. Jesus before his opponents — Mark's council scene and John 5's discourse.
4. The Father's testimony — baptism, transfiguration and listening to the Son.
5. Thomas's confession — the risen Jesus, testimony and belief.
6. The purpose of John's Gospel — selected signs, identity and life through his name.

All six proposed units are retained. Titles receive only the explanation necessary for these scenes; their fuller treatment belongs to item 12. The reading is comparative, not a merged Gospel transcript.
