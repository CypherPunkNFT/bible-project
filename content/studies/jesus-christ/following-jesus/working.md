# Following Jesus

**Content pass status:** item 16, first full draft ready for review, 2026-10-13. [Content draft](content.md); [passage and editorial notes](passage-notes.md).

## Purpose and reader scope

Study what Jesus asks of those who follow him, keeping cost, dependence, grace, and community together. Readers should distinguish the first disciples’ circumstances from subsequent applications without dismissing the force of Jesus’ commands.

## Content divisions

1. Come ye after me — the fishermen, Levi, and Jesus’ initiative.
2. Take up his cross — Peter’s confession and correction; allegiance for Jesus’ and the gospel’s sake.
3. Counteth the cost — the multitudes, family and possessions, tower and king.
4. Learn of me — revelation, invitation, rest, and Jesus’ character.
5. Abide in me — dependence, fruitfulness, love, and friendship.
6. Teaching them to observe — the eleven, all nations, baptism, teaching, and promised presence.

Each division has two subsections. These are content boundaries, not approved interface or layout decisions. The original accepted outline remains in `../ideas.md`.
