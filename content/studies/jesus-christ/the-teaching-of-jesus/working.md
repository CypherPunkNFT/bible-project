# The teaching of Jesus

**Content pass status:** item 17, first full draft ready for review, 2026-10-13. [Content draft](content.md); [passage and editorial notes](passage-notes.md).

## Purpose and reader scope

Explore the arguments and settings of Jesus’ teaching through sustained reading units. Ask who listens, what prompts the teaching, what reasons Jesus gives, and what response follows. This is a selected introduction, not an exhaustive commentary on every saying.

## Content divisions

1. He opened his mouth, and taught them — the Sermon on the Mount’s setting, blessings, fulfilment, and movement.
2. After this manner therefore pray ye — audience, the Father’s knowledge, shared petitions, forgiveness, and asking.
3. Love your enemies — active mercy, judgment, forgiveness, and correcting hypocrisy.
4. A man’s life consisteth not in the abundance — inheritance dispute, rich fool, provision, kingdom, generosity.
5. Whosoever will be great among you — requested seats, cup and baptism, service, and ransom.
6. Heareth these sayings of mine, and doeth them — profession, fruit, the two builders, judgment, and authority.

Each has two subdivisions, reading references, a question, and an application. These are content units, not approved website components. The accepted outline remains in `../ideas.md`.
