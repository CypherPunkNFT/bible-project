# People of Scripture study ideas

Organize content development around the existing People and Long Memory features without duplicating their site implementation. Existing feature names identify content areas, not approval of new redesigns.

These are content proposals for review. Subcards are working content divisions, not site components. Passage lists are starting points for later reading and verification, not completed research.

## 36 People and genealogies

**Purpose:** Explore lives, relationships and family connections named in Scripture.

**General direction:** Inventory existing person records and identify where explanation is needed beyond the directory.

**Possible subcards:** Individual lives; families; shared names; relationships; narrative setting; limits of identification.

**Starting passages or sources:** References from the existing people records.

## 37 Long Memory

**Purpose:** Follow how a person is named, speaks, acts and is remembered across Scripture.

**General direction:** Use the existing feature as a content base; separate exhaustive name references from curated interpretive selections.

**Possible subcards:** Mentions; words spoken; pivotal moments; remembered later; passage context; limits of counts.

**Starting passages or sources:** Existing Long Memory data and selected passages for each person.

## 38 Prophets in their times

**Purpose:** Connect named prophets with their setting and message.

**General direction:** Reuse existing prophet content; distinguish the historical person, a book’s voice and traditional attribution.

**Possible subcards:** Calling; setting; message; symbolic actions; response; later remembrance.

**Starting passages or sources:** Passages from existing prophet records.

## 39 Rulers and their choices

**Purpose:** Study rulers through the accounts of their actions and judgments.

**General direction:** Preserve each biblical writer’s perspective rather than reducing a reign to a simple score.

**Possible subcards:** Accession; decisions; worship; justice; prophetic encounters; the account’s verdict.

**Starting passages or sources:** Samuel, Kings, Chronicles and related prophetic passages.

## 40 The apostles and their witness

**Purpose:** Follow the apostles through the Gospels, Acts and the letters.

**General direction:** Separate biblical accounts from later traditions and reuse existing apostle content.

**Possible subcards:** Calling; learning from Jesus; witness; journeys; letters; what the sources do not say.

**Starting passages or sources:** Gospels, Acts and relevant epistles.
