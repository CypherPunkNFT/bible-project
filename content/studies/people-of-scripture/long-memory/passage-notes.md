1. **Exodus 3:1–15.** The double address in 3:4 demonstrates occurrence versus verse counting. Narrative and pronouns surrounding named verses remain part of the scene. No claim that the source index exhausts unnamed references is made.
2. **Exodus 3:10–15.** Moses' questions alternate with God's answers. The current words lens selects 11–15; not all its words belong to Moses. No unsupported account of his inner psychology is supplied.
3. **John 21:15–19.** Questions, answers, commands, Peter's grief and the narrator's explanation of Jesus' saying all matter. The passage does not describe the later execution itself. Do not add traditional details of Peter's death or make language distinctions not established by this KJV reading.

Which departure Hebrews 11:27 describes and its relation to fear in Exodus 2 are interpretive questions, not resolved by labeling it later remembrance.
