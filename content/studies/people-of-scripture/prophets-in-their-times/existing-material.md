## Wording to avoid importing without review

The guide uses a “wrote a book” label for associated book records, and Joel's short description calls him the author. Study 38 does not inherit these labels as a conclusion about every stage of a book's authorship or compilation. It uses conventional book names and describes what the text attributes or narrates.

The guide's source text credits STEP TIPNR summaries and says its prophet inclusion list was hand-checked. This task inspected selected records and passages, not the entire inclusion audit or upstream summaries. No claim of new verification for every prophet follows.
