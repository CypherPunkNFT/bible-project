## Direction

Teach one habit: ask six things of any prophet, and keep what the text gives apart from what you were about to add. Only one direction was built, as with the earlier mock-ups, because this was an existing draft.

## The page

- **Opening.** Study trail, the three concepts in gold, the title, the lead, Hosea 1:1, and a drawing in thin line: a city gate, a broken clay jar before it, and a small cloud far off that drifts slowly. A visible button pauses it, it stops off-screen, and with reduced motion it is still. No person is drawn. Below it, four computed figures, each saying what it means and what it does not, and the statement of who was chosen.
- **01 The opening verses, taken at their word.** The signature. Rulers in rows with no years: Judah, then Israel, then foreign kings in no claimed order. Twelve prophets have a column, and a mark only at a ruler their text names in so many words. A line between Hezekiah and Josiah says Manasseh and Amon reign there and no opening names them. Huldah and Elijah are orange and say "from Kings". The six who name no ruler sit in a sentence below. Choosing a name sets its opening verse large, with each named thing underlined and listed in words, and says what is not in those words. A button then opens that prophet's six lines. Below 900 wide the rows become a list of names and rulers.
- **02 Six questions, asked of any prophet.** Six gold words on one rule. Choosing one opens its reading beside the rule and in the address: the main passage set first, the part headings, labelled readings and care notes, then Look closely and Your response. The acted-sign reading adds three acts under the same four questions. On a phone the words sit in two columns above the reading.
- **03 The record, with its silences.** Eighteen name buttons, each with six drawn marks: filled for a verse that gives it, half for quoted later with the name not given, empty for no account found. A name opens one sheet from the right, full width on a phone, with six lines of verse and phrase. A verse inside it opens in the same sheet, with a way back. The caption says an empty mark is not proof that nothing happened.
- **Close.** The way of reading, the invitation, the question, "Read the passage again before you answer" with Micah 6:6–8, links back, a small far-off gate, and the sources.

## Choices made, and why

- **No years anywhere.** Reigns have disputed dates and overlaps, and this page is about what the text names. The site's timeline holds its eras.
- **Silence is a declared state.** Each of the 108 lines is given a verse or declared empty in [study_lists.py](../../../../design/prophets-in-their-times-directions/study_lists.py), so an empty mark is never a gap in the data.
- **Colour never carries meaning alone.** "From Kings" and "in Kings" are written beside the orange. The underlines differ in style, and each is listed in words.
- **Unlike the sibling People pages.** No lights, no rings. Rows, a rule and a record.
