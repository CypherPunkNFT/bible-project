# Prophets in their times working notes

**Item 38 · People of Scripture · Rewritten to the study standard · 2026-10-30.** The first draft of 2026-10-13, in the King James, is replaced here.

## Purpose and central question

The prophets spoke to real people about real things. What does each text actually say about who sent him, when, to whom, and what came of it? Ask any prophet the same six things, and keep what the text gives apart from what you were about to add. The [reader-facing text](content.md) is the source of the [mock-up](../../../../design/prophets-in-their-times-directions/README.md).

## Content divisions

1. Calling: Jeremiah, Amos, Micah.
2. Setting: Haggai, Amos, Joel, Zephaniah, Zechariah, Malachi, Obadiah, Nahum.
3. Message: Amos, Micah, Malachi, Zephaniah, with words about other nations.
4. Acted sign: the jar, the marriage and three names, the crown.
5. Response: Huldah and Josiah, Haggai's hearers, Hezekiah in Jeremiah 26, a priest of Bethel, Ezekiel's listeners.
6. Remembrance: James on Elijah, 1 Peter 1:10–12, and what the New Testament says is fulfilled.

## Decisions

- **Widened as [the to-do list](../../../../../Pages/Topics/TODO.md) asked:** Hosea, Micah, Zephaniah, Zechariah and Malachi fully, with a line each for Obadiah and Nahum, then all sixteen book names in the figure and record.
- **Hosea's marriage is read as events, and labelled as this study's reading.** The text narrates it. Calvin and Fausset, who held otherwise, are given in their words. A care note says it is no rule for a marriage and no reason to stay in an unsafe home.
- **Joel's date stays open.** The heading does not settle it. Calvin and Fausset are shown, and neither is chosen.
- **Authorship and dating of books are not taken up.** The draft's line that a title "does not settle authorship" was cut, because it read as doubt against Article 1 of the Baptist Faith and Message. The page reads what the books say of their settings.
- **Huldah is a prophetess, as Scripture calls her.** A care note quotes Article 6 and draws no rule about church office from her, either way.
- **Fulfilment.** Acts 10:43 and Luke 24:27 state that the prophets speak of Christ. The page lists only the places the New Testament names, and says this is its listing rule, not a limit. The crown in Zechariah 6 is labelled as a reading, with Calvin named.
- **Prophecy today is not handled.** No reader is told to repeat an act or announce judgment over a place. Whether prophetic gifts continue is left out, because Baptists differ.
- **Nahum and Obadiah:** words about those peoples in those days. No modern nation is named.
- **Amos 5:** a care note against party use, and against softening the particulars.
