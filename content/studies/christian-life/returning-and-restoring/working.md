# Returning and restoring

**Content pass status:** item 10, first full draft ready for review, 2026-10-13. [Content draft](content.md); [passage and editorial notes](passage-notes.md).

## Purpose and central question

Explore confession, repentance and restoration after wrongdoing. How can individuals and communities pursue truth, changed conduct, forgiveness and comfort while retaining accountability and care for those harmed?

## Content divisions

1. Confession — walking in the light and Christ as advocate.
2. Godly sorrow — repentance and changed response, not a measure of emotional display.
3. Restore in meekness — the outline's gentleness in restoration, with mutual care and personal responsibility.
4. Forgiving one another — mercy received and given, distinguished from immediate trust or restored authority.
5. Discipline and comfort — correction, sufficient discipline and confirmed love.
6. Watchfulness — vulnerability, God's faithfulness and practical response.

All six category themes are developed. This is a content study, not a universal disciplinary procedure or a timetable for restoring responsibilities.
