# Faith in daily life

**Content pass status:** first full draft ready for review, 2026-10-13. Read [the content draft](content.md) and [passage and editorial notes](passage-notes.md). All six themes are developed. The outline below preserves the initial planning material.

## Central question

How do the letters connect faith with life at home, work, in society and in the use of possessions?

## Proposed subcards

| Subcard | Starting passages | Focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Marriage and family | Ephesians 5:21–6:4; Colossians 3:18–21 | Household relationships and responsibilities |
| Work and integrity | Ephesians 4:28; 1 Thessalonians 4:9–12; Colossians 3:22–4:1 | Honest work, responsibility and the historical setting of household instructions |
| Money and generosity | 2 Corinthians 8–9; 1 Timothy 6:6–19 | Contentment, wealth and giving |
| Hospitality | Romans 12:9–13; Hebrews 13:1–3; 1 Peter 4:8–11 | Welcoming and caring for others |
| Neighbours and authorities | Romans 12:14–13:10; 1 Peter 2:11–17 | Public conduct, neighbour love and authority |
| Conscience and disputed matters | Romans 14:1–15:7; 1 Corinthians 8 | Freedom, conscience and concern for others |

## Experience to explore

Begin with a setting, then connect instruction, historical context and present-day application. Give difficult passages room for explanation instead of reducing them to brief advice.

## Open questions

How should household passages and differing Christian interpretations be presented? Ancient slavery and modern employment must not be treated as interchangeable. Decide which conscience material belongs here and which belongs in Life together.
