# Freedom that serves

**Content pass status:** item 08, first full draft ready for review, 2026-10-13. [Content draft](content.md); [passage and editorial notes](passage-notes.md).

## Purpose and central question

Study freedom in Christ alongside love and responsibility. How do believers distinguish standing firm in liberty, refraining for another's sake and refusing what Scripture forbids?

## Content divisions

1. Called to freedom — Galatians' dispute about circumcision, justification and reliance on Christ.
2. By love serve one another — liberty and the life of the Spirit, developing the outline's love rather than self-indulgence theme.
3. Conscience — convictions, welcome and action before God in Romans 14.
4. The weaker brother — the harm described in 1 Corinthians 8 and the prohibition of idolatry in chapter 10.
5. Choosing restraint — Paul's voluntary restraint and the different meal settings in 1 Corinthians 10.
6. Doing good — restoration, burdens, responsibility and persevering service.

All six category themes are developed. First Corinthians 8–10 is followed as a connected argument. Contemporary reflections remain applications rather than new universal commands.
