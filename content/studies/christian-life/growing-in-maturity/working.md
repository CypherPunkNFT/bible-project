# Growing in maturity

**Content pass status:** item 07, first full draft ready for review, 2026-10-13. [Content draft](content.md); [passage and editorial notes](passage-notes.md).

## Purpose and central question

Follow the letters' descriptions of continuing Christian growth. How do renewed thinking, practice, perseverance and shared life contribute to growth in Christ, and how do the passages relate diligence to God's work?

## Content divisions

1. Renewing the mind — mercy, transformed thinking, humility and service.
2. Rooted in Christ — continuing in the one received, thanksgiving and stability.
3. Learning through practice — building on foundations and doing what has been learned.
4. Growing together — equipping, patient relationships and each part's contribution.
5. Pressing on — knowing Christ, admitting incompleteness and resurrection hope.
6. Adding to faith — God's provision, diligence and fruitful qualities.

All six category themes are developed. No spiritual ranking, score or prescribed timetable is introduced. Reflections are applications, not additional commands attributed to Scripture.
