# Learning to discern

**Content pass status:** item 06, first full draft ready for review, 2026-10-13. [Content draft](content.md); [passage and editorial notes](passage-notes.md).

## Purpose and central question

Explore how Christians are instructed to test teaching and make judgments. How can a believer examine claims, seek wisdom and judge faithfully while remaining humble and teachable?

## Content divisions

1. Try the spirits — the confession of Christ and the testing of spiritual claims.
2. Examine teaching — the gospel's content and the examination of supporting passages.
3. Wisdom from above — conduct, motives, peace and willingness to listen.
4. Mature judgment — understanding trained by use and growth with the body.
5. Good counsel — hearing before answering and weighing advice.
6. Disputed matters — distinguishing the issue and considering another person's good.

All six themes from the category outline are retained. The first uses the KJV's “Try the spirits” in its reader-facing title. Proverbs and Acts are identified supporting readings outside the epistles. Reflections are applications, not additional commands attributed to Scripture.
