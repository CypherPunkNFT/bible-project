# Learning to discern passage and editorial notes

Content pass, item 06. [Reader-facing draft](content.md); [working outline](working.md). First draft ready for review, not approved final copy.

## Scope and source basis

An introduction for general readers to examining teaching and making judgments. Distinguish spiritual claims from grounds for accepting them, discernment from suspicion, and conviction from an assertion of private certainty. This is not an exhaustive account of prophecy, church authority or doctrinal boundaries.

Read in the repository KJV on 2026-10-13: [1 John](../../../../data/plain/kjv/1JN.json), [1 Thessalonians](../../../../data/plain/kjv/1TH.json), [James](../../../../data/plain/kjv/JAS.json), [Hebrews](../../../../data/plain/kjv/HEB.json), [Galatians](../../../../data/plain/kjv/GAL.json), [Acts](../../../../data/plain/kjv/ACT.json), [Proverbs](../../../../data/plain/kjv/PRO.json), [Romans](../../../../data/plain/kjv/ROM.json), [Philippians](../../../../data/plain/kjv/PHP.json), [Ephesians](../../../../data/plain/kjv/EPH.json). Romans 14:7–12 was also read during item 05's source preparation.

Six full block quotations are KJV. Explanations and applications are editorial prose. Hebrews is not assigned a named author. The draft does not use external historical reconstructions to identify First John's opponents.

## Interpretive boundaries

- **First John:** the test is about Christ and purported spiritual teaching. Do not turn it into a technique for detecting hidden spirits in strangers or diagnosing people. Confession is not treated as reciting a sentence that validates every other teaching.
- **The letter's “we”:** the draft refers to the witness represented by the letter. A modern speaker cannot simply substitute themselves into verse 6 and call disagreement proof of error.
- **Prophecy:** continuation and cessation questions remain unresolved. The draft retains both the warning against despising and the instruction to test. No contemporary revelation is authenticated here.
- **First Thessalonians 5:22:** assigned in context without building a rule about all possible appearances or reputational suspicions from the KJV wording alone. No Greek lexical claim is made.
- **Galatians:** a gospel warning is not automatically a verdict on every secondary disagreement. The draft does not enumerate all doctrinal boundaries; a full account must follow Galatians' argument beyond its opening.
- **Berea:** the Scriptures in the narrative are not described as an already completed New Testament. The reading practices offered are applications; the verse is not made to resolve every question of interpretive authority.
- **James:** “conversation” concerns conduct; “easy to be intreated” is explained as openness to reason. Purity and peace are held together. Asking for wisdom is not treated as certifying every subsequent impression.
- **Hebrews:** maturity through practice is distinguished from obscure knowledge or infallibility. Going beyond foundational instruction does not mean rejecting its truth. This section ends at 6:3 and does not claim to settle the following warning passage.
- **Counsel:** Proverbs contains wisdom sayings, not a guarantee that majority advice will succeed. Modern evidence-gathering applications are marked as applications. First Thessalonians' “feebleminded” is paraphrased as fainthearted in this pastoral context, not used as a diagnostic label.
- **Romans:** differences over food and days are not made a blanket category covering all conduct. The distinction from Galatians is a question for study, not a mechanical test that settles every controversy. Conscience is respected without being declared incapable of correction.

## Connections and review decisions

**02 Life together** covers communal correction and disagreement. **04 Prayer in the letters** develops Philippians 1's prayer at greater length. **05 Faith in daily life** applies conscience to everyday settings. Here the emphasis is how judgments are formed and examined.

**07 Growing in maturity** will develop sustained growth beyond the narrower question of judgment. **08 Freedom that serves** will treat conscience and restraint more fully. **23 The Holy Spirit** can address gifts in greater depth; **27 Scripture and its witness** and **34 Reading a passage in context** can address the basis and practice of scriptural interpretation.

Possible later refinement: a worked example of examining a claim. Use a transparent textual example rather than inventing a contemporary opponent or caricaturing a tradition. No such example is required to complete the six present units.

All units include readings, exposition, questions and labelled reflection. No layout, artwork, navigation or website implementation is included.
