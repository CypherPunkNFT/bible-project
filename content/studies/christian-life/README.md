# Christian Life study development

Develop substantial studies of Christian life from the epistles, with distinct questions and ways of exploring Scripture. The aim is a collection comparable in substance to Long Memory and People & genealogies, rather than another directory of topic links.

## Confirmed direction

- The first study is **A new creation**, replacing the proposed title **Put off. Put on.**
- Begin by developing content and ideas in this workspace, independently of site infrastructure.
- Scaffold the collection, then work through the studies one by one.

## Content principles

Keep the instructions connected to their reasons: what God has done in Christ, who the recipients are, and how they are called to live. Read commands within their paragraphs and letters before combining them with other passages.

Do not force every letter into the same terminology or every subject into a before-and-after pair. Preserve the difference between a command, a prayer, a promise, an example and an editorial application.

## Boundaries between the studies

**A new creation** concerns identity and changed conduct. **Life together** develops relationships within the community. **Faith under pressure** develops endurance in difficulty. **Prayer in the letters** explores the prayers themselves. **Faith in daily life** develops settings such as work, home and the use of money.

Shared passages can connect studies, but each treatment should have a distinct question. Keep substantial explanations in one home rather than repeating them across every card.
