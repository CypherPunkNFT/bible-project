# Life together

**Content pass status:** first full draft ready for review, 2026-10-13. Read [the content draft](content.md) and [passage and editorial notes](passage-notes.md). The outline below preserves the initial planning material. All six sections are now developed.

## Central question

What does a community shaped by Christ owe its members, especially when care, correction or disagreement becomes difficult?

## Proposed subcards

| Subcard | Starting passages | Focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Love and welcome | Romans 12:9–13; 15:1–7 | Affection, hospitality and receiving one another |
| Bear burdens | Galatians 6:1–5 | Restoration, gentleness and responsibility |
| Encourage and build up | 1 Thessalonians 5:11–15; Hebrews 10:19–25 | Encouragement, patience and gathering together |
| Correct and restore | Galatians 6:1; 2 Thessalonians 3:14–15 | Correction that treats the other person as a brother |
| Gifts and service | 1 Corinthians 12:4–27; 1 Peter 4:8–11 | Different gifts serving one body |
| Unity through disagreement | Romans 14:1–23; Ephesians 4:1–6 | Conscience, welcome and unity |

## Experience to explore

Follow a relationship or a need through the relevant instructions. A gathering of people could help illustrate reciprocity, but first establish the content: who is addressed, what is asked, and why.

## Open questions

How should this study distinguish doctrinal disagreement, matters of conscience and harmful conduct? Which examples belong here rather than in Faith in daily life? Avoid treating every disagreement as the situation addressed in Romans 14.
