# Living the Christian Life study ideas

Explore how belonging to Christ shapes character, relationships, endurance and ordinary responsibilities. Keep the instructions connected to their reasons in each letter.

These are content proposals for review. Subcards are working content divisions, not site components. Passage lists are starting points for later reading and verification, not completed research.

## 01 A new creation

**Purpose:** Connect new identity in Christ with a changed life.

**General direction:** Begin with grace and reconciliation, then follow the old and new life in the epistles. The title is confirmed; its structure remains proposed.

**Possible subcards:** In Christ; the old and the new; words that build up; anger and forgiveness; desires and habits; walking in love.

**Starting passages or sources:** 2 Corinthians 5:14–21; Ephesians 2:1–10; Ephesians 4:17–5:2; Colossians 3:1–17.

## 02 Life together

**Purpose:** Explore the responsibilities believers have toward one another.

**General direction:** Develop care, correction and belonging within the Christian community, including relationships that become difficult.

**Possible subcards:** Love and welcome; bear burdens; encourage and build up; correct and restore; gifts and service; unity through disagreement.

**Starting passages or sources:** Romans 12:9–21; Romans 14–15; Galatians 6:1–5; 1 Corinthians 12.

## 03 Faith under pressure

**Purpose:** Read the letters alongside trials, temptation, loss and opposition.

**General direction:** Distinguish different kinds of suffering and the grounds for hope given in each passage.

**Possible subcards:** Trials and endurance; temptation; anxiety and prayer; persecution; grief and hope; contentment.

**Starting passages or sources:** James 1:2–18; Philippians 4:4–20; 1 Peter 3–5; 1 Thessalonians 4:13–18.

## 04 Prayer in the letters

**Purpose:** Discover what the apostles ask God to give their readers.

**General direction:** Read complete prayers before comparing their requests, reasons and connections to the surrounding letter.

**Possible subcards:** Wisdom and understanding; strength within; growing in love; a worthy walk; thanksgiving; prayer for others.

**Starting passages or sources:** Ephesians 1:15–23; Ephesians 3:14–21; Philippians 1:3–11; Colossians 1:3–14.

## 05 Faith in daily life

**Purpose:** Connect faith with ordinary settings and responsibilities.

**General direction:** Address home, work, money and public life with historical context, distinguishing ancient institutions from modern applications.

**Possible subcards:** Marriage and family; work and integrity; money and generosity; hospitality; neighbours and authorities; conscience.

**Starting passages or sources:** Ephesians 5:21–6:9; 1 Thessalonians 4:9–12; 2 Corinthians 8–9; Romans 12–15.

## 06 Learning to discern

**Purpose:** Explore how Christians are instructed to test teaching and make judgments.

**General direction:** Distinguish discernment from suspicion, spiritual claims from evidence, and wisdom from claims of private certainty.

**Possible subcards:** Test the spirits; examine teaching; wisdom from above; mature judgment; good counsel; disputed matters.

**Starting passages or sources:** 1 John 4:1–6; 1 Thessalonians 5:19–22; James 3:13–18; Hebrews 5:11–14.

## 07 Growing in maturity

**Purpose:** Follow the letters’ descriptions of continuing Christian growth.

**General direction:** Explore learning, practice, perseverance and growth together without inventing a spiritual ranking or progress score.

**Possible subcards:** Renewing the mind; rooted in Christ; learning through practice; growing together; pressing on; adding to faith.

**Starting passages or sources:** Romans 12:1–2; Colossians 2:6–7; Ephesians 4:11–16; Philippians 3:7–16; 2 Peter 1:3–11.

## 08 Freedom that serves

**Purpose:** Study freedom in Christ alongside love and responsibility.

**General direction:** Focus on the argument about liberty, restraint and service; link to daily-life examples instead of repeating all of them.

**Possible subcards:** Called to freedom; love rather than self-indulgence; conscience; the weaker brother; choosing restraint; doing good.

**Starting passages or sources:** Galatians 5:1–15; 1 Corinthians 8–10; Romans 14:1–15:7.

## 09 Bearing witness

**Purpose:** Explore how believers speak and live before those outside the church.

**General direction:** Bring together readiness to answer, gracious speech, conduct, courage and the limits of persuasion.

**Possible subcards:** Ready to answer; gentleness and respect; gracious conversation; conduct that commends; courage; prayer for open doors.

**Starting passages or sources:** 1 Peter 2:11–12; 1 Peter 3:13–17; Colossians 4:2–6; Philippians 1:12–30.

## 10 Returning and restoring

**Purpose:** Explore confession, repentance and the restoration of someone who has fallen.

**General direction:** Distinguish forgiveness, restored fellowship, consequences and accountability; do not imply that forgiveness requires unsafe access or instant trust.

**Possible subcards:** Confession; godly sorrow; gentleness in restoration; forgiveness; discipline and comfort; watchfulness.

**Starting passages or sources:** 1 John 1:5–2:2; 2 Corinthians 2:5–11; 2 Corinthians 7:8–12; Galatians 6:1–5.
