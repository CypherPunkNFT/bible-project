## Direction

Teach one habit: before you obey a line in a letter, look back for what is already so in Christ. The picture is a page folded down its middle, with what is already so on the left, what to do now on the right, and on the fold the small joining word the letter itself uses. Only one direction was built, as with the earlier mock-ups, because this was an existing draft.

## The page

- **Opening.** Study trail, the three concepts in gold, "A new" and "creation" in gold italic, the lead, 2 Corinthians 5:17, and a thin-line drawing: a rail, an old garment laid down in dotted line, a new one hung ready with a wash of the letters' colour, and a slow thread running between them. A visible button pauses the thread, it stops off-screen, and with reduced motion it is still. No person is drawn. Below it, four computed figures, each saying what it means, and the statement of what was chosen.
- **01 Already so. Now live by it.** The signature. Six lines, one per reading, each crossing the same vertical fold in the letter's exact words. Choosing a line opens its reading in place beneath it, and the line does not move. In Christ holds a small figure of four verses that say Behold, The old and the new holds Colossians 3's lists on two rails with love laid over them, and Words that build up holds the draft's Ephesians 4 lines of stop, instead and why, now in the verses' own words.
- **02 Eight more. The same fold.** Eight more places, from these letters and two others. Pointing or keyboard focus shows the verses on a line beneath, and choosing opens them on the sheet. One has no joining word and says so. Where the command comes first, the line says so.
- **03 A long way in. Then, therefore.** Five letters drawn as plain lines to one scale, length by verses, with a gold mark at the turn and small marks for earlier commands. James has no turn and the page says why it is there. The drawing is hidden from assistive technology. The references beneath, in text order, are the controls, and each one lights its mark.
- **04 A command. Now look back.** Three commands. The reader is asked to look first, and the answer stays closed until asked for.
- **05 Six questions, faced.** A plain ruled list. Each opens the one sheet from the right, with the verse, then this study's reading, then what else has been said.
- **Close.** The way of reading, the invitation to the one who is unsure, the question, "Read the passage again before you answer" with 2 Corinthians 5:14–21, the way back to Living the Christian Life, a dawn horizon for Romans 13:12, and the sources.

## Choices made, and why

- **A fold, not a hinge or a door.** Nothing swings. Bearing witness already has doors, and a fold keeps the two halves on one sheet.
- **The first half is not always God's act.** Some are what the readers have done or now are, so each line says whose it is in small words. Claiming "God did this" on all fourteen would be false.
- **No pairing of opposites.** The rails and lines keep the verses' order and do not invent an opposite for every word, as the draft itself warned.
- **The draft's central comparison moved inward.** Faith in daily life has since taken "ask the sentence why" as its habit, so stop, instead and why stays in reading 03 and this page teaches something else.
- **The turn is called this study's reading.** The page names commands before each mark, and says the opening of Romans and of Galatians is not all about what God has done.
- **Colour never carries meaning alone.** "Already so", "Now", the joining word and each mark's reference are all words.
- **Unlike the sibling Christian Life pages.** Faith in daily life uses rooms, Faith under pressure uses dark layers and Bearing witness uses doors. This one uses a fold and a thread.
