# A new creation

**Status:** study 01, rewritten in the Berean with a mock-up for the owner's review, 2026-10-30. [Study text](content.md); [passage and editorial notes](passage-notes.md); [design](design.md); [the mock-up and its record](../../../../design/a-new-creation-directions/README.md).

## Purpose and central question

Connect new identity in Christ with a changed life. What changes when someone belongs to Christ, and how do the letters connect what is already so with what they tell you to do?

## Content divisions

The six readings of the 2026-10-13 draft stay, in the draft's order.

1. In Christ: a different centre for life; grace and the life that follows.
2. The old and the new: learning Christ; putting on a shared life.
3. Words that build up: truth because we belong to one another; a change that reaches the hearer.
4. Anger and forgiveness: anger that must be addressed; forgiveness with a stated reason.
5. Desires and habits: freedom expressed in service; giving oneself to God.
6. Walking in love: the pattern is Christ; love with concrete responsibilities.

Added around them: six lines across a fold, eight more places, five letters drawn as lines, three commands to look back from, six hard questions, and a close with the way of reading, an invitation and the passage again.
