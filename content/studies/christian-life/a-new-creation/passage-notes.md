# A new creation: what changes when you believe: passage notes

Merge of draft 01 (A new creation) with draft 07 (Growing in maturity). Folder: christian-life/a-new-creation. All Scripture quoted from the supplied Berean Standard Bible text.

## 2 Corinthians 5:14–17 (part 01)

**Context.** Paul defends his ministry to the Corinthians (chapters 2–7). Verses 14–17 ground his life in Christ's death "for all" and end in the new-creation sentence; verses 18–21 (reconciliation and the appeal, owned by the cross study) follow.
**Main readings.** (1) Of the individual believer: "he is a new creation" (Berean; Jamieson, Fausset and Brown per draft 01; this site's reading, with article IV). (2) A wider statement, "there is a new creation", in some modern translations; not argued from any source read here. Calvin (per draft 01) heard a requirement in it. "According to the flesh" in verse 16 read as "by human standards".
**Links.** The cross, the empty tomb and the throne (25) for 5:18–21; Saved by grace (24) for Ephesians 2:8–10, quoted; The hope to come (30) for the renewal of all things.

## Romans 6:1–14 (part 02; question on baptism)

**Context.** Answers the objection raised by Romans 5:20–21 (grace abounding). Union with Christ in death and rising is pictured in baptism; then the commands to count, not let reign and present.
**Main readings.** Baptists (article VII; Second London Confession 29) read baptism here as the public sign of union already received by faith. Lutherans (Small Catechism) and other sacramental traditions read baptism as a means by which God gives forgiveness; shown as theirs. "Not under law, but under grace": the confession does not settle how the law of Moses applies; Baptist views (three-kinds-of-law, new covenant theology, dispensational) are not chosen between.
**Links.** Baptism and the Lord's Supper (59) quotes 6:3–4; The Ten Commandments (61); Leviticus (47) shows the law views.

## Romans 12:1–2 (part 02)

**Context.** The turn in Romans from eleven chapters on God's mercy to how to live. Temple sacrifice described in Israel's own terms before Paul's use of it.
**Main readings.** Calvin (paraphrased, check below) on mercy as the ground of obedience. Draft 07's caution kept: the verse is not a technique for foreknowing every personal decision.
**Links.** The Holy Spirit (23) owns 12:3–8; Faith in daily life (05) owns 12:9–13:10, from which 12:18–19 is quoted in part 04.

## Ephesians 4:17–5:2 (parts 03 and 04)

**Context.** Follows the unity and gifts of 4:1–16 (owned by The church and its calling, 28). Contrast of former Gentile life with "learning Christ"; old self / new self; four three-part commands; imitation of God.
**Main readings.** "Put off… put on" read either as instruction (taught to put off) or as accomplished (Colossians 3:9 "have taken off"); this study holds both together and does not make stages. "Be angry, yet do not sin" (4:26, echoing Psalm 4:4, not discussed): read as a limit on anger, not a command to be angry; Calvin on faults of anger (check below). "Gentiles" explained as non-Jewish peoples, describing the readers' former life, not a slur on any people.
**Links.** The Holy Spirit (23) for 4:30; Life together (02); Learning to discern (06) quotes 4:14 elsewhere.

## Colossians 3:1–17 (parts 03 and 04)

**Context.** Follows the warning against false teaching in chapter 2. Raised with Christ (3:1–4), put to death and put aside (3:5–11), clothe yourselves (3:12–17).
**Main readings.** "Being renewed… in the image of its Creator" (3:10) links to the image of God (Genesis 1); "the elect" explained as chosen, with the Calvinist / non-Calvinist Baptist difference left open. "Put to death" given a safe-messaging note.
**Links.** Every life in God's image (62); Humanity, sin and renewal (29) quotes 3:9–10; Fear, anxiety and despair (56); Life together (02).

## James 1:19–20 with Ephesians 4:26 (part 04)

**Context.** James turns from God giving birth by the word of truth (1:18) to hearing and doing; Ephesians 4:26 read beside it.
**Main readings.** Both limit anger; neither calls every feeling of anger sin.
**Links.** Faith under pressure (03) walks James 1:2–18.

## Hebrews 5:11–6:3 (part 05)

**Context.** A digression before the argument on Melchizedek; it leads straight into the warning of 6:4–8, which this study does not walk.
**Main readings.** Maturity as judgement trained by use; the "foundation" as basics to build on, not discard. The warning in 6:4–6 is read by Baptists as describing those who were never truly regenerate or as a hypothetical/means of perseverance; by Wesleyan and Arminian Christians as real loss. Handed to Chosen and kept.
**Links.** Learning to discern (06); Chosen and kept (58).

## 2 Peter 1:3–11 (part 05)

**Context.** After the greeting; God's provision (3–4), the chain of qualities (5–7), its fruit and the warning (8–9), calling and election made sure (10–11).
**Main readings.** "Partakers of the divine nature": this site reads it as sharing God's moral character by gift; Eastern Orthodox theosis teaching mentioned without quotation. "Make your calling and election sure": confirmed by life, not earned; details of election left open between Calvinist and non-Calvinist Baptists (article V). Draft 07's note kept: the list is not a ladder.
**Links.** Chosen and kept (58); The Holy Spirit (23) for Galatians 5:16, 22–23, quoted.

## Philippians 3:7–14 (part 06)

**Context.** Paul's warning against those who trusted in circumcision (3:2–6), then his re-counted ledger and forward pursuit; continues to 3:21.
**Main readings.** Righteousness "from God on the basis of faith" as justification by faith (article IV). "Forgetting what is behind" read with draft 07's care: not erasing past harm or responsibility.
**Links.** The good news (54); Saved by grace (24).
