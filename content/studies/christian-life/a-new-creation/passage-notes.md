# A new creation passage and editorial notes

Working notes for [the reader-facing text](content.md). Rewritten 2026-10-30 from the Berean Standard Bible for the mock-up (the King James draft of 2026-10-13 is in git history). Built page: [the mock-up and its record](../../../../design/a-new-creation-directions/README.md).

## Source basis

Every anchor passage was read in the site's Berean files, [data/plain/bsb/](../../../../data/plain/bsb/). The build checks every block quotation and every quoted phrase against the verses named in its own paragraph. That proves the words are there. It does not prove a reading is right.

The six readings, their order, their references and their reflection prompts are the draft's. Wording moved from the King James to the Berean, and "Consider" and "Reflect" stay as the draft had them.

## The new shape

The draft's design made its central figure a comparison of put away, practise and reason. A sibling study, Faith in daily life, has since taken "ask the sentence why" as its habit. So this study looks at something else in the same passages: the letters say a thing as done and then as a command, often in the same word. In the Berean: reconciled, put on, forgave and forgive, live and walk, loved and love, died and dead, died and put to death, clothed and clothe. The draft's four Ephesians lines stay, inside reading 03, as what the draft wanted of them, kept short.

## Interpretive boundaries

- **New creation.** Read first of the person (2 Corinthians 5:17, with Article 4), with the wider renewal given to Revelation 21:5. Calvin's "let him be" and the wording "there is a new creation" are reported. No writer for that wording was read.
- **Grace and works.** Ephesians 2:8–10 is one paragraph. The argument over what "this" in 2:8 refers to is not taken up.
- **Put off, put on.** Ephesians says "to put off", Colossians "have taken off". Compared in English only, and not turned into stages.
- **Baptism.** Romans 6:3–4 is kept in view. The site's voice follows Article 7: an act of obedience that pictures death, burial and new life. The Lutheran teaching is shown as theirs. This replaces the draft's refusal to settle it, which the doctrinal foundation does not allow.
- **Security.** Galatians 5:21 is kept at full strength and read with Article 5. The Methodist Articles are shown as another reading. The site does not tell any reader their spiritual state.
- **Flesh.** Not the body. Romans 6:13 asks for the body to be given to God.
- **Put to death.** Named practices, with a care note added by the site.
- **Anger.** Not read as an order to be angry, or as a rule that every conflict ends by sunset. Calvin's "before the sun has set" sits closer to a clock than this study does, and his words are given.
- **Forgiveness.** Application marked as the site's, with a care note. Luke 17:3 is named. Whether one forgives before repentance is not settled, and no writer on it was read.
- **Romans 7.** Calvin and Wesley are reported. No choice is made, and the answer to "why do I still sin?" does not rest on that chapter.
