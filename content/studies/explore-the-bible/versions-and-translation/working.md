# 35 — Versions and translation

First content draft ready for owner review · 2026-10-13 · Not final-copy approval.

## Purpose and scope

Explain what readers compare when using Bible editions. [The draft](content.md) develops six units: translation choices, language, edition identity, notes, passage comparison and choosing for a reading purpose. Two subdivisions per unit are content organization, not a design specification.

## Confirmed development decisions

- Comparison texts are the local KJV, ASV and WEB records, not text reconstructed from memory.
- Use observable vocabulary differences and surrounding context; do not rank whole translations from isolated examples.
- Modern translation policies are not inferred from the six Scripture anchors. In particular Luke 1:4 and Nehemiah 8:8 are explicitly distinguished from claims about modern editions.
- The WEB project's preface and edition page supply limited attributed information. Their source-text judgments and wider historical claims are not adopted wholesale.
- Edition dates, contents, note conventions and textual variants are distinct questions. Reserve detailed manuscript history and canon debates for the academic path.
- Local catalogue abbreviations are not guaranteed to match publisher abbreviations. See [passage notes](passage-notes.md) before expanding edition examples.
- No legal/licensing advice, site changes, builds, design or deployment.

## Reuse and outstanding editorial work

Existing VersionsPage, structure/coverage descriptions, numbering labels, catalogue and import mapping were inspected. This study adds a reading guide around existing resources rather than a competing catalogue. The local KJV entry's 1611 year does not establish the precise textual revision of the stored file; do not add a revision claim without a source audit.

Before publication, retain full edition/source identifications for excerpts and decide whether the source-preface references should remain inline or move into supporting notes. Check digital edition currency when publishing; local excerpts are verified against the current workspace snapshot only.
