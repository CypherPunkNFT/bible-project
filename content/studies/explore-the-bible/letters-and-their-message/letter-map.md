## What is reused and what is added

Reuse: the 21-entry collection, its passage-bound outlines and references, and its existing research trail. Selected historical fields should be reviewed before reuse, not rewritten from memory.

Added here: a six-section reader-facing orientation, a complete-letter exercise in Philemon, comparison questions and explicit limits on reconstructing occasions. This map makes coverage visible without pretending the present study newly drafts all 21 introductions.
