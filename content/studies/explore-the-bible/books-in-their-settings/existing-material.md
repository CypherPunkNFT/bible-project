## Selected checks and decisions

- Inspected Hebrews and Philippians author, audience, date, occasion and outline fields. Used their textual leads, not their external historical conclusions.
- Inspected Ruth's context record. Its opening is explicitly set in the days of the judges without naming a judge. A later David entry is not permission to date Ruth's composition from David's reign.
- Read the Gospel comparison prompts. Preserve the distinction between narrative order and a reconstructed calendar when this study is later expanded book by book.
- Letter coverage totals 13 + 1 + 4 + 3 = 21. Prefer developing and reviewing this existing material to writing an unrelated second collection for study 32.
- The present deliverable adds a reader-facing method with worked examples. It does not certify complete introductions for all books.

## Reusable content brief for later individual introductions

For each book, record: what it says about its writer and hearers; stated setting; form or forms; an outline with passage bounds; central concerns demonstrated from the text; and a reading route through the whole work. Keep an explicit list of unknowns. Add attributed historical proposals only when useful and sourced. Start from existing records and retain their references rather than discarding them.

No routes, components, generated datasets or website assets were changed for this inventory.
