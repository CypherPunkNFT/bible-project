## Direction

Teach one habit: before you ask what a line promises, ask who is speaking and what kind of saying it is. The page does
this itself before the reader is asked to. Every printed Bible passage carries a speaker line above its words: who
speaks (Job, the friends, God from the whirlwind, a father, Wisdom, Folly, the Teacher, the closing voice, the woman),
what kind of saying it is (a report of a debate, a proverb, a searching question, a song), and, where the Bible itself
judges the speaker, its verdict in its own words. One direction was built, because the text already existed.

The shape comes from the text, not from a template. Job 28 is a descent: a miner sinks a shaft through the dark and
finds silver, and wisdom is not down there; then the poem turns upward, to the God who "understands its way". The four
books are four ways of speaking, and three of them end at the same floor, the fear of the LORD. The Song of Songs does
not, and the page does not pretend it does.

## The page

- **Opening.** The standard heading: study trail (Study / Explore the Bible / The wisdom books), the four concepts in
  gold capitals, *The wisdom* in ink with *books* in gold italic, the lead, and Job 28:28 with its speaker line. Beside
  it, in a faint circle and dotted orbit, a windlass over a mine shaft, its rope going down past veins of silver into
  the dark, the bucket rising and falling slowly; the motto "Silver from the mine. / Wisdom from God." No face or
  figure of God. Four counts with what they mean, and a row of links to every part.
- **00 Who is speaking?** The habit paragraph, the setting and the selection, then the page's main figure: four books
  as four columns of floating words joined by thin lines, no boxes. Each column is headed by its kind of saying, with a
  small shape for each kind (two facing lines for a debate, a balanced pair for a proverb, an open arc for a question, a
  wave for a song), so colour is never the only carrier. Under each kind, its voices; each voice opens a verse where it
  speaks. Eliphaz and his two friends carry God's verdict on what they said about Him, "you have not spoken about Me
  accurately" (42:7); Elihu, who is not named in it, does not. Three columns run down to one shared line, named by
  the page as the fear of the LORD and quoted at each point in its own verse's words: "the fear of the Lord" (Job
  28:28), "The fear of the LORD" (Proverbs 1:7 and 9:10) and "Fear God" (Ecclesiastes 12:13). The Song's line stops
  short at "love is as strong as death" (8:6), with the sentence: the Song does not name the fear of the LORD, and this
  page does not add it. The care note with the crisis lines stands directly under the figure, before any part.
- **01 The shaft (Job 28).** A cross-section drawn from the poem's own order: the mine for silver, the farthest
  darkness, the path no falcon has seen, the deep that says "It is not in me", the gold that cannot buy it, Abaddon and
  Death that have only heard a rumour. At the bottom, nothing. A line then rises past them all to "God understands its
  way" and the verse that opens the study. Each layer is a verse link. On a phone the drawing is left out and the
  layers stay as a plain list.
- **02 What God asks (Job 38–42).** Two lines across the top: what the reader is told (the council in heaven, Job 1–2)
  and what Job is told (nothing of it). Then the questions as floating words along one arc in their order in the text:
  foundations, morning stars, sea, dawn, snow, desert rain, lioness, raven, mountain goat, wild donkey, wild ox,
  ostrich, war horse, eagle, Behemoth, Leviathan; each opens its verse. Then the two verdicts in God's words, side by
  side: Job rebuked to his face (40:8) and called "My servant Job" (42:7); the friends "have not spoken about Me
  accurately" (42:7).
- **03 Two doors and two tables (Proverbs 1–9).** The same line over the front door (1:7) and the back door (9:10),
  drawn as two lintels with the father's talks between. At the end, Wisdom's table and Folly's table side by side: the
  build proves their first words are the same (9:4 and 9:16), then shows what each offers and what Folly does not say
  (9:18).
- **04 The pair (Proverbs 26:4–5).** The two sayings mirrored, "Do not answer" against "Answer", with the line under
  them: wisdom is knowing which moment you are in. Then three lines read two ways, as a promise and as a proverb, each
  set against the verse Scripture puts beside it.
- **05 Fourteen pairs (Ecclesiastes 3).** All fourteen pairs of times, read out of the Berean by the build and counted
  against the study's "Fourteen pairs", in two columns of short words. The page sets no count of "under the sun"
  against "the hand of God" (2:24), because that contrast would choose one of the two readings of the book.
- **06 The refrains (Song of Songs).** The three warnings (2:7; 3:5; 8:4), the build proving that the clause "Do not
  arouse or awaken love until the time is right" is the same each time (8:4 leaves out the oath by the gazelles), and the three lines of belonging stepping down the page with the half that
  changes marked (2:16; 6:3; 7:10). The two Baptist readings of the Song sit side by side at equal size; the page
  chooses neither.
- **Close.** The way of reading as four questions (who is speaking, what kind of saying, what it does not promise,
  where it points), the close, the invitation, the question for the reader, and Job 28:20–28 printed in full, then the
  sources and a back link to Explore the Bible.
- **Every reference** opens its verses in a sheet, in the Berean. Notes the study still has to check show as small gold
  marks where they stand.

## Colour

The site's Poetry teal, because all four books stand in the site's Poetry section (Job to the Song of Songs); it
carries the speaker lines and the figure's lines. Silver-grey carries the mine and its ore in parts 00 and 01 only.
The site's gold carries the concepts, the title's turn, the notes still to check and the one shared line, the fear of
the LORD. Colour is never the only carrier: every voice is named, every kind has a shape, every verdict is in words.

## Doctrine and care

The site's voice follows the Baptist Faith and Message 2000. Salvation is by personal faith in Christ (article IV,
quoted in the invitation and in part 04); the Son is eternal (article II), so Proverbs 8 is read first as a poem about
God's wisdom and not line by line as a statement about the Son; the dead will be raised and judged (article X);
giving is not a deal that buys wealth (article XIII); marriage is one man and one woman for life (article XVIII). Where
Baptists differ, both views are shown at the same size and the page does not choose: what Behemoth and Leviathan are,
how to read Ecclesiastes as a whole, and the plain and the Christ-and-church readings of the Song. The friends' words
are printed as words the Bible reports as wrong, and the speaker line says so. Job's ending is not shown as a promise
that every loss is repaid. The crisis and domestic-violence lines stand where the study puts them, each still marked
for checking. Wisdom and Folly are drawn as words, not as women. No face or figure of God, of Christ or of the Father.
