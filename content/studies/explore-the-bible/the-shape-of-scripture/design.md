## Direction

The page changes its shape with the writing. The study's whole point is that a command, a lament and a letter ask different things, so each passage is set large in a shape of its own before any comment. Only one direction was built, as with the earlier mock-ups, because this was an existing draft.

## The page

- **Opening.** Study trail, the four questions in gold, the title, the lead, Luke 24:44, and a line drawing of six kinds of writing on one river, from a tree to a city. Below it, four computed figures, each with what it means.
- **01 One word. Six shapes.** The six names are always visible. Choosing one sets its passage in its form:
  - Law: Exodus 20:2–3 cut in one arched stone. The caption says the layout is this page's.
  - History: Ezra 1:1–3 with a margin saying whose voice it is.
  - Poetry and Wisdom: all of Psalm 13 climbing in three steps, then Proverbs 26:4–5 facing across one rule.
  - Prophets: Isaiah 1:16–17 with its verbs marked, and 1:18 set apart.
  - Gospels and Acts: an open book, Luke's last page and Acts's first, with shared words marked.
  - Letters and Revelation: Ephesians 4:1–3 and Revelation 1:4 set as letters, then Genesis 2 facing Revelation 22.

  Under the form, four questions run one above another: form, hearers, link, own words. "Walk this reading" opens the full text in one column. The view is in the address.
- **02 One book. Many kinds of writing.** One kind of writing at a time. The list of places is the real content at every width. From 900 wide up, a rail of all 66 books in the site's colours sits above it as a picture of the same list.
- **03 Every later part. Looks back.** A staircase of 21 steps, where rows are later parts and columns earlier ones. Choosing a step shows both verses with the words marked, and says whether it names, retells or shares words. The kind is carried by a word and a small shape, not by colour. Below 700 wide it runs down the page in groups.
- **04 Name it yourself.** Three passages not walked earlier, one at a time, with a choice of kinds and the reason.
- **Close.** The way of reading, the invitation, a question for the reader's own life, the passage again, links on, a river, and the sources with how far each was read.

Passage links everywhere open one sheet from the right, with the Berean text and a link to the reader.

## Against the owner's taste

One big thing per band, large type, and long text in one column. No look is repeated from a sibling: the other Scripture and Theology mock-ups use cards, tables and strips, and this one is mostly type. The rail is the only narrow device, and it is a picture above a list.

## Doctrine and care

The site's voice follows the Baptist Faith and Message 2000, Articles 1 and 4. Scripture is treated as "truth, without any mixture of error", and a form is never leave to doubt what a passage presents as having happened. Salvation is stated as personal faith in Jesus Christ as Lord. The law for Christians and the days of Genesis 1 are named as not settled here. Types and connections are claimed only where the New Testament draws them, or are labelled as this study's. See [passage-notes.md](passage-notes.md) for the rest.
