## Interpretive limits

1. **Divisions:** this site's reading guide, not Scripture's. Books cross them, and most of the Law's pages are story. "Mainly another kind" in section 02 is this study's judgment.
2. **Exodus 20:** the stone is this page's layout. Scripture does not say how the words sat on the tablets, and verse 2 is not itself a command. The first draft's idea of two tablets was dropped for that reason.
3. **The law for Christians:** named as open. One historic Baptist confession is reported, and the Baptist Faith and Message does not set out that division.
4. **Joshua 1:7–9:** a word to one man about to lead Israel into the land, tied to keeping what Moses commanded. Not a promise of wealth. This is flagged as the site's reading. It does not cite an article of the confession, because none speaks to this verse.
5. **Song and truth:** the form tells how a passage tells the truth, and gives no leave to doubt what it presents as having happened. Exodus 14–15 and Judges 4–5 are the worked examples. The days of Genesis 1 are not handled here and no position is taken.
6. **Job's friends:** Scripture truly records what God says was not accurate. Paul quotes Eliphaz in 1 Corinthians 3:19, so not every sentence is false, and the book's verdict is not extended to every line.
7. **Proverbs 26:4–5:** the instructions and reasons differ. The teaching about weighing an answer is this site's reading, and it is labelled so.
8. **Isaiah 1:16–18:** the order on the page is not a price list. A care note says the cleansing is the LORD's word and points to faith in Christ, labelled as the site's own word and not Isaiah's. Isaiah's speech is not read as a rejection of all worship.
9. **Luke 24:44–47:** Jesus's words support reading the Old Testament as about Him. They do not excuse reading an earlier passage out of its setting, and they are not used to give a fixed ancient table of contents. A connection the New Testament does not draw is to be owned as the reader's own.
10. **Genesis 2 and Revelation 22:** a comparison of shared words in English, and not a quotation. A garden and a city remain different.
11. **Section 02's marks and section 03's steps:** this study's selection. A telling word is checked in each verse, which proves the word is there and not that the passage is that kind. A step called "shares words" does not prove a writer meant the link. Section 03's "earlier" means earlier on the page, not earlier in date. All comparisons of wording are in the English of the Berean, and word-for-word means word for word in English.
12. **Judges 9:8–15:** the trees are Jotham's way of making a point inside a real account, as the verses around it say. This is not a precedent for reading other narrative as made up.
