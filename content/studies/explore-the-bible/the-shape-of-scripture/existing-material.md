These content divisions do not authorize changing chart labels, counts or book membership. The draft also does not reproduce all existing chart measures or certify their calculations. Its added work is sustained explanation, connected readings, questions and interpretive boundaries.

## Limits and related work

The existing guide links to an outside literary-styles resource; that linked page was not used as a source for new claims. The structure and coverage tools serve different purposes: an edition's book coverage is not the same question as literary form. Expanded comparisons of canons or arrangements require their own sourcing and should not be inferred from data labels alone.

Use the [study 31 inventory](../books-in-their-settings/existing-material.md) for book-introduction resources and the [study 32 map](../letters-and-their-message/letter-map.md) for the complete letter collection. Preserve those assets instead of copying them into a new introduction database.
